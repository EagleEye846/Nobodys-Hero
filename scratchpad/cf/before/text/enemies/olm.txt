[intro]
A pale shape unfolds from the shallows, a good deal longer than it first looked. Its neck rises. It seems to apologise. An OLM!

[flavor]
OLM turns its blank face aside. It hates being looked at, and you are looking.

OLM fans its red frills, then folds them again, as if it isn't sure it's allowed.

OLM is very old. It has had a long time to practise being polite, and nothing else to do.

A drop of water runs down OLM's snout and falls off the end. It lets it.

Smells like wet stone and a very clean cellar.

# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/olm.js.
[talk: Inspect]
OLM - HP 1180
A long, pale cave salamander, older than the stair. Blind, terribly polite, and hates being looked at. Hits like cold water, slowly, and a lot.

[talk: Look away]
You look at the wall with great interest. OLM lets out a long breath it's been holding for about a century. It loosens a coil.

[talk: Apologise for staring]
You cover your eyes with both hands, by way of apology. OLM says nothing. Then, very quietly, one frill unfolds, which you gather is a thank you.

[talk: Bow]
You bow. OLM bows back, much lower, and for much longer. You're not sure when you're allowed to stop. Neither is it.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
OLM folds up in the shallows, very neatly, and stops. Even now it hasn't made a mess.

[pacified]
OLM bows its long neck, thanks you for your consideration, and sinks into the dark water. The ripples don't even reach the shore.
