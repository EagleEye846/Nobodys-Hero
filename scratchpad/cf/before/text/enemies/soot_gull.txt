# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/soot_gull.js.
[talk: Inspect]
SOOT GULL - HP 5600
A sea gull a long way from the sea, gone grey all over, and still a thief. Dives along a marked line, throws feathers, drops stolen embers. If a dive or an ember lands, it takes food from your bag.

[talk: Turn out your pockets]
You turn out your pockets to show there's nothing in them. SOOT GULL hops closer for a good look, and is so let down that it gives back what it took, as if it never wanted it.

[talk: Point at the sea]
You point east, back the way you came, where the sea is. SOOT GULL follows your finger. It looks for a long time. A gull that far from the sea knows exactly what you mean.

[talk: Brush off its wing]
You hold out a hand, slowly, and brush the soot off one wing. It's white underneath. SOOT GULL stares at it, then lets you do the other one, and drops what it was holding to keep still.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
SOOT GULL tumbles over in a puff of soot, and the ember rolls out of its beak and goes out on the road. It leaves a feather behind.

[pacified]
SOOT GULL shakes itself, a great cloud of soot coming off, and for a moment it is nearly white. Then it lifts off and heads east, toward the sea, crying all the way.
