# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/moonjelly.js.
[talk: Inspect]
MOONJELLY - HP 2000
A jellyfish that glows like a moon. Moves only on the pulse, its babies hop together on one beat, and its tendrils sting only where the moonlight runs along them.

[talk: Hold your breath]
You hold your breath, and your arms, and your boots, and wait. MOONJELLY comes closer, and then closer, and the light inside it goes slow. It has never had anything be so quiet next to it.

[talk: Dim your light]
You cup your hands round your light until it is only a glow on the ground. MOONJELLY tilts, like a head tilting. It is, for once, the brightest thing in the dark, and it sounds pleased.

[talk: Drift along]
You walk alongside it, slowly, at exactly its speed, and don't hurry it. MOONJELLY keeps pace. After a while neither of you is going anywhere in particular, and it's wonderful.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
The light in the bell goes down and down. MOONJELLY sags, folds, and sinks into itself like a lamp being turned low, until there's only a ring of small bells on the water, ringing very quietly.

[pacified]
MOONJELLY glows all the way through, soft and warm. It rises, turns once, and lets a little scatter of bells fall for you. Then it drifts off, trailing its light, and lets you by.
