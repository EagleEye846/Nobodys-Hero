[intro]
There is a four-leaf clover in the path, and under it, very slowly, a snail. Its eyes are on stalks and are mostly shut. A CLOVER SNAIL!

# Shown at the start of each of your turns, in order, then repeating.
[flavor]
CLOVER SNAIL yawns. You feel the yawn go round the whole field.

CLOVER SNAIL's eye-stalks droop together, then snap back up. It would like you to think it was thinking.

A little Z drifts out of CLOVER SNAIL and over your head. You catch yourself nodding.

CLOVER SNAIL looks at its shell, then at you, then at its shell. It seems to be counting naps.

Smells like cut clover, and a warm afternoon with nothing in it.

[flavor: spareable]

# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/clover_snail.js.
[talk: Inspect]
CLOVER SNAIL - HP 1900
A sleepy green snail with a clover for a shell. Its slime trail dries from the tail, its lucky four-leaf clovers do you good, and its Z's drift in the moment you stop. Don't join in.

[talk: Hum it a lullaby]
You hum something soft, low and old. CLOVER SNAIL listens with both eyes shut. It sways. At the end it opens one eye and, in the smallest voice, asks if you know any others.

[talk: Find the lucky leaf]
You look over the shell, leaf by leaf. There are four, which is the usual number for a clover, and the unusual one for a lucky one. CLOVER SNAIL watches you work, and gives the smallest nod when you point at the right one.

[talk: Sit beside it]
You sit down beside CLOVER SNAIL on the warm grass. Neither of you says anything. A bee goes by. A cloud goes by. After a long time CLOVER SNAIL says that was lovely, and that you must come again in an hour.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
CLOVER SNAIL rolls onto its back, with its shell tilted skyward and its eyes shut. It says, from a long way off, that it will just have a little lie down, and does not move again for some time.

[pacified]
CLOVER SNAIL tucks its eyes in, pulls up its clover like a quilt and goes properly, beautifully to sleep, with a small smile on its face. A single Z goes up, and up, and stays there, like a cloud.
