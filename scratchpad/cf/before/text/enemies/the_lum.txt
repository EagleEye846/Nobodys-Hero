# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/the_lum.js.
[talk: Inspect]
THE LUM - HP 26000
The last chimney of a burnt farmhouse, its fire never out, keeping the road to the buried town. Bricks, smoke rings, sparks, coals; when its hearth catches, fire on the floor and its own pot. Not wicked. Gruff.

[talk: Warm your hands]
You hold your hands out to its hearth, the way you would at home on a cold night. The fire is very warm. THE LUM stops. It has not been used for that in a long time. Its smoke goes up a little slower.

[talk: Sweep the hearth]
You find a stub of brush in the ash and sweep its hearthstone clean, out from the grate to the edge, the way somebody used to. THE LUM looks down at it. Its eyes go a little dimmer, and softer.

[talk: Set a brick back]
A brick has fallen off its stack into the ash. You pick it up, knock it clean and push it back into its gap. It fits. THE LUM shifts its weight, as if a tooth had stopped aching.

[talk: Straighten the mug]
You reach up to the mantel and turn the mug so its handle faces out, ready for whoever it was for. THE LUM watches you do it, and says nothing at all for a long time.

[talk: Bank the fire]
You rake its coals in close and cover them over with ash, the way you bank a fire for the night, so it keeps till morning without anybody minding it. THE LUM's fire sinks down to a warm red. It doesn't go out. It rests.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
THE LUM settles back onto its hearthstones with a long sigh of smoke. Its fire sinks to a low red glow. It doesn't go out. A brick comes loose from the hearth and drops at your feet.

[pacified]
THE LUM breathes out one slow ring of smoke, and steps back to the side of the gate. "Go on up, then," it says, very low. "Wipe your feet. And take this. It's warm. It'll stay warm." A brick comes loose from its hearth and drops at your feet.
