# Shown at the start of each of your turns, in order, then repeating.
[flavor]
CORRIE floats over nothing. His coat is far too big for him. The sleeves are rolled four times and still hang past his hands.

The glow in the fork of his staff flickers like a candle in a draught.

Water runs off his hem and never reaches the floor.

He holds the staff the way you'd hold something you were told not to touch.

[flavor: storm]
The storm pours out of him. Somewhere in the middle of it, a small boy is shouting.

The tide is coming up. Every turn, a little higher.

Rain lashes the lamp room's glass. The lightning finds the water first.

TIP: Haddock's bell, rung by you and Bramble together, sends the water back down.

[flavor: whirlpool]
The sea under the tower is turning. Everything it ever took is going round in it.

Planks. Oars. A figurehead with salt in her hair. Round and round.

Below the waist, CORRIE isn't there any more. Only the whirl.

The pull's in your bones now. Don't let it take you to the middle.

[flavor: lamp]
The great lamp turns, slow. Each time the beam crosses him, he flinches.

He's only a boy now, mostly. A tired one. The staff is too heavy for him.

The water on his face might be the sea. It might not.

Hide behind the pillars when the beam comes round.

# One section per TALK option (the names must match corrie.js).
[talk: Check]
CORRIE - HP 36000
A boy who tried to stop the sea and became the storm. Darts, boats, a spell circle, a lullaby. Then the tide, the whirlpool and the lamp. He can't be talked down. The storm won't let him hear.
His bullets come too thick to feed on: grazing them gives little SP. Spend it well.

[talk: Ring Haddock's bell]
You and Bramble take Haddock's bell by its cord together and ring it, hard. The note goes out over the water, and for a moment the storm holds its breath.
The sea pulls back. The next attack will be gentler.

[talk: Wave to him]
You wave to him across the water, both arms, the way you'd wave a boat in.

[talk: Hold out a hand]
You hold out a hand.

# Any kindness at all (a TALK, Pacify, a candle, a friend's kind word): the
# storm answers, not him. The first time, all of [no_mercy]; after that, one
# of [no_mercy_again].
[no_mercy]
The wind tears it away and throws it back into the sea.

Somewhere in the storm, he might have heard. If he did, the storm didn't let him.

[no_mercy_again]
A wave comes over the rail, and he's gone behind it.

The thunder answers instead. It doesn't sound like him.

The rain is too loud. Nothing gets through to him.

The whirl takes it and turns it round and round until it's gone.
