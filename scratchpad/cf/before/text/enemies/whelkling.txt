[intro]
Out in the dark water a cluster of tiny green lights comes up, swaying. Under them, a shell. Under the shell, two eyes on stalks, and a claw. A WHELKLING!

WHELKLING taps its big claw on its shell. It sounds hollow, and expensive.

WHELKLING pulls right in, and then comes out again, a little, to see if you've gone.

Smells like a rock pool at night, and lemon.

# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/whelkling.js.
[talk: Inspect]
WHELKLING - HP 1950
A hermit crab in a big whelk shell crusted with glowing anemones. Hops into shells biggest first, topples the way it leans, and its anemones go off round you like a clock.

[talk: Admire the shell]
You lean in and look at the shell properly: the spiral going round and up, the bands, the little lights on top. You nod slowly. WHELKLING comes all the way out to see you looking, and turns, so you can see the other side as well.

[talk: Offer a bigger shell]
You find a bigger shell on the floor of the cave and hold it out. WHELKLING looks at it a long while. It gets halfway out of its own, very carefully, and then gets back in. It keeps yours, though. You can tell it's going to think about it.

[talk: Sit still by the pool]
You sit down on the wet rock at the edge of the pool and wait, in the dark, the drips going. After a while WHELKLING's little lights come up beside you and stay there, and nobody says anything, which suits you both.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
WHELKLING pulls all the way into its shell and shuts the door with its claw. The shell rolls over, slowly, into the dark water, and the little lights go out one at a time.

[pacified]
WHELKLING gives you a small wave of its big claw. Then it backs away into the dark water, and you watch its little green lights go down, and down, and stop, somewhere at the bottom, still glowing.
