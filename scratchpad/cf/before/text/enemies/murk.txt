# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the

[talk: Inspect]
MURK - HP 1250
A patch of the dark that got up and followed a light. It's afraid of it, and hungry for it, which is a hard way to live. Eats light, and whatever's in it.

[talk: Cover your light]
You cup your hands round your light until it's only a glow between your fingers. MURK stops lunging. It leans in, very slowly, and warms its eyes at it.

[talk: Share a little light]
You hold out a spark of your light. MURK takes it in the smallest, most careful gulp you've ever seen, and glows from the inside for a second. It goes very quiet.

[talk: Sit with it in the dark]
You sit down in the dark beside MURK and don't say anything. It lets you. After a while it shuffles a little closer, and the dark round you gets, if anything, slightly warmer.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
MURK comes apart into thinner and thinner dark, until it's only the ordinary kind.

[pacified]
MURK shrinks to a small, soft patch of dark and settles in a corner, where it is most comfortable. It watches your light go with both eyes, and doesn't follow.
