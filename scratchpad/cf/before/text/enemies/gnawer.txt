# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/gnawer.js.
[talk: Inspect]
GNAWER - HP 1250
Blind, old, and with far too many teeth. It hears everything, including you thinking about running. Gnaws bones, floors, and you.

[talk: Hum something]
You hum, low and slow. GNAWER's ears swing round to you and stay there. Its jaw stops, mid-chew, and stays stopped until you run out of breath.

[talk: Offer it a bone]
You hold out a bone you found. GNAWER takes it with surprising delicacy, turns it over, and gnaws it into a very small bone. It's the happiest you've seen anything look down here.

[talk: Tap a slow rhythm]
You tap a slow beat on the stone. GNAWER listens. Then it taps back with one tooth, a little behind. You do this for a while. It's a duet.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
GNAWER's jaw closes at last, with a click like a lock. The rest of it follows quietly.

[pacified]
GNAWER lays its big ears down and settles with its chin on a bone. Its jaw goes slack and quiet and, you realise, very tired. It lets you pass.
