[first]
Evening! I mean, afternoon. I mean, who can tell, with the Sunstone like it is.

I light these lamps by hand. They used to stay lit all night. Lately I'm going round twice.

[repeat]

Our king's a human, you know. Came down the stair with the Hero! Nobody else down here has got a human.

Mind you, nobody else down here has got a town.
