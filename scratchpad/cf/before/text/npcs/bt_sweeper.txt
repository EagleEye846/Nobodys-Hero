# --- SIDE QUEST: the good dustpan (it's in the Hall of the Visit) ---

[pan_ask]
Oh! You've got the look of somebody who goes places. Listen. I've lost my good dustpan. Last seen in the Hall of the Visit, where I swept the exhibits and the exhibits swept me.

It's got a dent in the side. You'll know it. It'll be looking at you like you owe it money.

[pan_wait]
Dustpan. Hall of the Visit. Dented. Don't let the curator tell you it's an exhibit.

[pan_done]
My dustpan! Oh, and it's still got the dent. Good. I wouldn't know it without.

Here. A pin. Don't tell the broom.

[pan_after]
I've swept the same spot six times today with the right dustpan. I've never felt so thorough.
