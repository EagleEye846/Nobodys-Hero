# THE COLD STOVE (the Last Light's foot): a little iron stove with its fire laid and not lit. A tiny errand
# of the life pass. (draft)

[first]
A small iron stove, its fire laid and never lit: kindling, a twist of paper, a match standing ready. It has a ring on the top the exact size of a kettle's foot, and nothing in it.

[repeat]
The stove, laid and cold. The ring on top is waiting for a kettle.

[kettle_ask]
Everything's ready, down to the match. It's only the kettle that's missing.

[kettle_done]
You set the kettle on the ring. It sits there, heavy and black and exactly right.

You don't light the fire. It's not yours to light. But now when somebody comes down the stair in the dark, there'll be a kettle on a stove, and a match standing ready, and that's something.

Something rattles in the kettle's belly. Under the lid, rolled up in a rag, is a spare lamp wick.

[kettle_after]
The kettle on the ring, the match beside it. Somebody will want that tea.
