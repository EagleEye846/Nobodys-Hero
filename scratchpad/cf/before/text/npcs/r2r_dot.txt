# DOT: a small herald kid on the square who has lost a toy trumpet. A tiny errand (the trumpet lies on the floor of the Hall of Heralds). The life pass.

[first]
Have you seen a trumpet? Little. Dented. It's got a sock in it.

I put the sock in myself. I'm not allowed to play it in the Hall. Then somebody took it. I think a herald did. I think they're keeping it safe. From me.

[repeat]
It's dented in a very particular way. I'd know it anywhere. It's the dent where I sat on it. I didn't mean to. It was a small dent. It got bigger.

[trumpet_back]
You hold out the little trumpet. Dot's whole face goes round.

THAT'S IT! It's got the sock in it! It's got the DENT in it!

She holds it to her chest for a long moment. Then she presses a folded paper into your hand.

It's a picture. It's you. See, you're very tall, and you've got a trumpet. It's the best picture I've ever done, so it's yours.

[trumpet_after]
I've decided I'm going to play it in the Hall. Quietly. Quietly enough that the sock doesn't notice.
