# LADLE'S LOST PROPERTY SHELF (his nook in the Old Drains): everything that has washed down the drains in
# fifty years, labelled in a tiny tidy hand, with one gap. (life pass, 15 November 2026)

# (draft)
[first]
A shelf of lost property, every piece with a tag in a tiny tidy hand. THIMBLE (BRASS). SPOON (ONE). PENNY (1843). DOLL'S SHOE (RED).

Under them, one tag with nothing on it: BOOT (LEFT). Somebody's been keeping a place.

# (draft)
[repeat]
The shelf of lost property. One tag, BOOT (LEFT), still has nothing on it.

# (draft)
[shelf_ask]
A shelf of lost property, every piece with a tag in a tiny tidy hand. THIMBLE (BRASS). SPOON (ONE). PENNY (1843). DOLL'S SHOE (RED).

Under them, one tag with nothing on it: BOOT (LEFT). Somebody's been keeping a place for it, with a clean cloth under the gap, all these years.

If it washed down, it's somewhere in the drains, in the dark.

[shelf_ask_bramble]
{thinking}A left boot. In the Sumps, probably. That's where everything ends up. We could find it.

# (draft)
[shelf_wait]
The shelf waits, with its gap and its clean cloth. BOOT (LEFT). It will have gone to the Sumps, where the silt settles.

# (draft)
[shelf_done]
You set the boot on the cloth. It fits the gap like a key in a lock. The tag says BOOT (LEFT), and for the first time it is true.

Something moves at the edge of the nook, very small and very fast. When you look, there's a new thing on the shelf: a little tin tag on a loop of string. It says KEPT SWEET.

It wasn't there before. It's yours, apparently.

[shelf_done_bramble]
{glee}He said thank you! Old Ladle said thank you! Without even coming out! That's the most polite thing that's ever happened!

# (draft)
[shelf_after]
The shelf of lost property, every piece in its place. BOOT (LEFT) is on its cloth. The whole shelf looks very pleased with itself.
