# GORSE (at the waystation, ash_waystation): a hedgehog from Hearth, the town up the road, out
# scavenging down the old road for what a hundred years has left lying about: hinges, pans, nails.
# Gruff, a bit prickly, secretly delighted to see anyone. One of Hearth's stubborn folk. She makes
# you want to see the town; she doesn't say what's up north, only that it's best left alone, and she
# never mentions the old woman or what burned the land. She tells you how to get in: the folk will
# dig you through for anything that's never gone cold, the Lum's hearth brick above all.
# Bramble speaks first, Ves answers. (draft)

[first]
{stern}Well, now. Boots. Three pairs of boots, and one of them small. I thought you were the wind, the way the ash was going on.

{smile}Gorse. Hearth, up the road, past the cut. Don't look at me like that. There IS a Hearth. Somebody's got to be stubborn about it.

{stern}I come down here and pick over what's left. Hinges, pans, the odd good nail. You'd be amazed what a hundred years leaves lying about. Mostly it's ash. Occasionally it's a very good pan.

{smile}Hearth's worth the walk, mind. Warm doors, a smith's fire you can hear from the gate. Only the gate's choked. Bring something that's never gone cold, and the folk will dig you in.

[first_bramble]
{glee}Is Hearth a proper town? With doors? And food?

[first_ves]
{bored}She said "a very good pan". I think that means yes.

[repeat]
{grin}Soup on every stove and a smith's fire you can hear from the gate. Every doorstep swept, every window sill with a pot of something green on it. Yes, green. Don't tell the neighbours.

{stern}It's only a squeeze. We've dug it out three times. It keeps filling. That's a town for you: you dig, it fills, you dig.

[repeat_bramble]
{thinking}Green? On a sill? In all this?

[repeat_ves]
{thoughtful}If somebody's still growing things in this, I'd like to meet them.

[repeat 2]
{stern}You'll want the gate. It's choked up to the lintel, mind. The folk will dig you through, if you show them something that's never gone cold. A brick off a hearth that's stayed lit. There's a chimney by the gate, a stubborn one. Light sleeper.

{smile}If it'll let you have a brick, show them that, and they'll open the ground for you like a door.

[repeat_bramble 2]
{surprised}Is it a... friendly chimney?

[repeat_ves 2]
{bored}"Light sleeper." That's not an answer, is it.

[repeat 3]
{stern}And that black orchard? The green shoot, by the third tree from the road. That's mine. I put one in every trip. Most of them the wind takes. This one took.

{grin}Don't step on it.

[repeat_bramble 3]
{proud}We didn't! We said hello to it!

[repeat_ves 3]
{smile}We said hello to a shoot, Bramble. It didn't say anything back.

[repeat 4]
{stern}If you hear a lot of grinding up north, past the town, keep walking. The ground goes hot up that way. There's something up there that's best left to its own business.

{stern}That's all I'll say. I'm a scavenger, not a poet.

[repeat 5]
{smile}Go on, then. I've a hinge to find.

[repeat_bramble 5]
{glee}See you in Hearth!

[repeat_ves 5]
{smile}Mind the pans.
