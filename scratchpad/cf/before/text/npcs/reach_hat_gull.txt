# THE HATLESS GULL (the Reach's first rock): a gull that wants a hat. A tiny errand of the life pass. (draft)

[first]
A gull on a rock, very upright, looking at the sea like a magistrate. It has a small round dent in the feathers on top of its head, where something used to sit.

[repeat]
The gull, upright and affronted. It glances at your head, then at the sea.

[hat_ask]
The gull gives you a long, steady look. Then it looks at its own head. Then at yours. It's obvious what it wants, and you aren't going to be the one to say it.

[hat_done]
The gull takes the sou'wester in both feet, somehow. It puts it on. It shakes itself. The hat slides over one eye, and the gull doesn't care.

It looks a hundred times more important. It walks round in a slow, grand circle, and stops, and drops a feather at your boots without looking at you, the way a king drops a coin.

[hat_after]
The gull, in a yellow sou'wester, standing at the very edge of the rock. It looks as if it's in charge of the sea.
