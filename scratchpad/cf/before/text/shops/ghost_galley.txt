# THE GALLEY: the ghost cook of the big wreck in the Wreck Yard, who went down with her forty
# years ago and hasn't noticed. Delighted to have diners at last. Cheerful, fussy, forgetful,
# a bit lonely; never sad about it. He cooks with a cold stove and it comes out hot anyway.
# Each section is one line of the shopkeeper's, shown in the shop's text box.
# Sections starting with "talk:" become topics under TALK, in this order. All (draft).

[greeting]
Diners! Real ones! Sit anywhere, sit everywhere, the crew won't mind. They never come down to eat any more. I keep it hot for them.

[greeting 2]
You came back! Nobody comes back. Well. The tide does. And now you.

[greeting 3]
My regulars! I've kept your places. I've kept everybody's places.

[menu_prompt]
What'll it be? Don't say "nothing". Nobody leaves my galley on nothing.

[buy_prompt]
Hardtack, cocoa, and the soup. Always the soup. There's always more soup.

[sell_prompt]
Selling to the cook? I'll take anything for the pot. I've put worse in it. I've put a boot in it.

[talk_prompt]
Pull up a bench. Mind the one on the end, it's for Mr. Pask. He's late.

[bought]
There you are. On the house. ...No, it isn't, the purser'd have my hide. Coins in the jar.

[bought_item]
Hot as you like. Don't ask me how. The stove's been out for years, and the soup doesn't know.

[bought_skill]
That's not on my menu. I only do food. And opinions.

[skill_sold_out]
Only food here. And the soup.

[sold]
Into the pot it goes. Thank you kindly.

[nothing_to_sell]
Nothing for the pot? Then have something out of it.

[cant_sell]
You're wearing that, friend. Take it off first, with C. Not in my galley, I've just mopped.

[owned]
You've already got one. Have soup instead. Soup doesn't run out.

[wont_fit]
That's not for you, I think. Not for anyone in this galley.

[too_poor]
Short a few coins? Ah. The purser counts them. He counts everything. Go and find a few and come back hungry.

[bought_stored]
Your pack's full, so I've sent it on to your chest, by the next save light. Don't ask me how. I'm a cook.

[bag_full]
Your pack's full and so's your chest. Eat something now, then. Go on. I'll watch.

[farewell]
Come back hungry! The soup'll be on. The soup's always on.

[talk: Who are you?]
Cook! Ship's cook. Forty years, man and boy. Well. Man. Then... whatever this is. I don't hold with names in a galley. You just shout "COOK" and I come.

[talk: The soup]
Fish, a bit of everything, and a pinch of something I won't tell you. The secret's that it never stops. I put the lid on that pot forty years ago and it's still simmering.

[talk: The crew]
They're up on deck. Or down in the hold. Or somewhere. They don't come down to eat any more, and I keep their bowls warm anyway. It's what a cook does. You keep the bowls warm.

[talk: The cold stove]
Is it cold? So it is. Huh. Well, don't tell the soup.

[talk: Bramble]
The small one has the right idea about food. Look at him. Two bowls and asking about the third. That's a crewman, that is.

[talk: Ves]
Your tall friend's had nothing but a crust. I've noticed. Cooks notice. I've put a bowl by her elbow. She'll eat it when she thinks I'm not looking. They always do.
