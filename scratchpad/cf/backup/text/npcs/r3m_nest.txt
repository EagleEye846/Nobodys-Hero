# THE MOORHEN'S NEST (the Rushes, in the Lantern Marsh): a nest on stakes, one egg short. (life pass, 15 November 2026)

# (draft)
[first]
A nest on three stakes at the edge of the water. Three speckled eggs and a gap where a fourth would be.

# (draft)
[repeat]
The nest, one egg short. A pair of small dark eyes watches you from the reeds.

# (draft)
[nest_ask]
A nest on three stakes at the edge of the water. Three speckled eggs, and a gap in the middle where a fourth would be.

A pair of small dark eyes watches you from the reeds. They aren't angry. They're very worried.

[nest_ask_bramble]
{worried}There's one missing. Somebody's egg is out there. It'll have rolled off. I bet it's on the other side, near the start.

# (draft)
[nest_wait]
The nest, one egg short. The eyes in the reeds are still watching. They look at your hands, and then at your face, and then at your hands again.

# (draft)
[nest_done]
You set the egg in the gap. It settles with a little click, the way an egg does when it's home.

There's a rustle in the reeds, and a small dark bird steps out, and sits on the nest without a word, and looks at you for a long moment. Then she shakes herself, and a feather drifts down onto the boards by your feet.

You pick it up. It's a thank-you. It's a very small one. She's very busy.

[nest_done_bramble]
{glee}She came out! She SAT on it! That's the most beautiful thing I've ever seen. I'm going to cry. I'm NOT. I'm... allergic to eggs.

# (draft)
[nest_after]
The nest, full again. The moorhen sits on it with her eyes half shut. She doesn't look up, but one wing lifts a little as you pass, which is as good as a wave.
