# MARTA: a herald who knits, on the bench in the square. Her scarf is longer
# than the square. She has no opinions about the Hero, and will tell you so.

[first]
Mm. Hello, dear. Mind the scarf.

You'll be the one from the stairs. Everyone's very excited. I'm knitting.

Are you the Hero? I've no opinion. I've knitted for six so far and not one of them wanted a scarf. Big heroic types. Cold necks, every one.

Here. Marta measures your neck with her eyes. Mm. Give me a day.

Have you met Ves yet? The girl in the chapel, with the umbrella. Go and say hello, dear.

[repeat]
Knit one, purl one, a hundred years. You get a rhythm.

If you go up past the Lookout, dear, take mittens. The Hero's meant to be above all that. You are not, no offence.

That scarf of mine? Goes round the square twice and into the inn. The innkeeper uses the end for the soup pot handle.

[first after ves_in_the_chapel]
Mm. Hello, dears. Mind the scarf.

So you found Ves. Good. She's sat in that chapel long enough to take root.

Marta measures your neck with her eyes, then looks at Ves's scarf and nods. Her mum's work. Good wool.

Yours is next, dear. Give me a day.

[repeat after ves_in_the_chapel]
Knit one, purl one, a hundred years. You get a rhythm.

If you go up past the Lookout, dear, take mittens. The Hero's meant to be above all that. You are not, no offence.

That scarf of mine? Goes round the square twice and into the inn. The innkeeper uses the end for the soup pot handle.

# --- SIDE QUEST: mittens for Dunstan, and a scarf ---

[mittens_ask]
Oh, and dear. While you're about.

Marta finally looks up from the scarf, and holds out a pair of mittens without letting go of her needles. They're grey and thick, with a small trumpet knitted on the back of each.

For Dunstan. In the bell tower, south-east corner, with the earmuffs and the rope. Forty years he's rung that bell with bare paws, and every winter he says he's fine. Mind the rope in there.

You take the mittens.

[mittens_wait]
The mittens are for Dunstan, dear. The bell tower, south-east corner. He's the one with the earmuffs and the rope. Don't let him tell you he's fine.

[mittens_done]
Marta reads Dunstan's note twice, holding it very close, then very far away, then close again.

"Fits." Mm. He always says that. He said it about the first pair and the second, and it was the third before I found out he'd never worn any of them.

She puts her needles down. It's the first time you've seen her do it.

Now. Your neck, dear. I said a day, and I took five, so don't remark on it. I knit the stair stitch: one row for every step of the great stair, up and back, so the Hero should never have to count. Nobody's ever finished a Hero's scarf to the last row.

I have. Not for a Hero, mind. For you. Here.

[mittens_after]
Marta has started the next row of the next scarf. She doesn't look up.

Wear it, dear. It's not for looking at. It's for the neck.
