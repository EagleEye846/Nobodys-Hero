# ST_PIP: an otter child, seven and a half, on the beach in the sun, who built a very good sandcastle and has lost the bucket and the spade that built it.
# An errand: the bucket and spade are on the cove's sea floor, out at low tide (the tide bell in the cove). The life pass.

[first]
Hello! I'm Tibby! I'm seven and a half! I'm building a castle! Well, I've BUILT it. I'm just standing next to it so nobody steps on it.

[repeat]
It's got a moat. The moat's real. The sea comes in, that's what it does.

[ask]
I had a bucket. And a spade. A blue one and a red one. And I put them down by the water for ONE minute, and then the sea went out, and when it came back they'd GONE. Somebody took them, or the sea did. I think the sea did.

[ask_ves]
{smile}Blue bucket. Red spade. Seven and a half. Understood.

[ask_bramble]
{determined}We'll find them. The sea doesn't get to keep a bucket.

[found]
You hold out the bucket and the spade. Tibby looks at them. Tibby looks at you. Tibby looks at them again, very slowly, as if they might not be real.

"My BUCKET," Tibby says, in a small voice. "It's got my CRAB in it. Look. Look, he's still in there." He is. He has the face of someone who has been through a lot and has, in the end, been cheated of a very good adventure.

Tibby puts the bucket down and lets the crab out and watches him scuttle off, a bit sideways, a bit slow. "He'll be all right," Tibby says. "He's tough."

Then, from a pocket, a lolly stick with a bit of red paper. "That's my best flag. I made it for the top of the castle, but I haven't got a castle that's good enough. You have it."

[found_ves]
{smile}A flag, for a bucket. Fair trade.

[found_bramble]
{proud}King of the beach. That's us now. We're royalty.

[after]
The crab's gone home. The castle's still standing. The flag's the best thing on it. The castle's the best thing on the beach. That's seven and a half for you.

[repeat 2]
I'm going to build a BIGGER castle tomorrow. With a gate. And a drawbridge. And a thing for the gull. Gulls need somewhere to stand, or they get cross.

{glee}Can I do the drawbridge? I'm really good at bridges.

[first after the_light_answers]
The sun's out and the sand's SO hot! And I've made a thing! It's a different kind of castle! It's got a window!

[repeat after the_light_answers]
Windows are new. I'd never needed one. Now I've made a lot. I'm calling it "The Castle of the Sun."
