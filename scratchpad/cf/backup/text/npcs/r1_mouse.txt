# R1_MOUSE: Fennel, a mouse in a scarf by her door in the Ruins' entrance hall. A tiny errand of the life pass: her lucky button is lost. (draft)

[first]
A mouse in a knitted scarf sits outside her tiny door, twisting her paws. She sees you, and freezes, all at once, the way mice do.

"You're very big," she says. "Don't be alarmed. I'm Fennel. I live here. It's the only entrance with a doormat."

[repeat]
Fennel sits outside her tiny door and watches the corridor. She keeps a very careful eye on the dummy.

[button_ask]
"I've lost my button," Fennel says, in a rush. "Brass. Round. Shiny. It's my lucky one. I was going to give it to the Hero, for luck, when they came, and then I dropped it, and then... well. Nobody's come, so it's only me who's out a button."

"If you see it, I'd be so grateful. I think it rolled towards the flowers. Everything does."

[button_done]
You put the button down in front of her. She looks at it for a long moment, then at you, then at it again.

"Oh," says Fennel. "Oh! It's the right one. It's even still shiny."

She gives it a quick, rough polish on her scarf, tucks it into her pocket, and gives you something in return, with both paws: a scarf. It's about as long as your thumb.

"I made it," she says. "It's for when you're cold. Just... the thumb. But it's the thought."

[button_after]
"It's still shiny," Fennel says, patting her pocket. "Thank you. Mind the dummy."
