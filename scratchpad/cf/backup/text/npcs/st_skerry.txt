# ST_SKERRY: a seal pup in a bobble hat (the one with the paper boats), who has lost a cat. An errand: Pilchard is asleep somewhere
# nobody would think to look: behind the crates at the far end of the lagoon's promenade. Skerry hops about the market's south-west corner. The life pass.

[first]
Have you seen a cat? Ginger. Big. Sulks. Name of Pilchard. He went off after the storm began and he hasn't come home, and my gran says he'll be at the fish stall. He's not at the fish stall.

[repeat]
Pilchard. P-I-L-C-H-A-R-D. If you see him say it like that and he'll know it's me.

[ask]
Gran says he'll turn up. Gran said that about my other cat too, and he turned up, but he was a different cat. It was a bit of a shock.

[ask_ves]
{smile}Ginger. Big. Sulks. We'll look.

[ask_bramble]
{determined}Leave it to us. We'll find Pilchard. We'll find him and everything.

[found]
You hold out the cat. Pilchard looks at Skerry. Skerry looks at Pilchard. Nobody says anything for a long moment, and then Pilchard puts his whole face into Skerry's bobble hat and goes limp as a sack.

"PILCHARD!" Skerry says. "You were at the LAGOON! That's miles! He's never been to the lagoon!" He holds the cat very hard. "I've got nothing for you. I've got... hang on."

He roots through every pocket, and out comes a medal made from a bottle top, with a bit of ribbon and a crude drawing of a cat on it.

"WORLD'S BEST CAT FINDER," he says. "I made it for if anyone ever did. Nobody ever did."

[found_ves]
{smile}A cat in a hat, a medal in a pocket. That's a good day.

[found_bramble]
{proud}World's best cat finder. That's on my list now. I've got a LIST.

[after]
Pilchard's in the hat. He won't come out of the hat. I've given up. I'm just wearing a cat now.

[repeat 2]
Gran says I'm not to feed him fish at the stall. I said the fish is for the CAT. She said, "Whose cat?" I said, "Mine." She said, "Exactly."
