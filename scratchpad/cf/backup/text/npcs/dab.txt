# DAB: a small cave-frog kid on the King's Road, who has lost his tin of sweets. (The tin is in the
# grotto where Tuck finds you; see glimmer_tuck.) A tiny errand: bring the tin, get a lucky pebble.

[first]
Have you seen a tin? Small. Rattles. It's got my sweets in it, and my lucky pebble's in with the sweets, so it's practically two tins.

I put it down in the grotto at the back, to have a proper look at the biggest crystal. Then there were voices, and I ran. I'm still running, a bit. On the inside.

[repeat]
It's got sugar on the lid. That's how you know it's mine. That, and the thumbprint. I did the thumbprint on purpose.

[tin_back]
You hold out the tin. Dab's whole face goes round.

IT'S MINE! You found it! It rattles! It's all still in it!

He opens it, counts, closes it, and presses something into your hand.

That's my lucky one. It's lucky. It's yours now, for exactly the amount of luck you've got coming. Don't lose it. It's not insured.

[tin_after]
I've eaten two. For the shock. The rest are for emergencies, and I'm having one now.
