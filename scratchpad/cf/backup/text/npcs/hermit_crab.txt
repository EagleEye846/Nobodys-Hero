# THE HERMIT CRAB (Chapter 5, the life pass): a hermit crab in a shell much too small for it, on the Landing's shingle (wrack_landing). It wants a bigger one: the big whelk shell lies in a hollow at the far corner of the Pools (wrack_pools). (draft)

[first]
Excuse me. Excuse me. Yes, you. I am between homes.

This one has been very good to me. But a person grows. Look at it: I have to keep my elbows in.

I want a whelk. A proper one. Big as a loaf, with a doorway.

There was one by the Pools, in the far corner. I'm told. I can't go. I have no transport.

[repeat]
A big whelk shell, in the far corner of the Pools. I'd be so grateful. I'd be in it.

I'm doing my best to look patient. It isn't easy in this shell.

# The big whelk is in your pack.
[shell_done]
Oh. Oh, look at it. It's got a doorway. A real one.

One moment. Look away. It's a private thing.

...There! How do I look?

Take my old one. It's a good shell. It's only a bit tight. I don't want to see it go to a stranger.

[shell_after]
I can turn round in here. I can turn round! Look!

...I'll do that again in a minute.
