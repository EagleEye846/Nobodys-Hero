# --- Chapter 4: tally tokens. The miners hung one on a board going down, and took it off coming up. ---

[tally_pick]
A little brass token lies in the dust. A pick is stamped on it.

[tally_pick_ves]
Miners hung one on the board going down and took it back coming up. Fair system. Mostly.

[tally_lamp]
A little brass token, kicked into the corner. A lamp is stamped on it.

[tally_lamp_ves]
Somebody lost their lamp token. I bet they minded the walk back more than the token.

[tally_cart]
A little brass token, up on the ledge. A cart is stamped on it.

[tally_cart_ves]
Up here? Either somebody hid it, or somebody had very bad luck and very good knees.

[tally_canary]
A little brass token with a tiny bird on it, half under a stone.

[tally_canary_ves]
A token for the canary. They counted the bird. I respect that.

[tally_candle]
A little brass token, tucked in under the crystals. A candle is stamped on it.

[tally_candle_ves]
Everything in here sparkles, and this is the one thing I'd actually keep.

[tally_rope]
A little brass token in the corner. A coil of rope is stamped on it.

[tally_rope_ves]
Picks, lamps, ropes. Whoever made these had a short list of things to worry about.

[tally_bucket]
A little brass token wedged between two stones. A bucket is stamped on it.

[tally_bucket_ves]
A bucket, on a lake shore. I assume somebody is still bailing.

[tally_hammer]
A little brass token in the dust of the passage. A hammer is stamped on it.

[tally_hammer_ves]
Dropped on the way out of town. Whoever it was never came back for it. Neither would I.

[tally_boot]
A little brass token at the very end of the crawl. A boot is stamped on it.

[tally_boot_ves]
A boot token. Down here. I'd have gone home by now. Possibly sooner.

[tally_whistle]
A little brass token behind the old bedroll. A whistle is stamped on it.

[tally_whistle_ves]
A whistle. So somebody knew when to stop. I like them already.

[tally_done]
That's all ten. You thread them on a bootlace, and they chink together.

You got a TOKEN STRING, from the dust under the last one.

[tally_done_ves]
Ten tokens, ten shifts. Hang them up properly and nobody's missing from the board.

# --- Chapter 5: sea glass. ---

[glass_green]
A smooth green chip of glass, rubbed soft by the sea.

[glass_green_bramble]
Look! It's like a boiled sweet! Can I keep it? Can we keep it?

[glass_blue]
A smooth blue chip of glass in the sand.

[glass_blue_ves]
Blue is the rarer one. Don't ask me how I know. I just do.

[glass_white]
A smooth white chip of glass, tucked by a pool.

[glass_white_bramble]
It was a bottle once! Do you think there was a message in it?

[glass_amber]
A smooth amber chip of glass in the sand.

[glass_amber_ves]
Somebody's ale bottle, a long time ago. Now it's art.

[glass_brown]
A smooth brown chip of glass by the slip.

[glass_brown_bramble]
Brown glass looks like toffee. I'm not going to lick it. ...Ves is looking at me.

[glass_frosted]
A pale, frosted chip of glass in the corner of the quay.

[glass_frosted_ves]
The sea does that. Years of it. I haven't the patience, so I'm impressed.

[glass_lilac]
A smooth lilac chip of glass at the edge of the water.

[glass_lilac_bramble]
Purple! Do they make purple bottles? Who drinks out of those?

[glass_pink]
A smooth pink chip of glass, lying in the shallows.

[glass_pink_ves]
Pink, in a flooded well. Of course it is. I'll take it.

[glass_black]
A smooth black chip of glass in the tussock.

[glass_black_bramble]
It's a bit of night! Somebody cut a bit of night out and the sea rubbed it smooth!

[glass_red]
A small red chip of glass at the edge of the rock.

[glass_red_ves]
Red. The rarest, apparently. At the very end of everything, naturally.

[glass_done]
That's all ten. You line them up in your hand and the light goes through every one.

You got a JAR OF SEA GLASS, and 100 gold from the bottom of your pocket where it had gone missing.

[glass_done_bramble]
It throws colours on the wall! All ten! Can the jar sit by my bed? Please?

[glass_done_ves]
Ten colours out of one beach. The sea is better at this than I am.

# --- Chapter 6: pressed flowers. ---

[flower_marigold_ves]
Somebody pressed it in a book and the fire missed the page. I'd like to meet the book.

[flower_cornflower]
A scrap of card with a pressed cornflower on it, still blue.

[flower_cornflower_bramble]
It's still BLUE! Everything else is grey and this one stayed blue!

[flower_poppy]
A scrap of card with a pressed poppy on it, still red.

[flower_poppy_ves]
Poppy. Red as the rags on the cairns. Whoever pressed it meant it to last.

[flower_violet]
A scrap of card with a pressed violet on it, in the burnt orchard.

[flower_violet_bramble]
A violet! In the orchard! I'm not crying. That's the ash.

[flower_harebell]
A scrap of card with a pressed harebell on it, on the bare sea floor.

[flower_harebell_ves]
They grow on cliffs. Somebody carried this a long way to press it.

[flower_foxglove]
A scrap of card with a pressed foxglove on it.

[flower_foxglove_ves]
Foxglove. Pretty and poisonous. I do know some people like that.

[flower_clover]
A scrap of card with a pressed clover on it.

[flower_clover_bramble]
Is it a lucky one? It looks like a lucky one. Four leaves? ...No. Still nice.

[flower_heather]
A scrap of card with a pressed sprig of heather on it.

[flower_heather_ves]
Heather. Purple, on a grey road. I was almost getting used to the grey.

[flowers_done]
That's all eight. You slip them between the pages of a little book, and they stay.

You got a BOOK OF FLOWERS, and 110 gold from the back of the book where somebody had hidden it.

[flowers_done_bramble]
Eight flowers that the fire missed. We should show Hearth.

[flowers_done_ves]
They survived all that. The least we can do is not crush them.

[charm_sun]

[charm_moon]
A little clay charm, tucked against the wall. A crescent moon is pressed into it.

[charm_star]
A little clay charm, half under a flower bed. A star is pressed into it.

[charm_leaf]
A little clay charm in the corner. A leaf is pressed into it, veins and all.

[charm_key]
A little clay charm in a quiet corner. A key is pressed into it. Somebody wanted the Hero to feel welcome inside.

[charm_bell]
A little clay charm behind the furniture. A bell is pressed into it, and the clay is thumbprinted all round the edge.

[charm_flame]
A little clay charm by the wall. A flame is pressed into it. The glaze has gone smoky.

[charm_feather]
A little clay charm at the end of the room. A feather is pressed into it.

[charms_done]
That's all eight. You thread them on a cord, in the order of a day: sun, moon, star, and on.

You got a CHARM CORD, and 60 gold. It was knotted in the end of the cord.

[pin_axe_ves]

[pin_lamp]

[pin_lamp_ves]

[pin_bell]

[pin_bell_ves]
Out of the wind, off the path. Whoever lost this wasn't looking.

[pin_kettle]
A little enamel pin by the gate. A kettle is on it.

[pin_kettle_ves]

[pin_boot]

[pin_boot_ves]

[pins_done_ves]
Nine climbers. I'd like to say they all got home.

# --- Chapter 3: Hollin marbles. Marbles the woodcutters' children left behind. Bramble answers. ---

[marble_cats_eye_bramble]
A cat's eye! Those are the BEST. The yellow bit looks right at you.

[marble_oxblood]
A glass marble, dark red like old wine, right up in a corner of the wood.

[marble_oxblood_bramble]
An oxblood! My grandad had one. He said it won him a goat.

[marble_aggie]
A glass marble at the edge of the lane, amber with a brown streak through it.

[marble_aggie_bramble]
An aggie! That means it's a real stone one, on the outside. Or the other way round. I forget.

[marble_swirl]
A glass marble in the corner of the lookout, white with a blue twist.

[marble_swirl_bramble]
It's like a little frozen storm. Can I look at it a bit longer?

[marble_clearie]
A clear glass marble in the leaves. The light goes straight through it.

[marble_clearie_bramble]
A clearie! You can see the whole wood upside down in it. Look!

[marble_galaxy]
A glass marble, nearly black, with a purple twist and tiny specks in it.

[marble_galaxy_bramble]
It's got STARS in it. Somebody lost a sky out here.

[marble_tiger]
A glass marble in a patch of dry leaf, gold with dark stripes.

[marble_tiger_bramble]
A tiger! Rrrr. Sorry. It's what you do.

[marble_peewee]
A very small green marble, by the water.

[marble_peewee_bramble]
A peewee. The smallest. They always win by being too small to hit.

[marble_steelie]
A heavy grey marble in the reeds. It's iron, not glass.

[marble_steelie_bramble]
A steelie! It's cheating. It's the best kind of cheating.

[marble_corkscrew]
A glass marble on the ground by the square's far corner, white with a red twist like a barber's pole.

[marble_corkscrew_ves]
A corkscrew. Small, round, and it rolled all this way to the quiet end. I know how it feels.

[marbles_done]
That's all ten. You tip them into a little cloth bag, and they chink like a small laugh.

You got a BAG OF MARBLES, and 30 gold that was knotted into the drawstring.

[marbles_done_bramble]
Ten marbles! We could have a TOURNAMENT. You'd lose. Sorry. I'd teach you.
