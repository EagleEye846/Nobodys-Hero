[welcome_sign]
Welcome to the Ruins.
Use the arrow keys to walk.

Hold X or Shift to run.
Press Z to read signs, and C to open your menu.

The old floor here is fragile. Careful where you jump.

[east_sign]
This is the east room.
A rogue dummy haunts it. Touch it and it attacks!

The doorway to the north leads to the Lantern Hall.

[hall_plaque]
LANTERN HALL
The lanterns here were lit by the first Keeper, and were never to go out.

Still, dark water.

[ruins_pillars]

[ruins_statue]

# --- Lantern Hall puzzle: light the lanterns in the old order ---
[hall_riddle]
The lanterns of this hall were lit in the old way:
First the east, where the sun wakes.
Then the heart.
And last of all, the west, where it goes to sleep.

Scrawled underneath, in a different hand:
...or something, I don't know.

[hall_lantern]
You light the lantern. Its flame is small, but steady.

[hall_gate]
An iron gate. Words are carved above it:
LIGHT THE WAY.

[hall_gate_open]
The last flame catches... and with a long groan, the gate swings open.

[hall_gate_reset]
The flames gutter and go out, one by one.
That wasn't the old way.

# --- Pillar Room puzzle: two levers, one hidden ---
[pillar_lever]
You pull the lever. Stone grinds somewhere nearby.

[pillar_gate]
A wide iron gate. Two lever-shaped marks are carved into the stone beside it.

One lever must be hiding somewhere. The other is up high, out of easy reach.

[pillar_gate_open]
Clunk. The gate rattles open.

# Set dressing and small things in the Ruins (round 2 of the life pass, 15 Nov 2026). (draft)

# ruins_entrance

[r1_en_banner]
A banner hung from a rod, faded to the colour of old tea.

There was a sun on it once. Now it's mostly an idea of a sun.

[r1_en_banner2]
A shorter banner, blue, the hem torn into tongues. A moth has made it a home and a living.

[r1_en_post]
A cabinet of pigeonholes, every one labelled FOR THE HERO. Nearly all of them are empty.

One holds a letter, addressed in a nervous hand: TO WHOEVER TURNS UP.

You decide it isn't yours to open.

[r1_en_mural]
A painting in a frame, flaked almost bare. A bright sun, and a small figure under it with something held up.

The face has gone altogether. So has most of the sky.

[r1_en_wipe]
A brass plaque, polished at the edges: PLEASE WIPE YOUR FEET.

Underneath, scratched in later and smaller: ALL OF THEM.

[r1_en_ivy]
Ivy, coming in through a crack no wider than a thumb. It has done very well for itself.

[r1_en_hole]
A tiny arched door at the foot of the wall, with a mat the size of a stamp and a lantern the size of a thimble.

The lantern is lit.

[r1_en_mat]
A doormat, worn through in the middle. It says WELCOME, HERO in careful letters.

The word HERO has been crossed out. Beside it someone has written ANYONE. Under that, smaller: (NOT THE DUMMY).

[r1_en_chalk]
A slate on an easel, chalked:

DAYS SINCE LAST DUMMY INCIDENT: 0

The zero has been rubbed out and redrawn so many times it's gone shiny.

[r1_en_candles]
Three stubs of candle in a saucer, burning low. Somebody keeps them lit, and trims them, and the wax is still soft.

[r1_en_candles2]
More stubs in a saucer. The flames all lean a little toward the north door, as if they'd like a look at the Lantern Hall.

[r1_en_rubble]
Fallen stonework, heaped. Something's been living in the gaps. You hear it stop breathing when you lean in.

[r1_en_column]
A fallen column, snapped in two. Someone has used it as a bench, and someone else as a bed for moss. The moss won.

[r1_en_urn]
A cracked urn with a fern in it. The fern is delighted about the crack.

[r1_en_bench]
A stone bench, worn into a hollow by a great many people sitting down to think about it.

[r1_en_boot]
One old boot, standing up straight. There's no sign of the other one, or of the foot.

[r1_en_hop]
A hopscotch, chalked on the floor. The numbers go up to nine. After nine someone has written: ???

[r1_en_bones]
The skeleton of a very small mouse, in a thimble for a helmet, a toothpick sword in its paw.

It's lying down very bravely. You take a moment.

[r1_en_tip]
A jar beside the shop, with a hand-lettered label: TIPS. (NOT BRIBES. THEY ARE DIFFERENT.)

[r1_en_crate]
A crate of stock, lid off, packed in straw. The straw is a bit warmer than straw ought to be.

[r1_en_books]
A heap of damp books. The top one is open at a page that says Chapter One. The rest is mould.

[r1_en_leaves]
Dry leaves, a long way from any tree. The Ruins have a draught with a very good sense of direction.

[r1_en_shell]
A snail shell, empty, with a silver trail going the way it went. You can't tell if it was fast or just determined.

[r1_en_toad]
A ring of pale toadstools. You step round it out of respect. Nothing happens, which is what they wanted.

[r1_en_puddle]
A puddle, under a drip that has been dripping for a very long time. The puddle is patient too.

[r1_en_wax]
Old wax on the floor in drips, as if a candle had come this way, thinking.

[r1_en_flower]
A small pink flower growing out of a crack in the floor. You stand very still, so as not to upset it.

[r1_en_flower2]
Another one. Whoever keeps putting flowers in the cracks hasn't heard that cracks are bad.

[r1_en_stash]
A mouse's treasury behind a heap of rubble: bottle caps, shirt buttons, a bent spoon and one real coin, all sorted by size.

You take only the coin. A very small voice, somewhere, says nothing, loudly.

[r1_lh_books]
A stack of damp books held together by their mould. The top one is open at a page that just says: INTERVAL.

[r1_lh_stump]
The stump of a pillar, broken off at the knee. Ivy has moved into it as if it was always going to.

[r1_lh_column]
A fallen pillar, lying where it fell. Somebody has swept carefully round it, every day, for years.

[r1_lh_cage]

Whatever lived here left on its own.

[r1_lh_candles]

[r1_lh_urn]
An urn holding a dead stick that is quite sure it will bloom.

[r1_lh_toad]
Toadstools in a ring at the foot of a pillar, all facing the light, as if at a concert.

[r1_lh_helm]

# pillar_room

[r1_pi_banner]
A banner so old the thread has given up. It's only the dust that's holding the shape.

[r1_pi_mural]
A painting of two small figures hauling on one enormous lever. The lever is painted extremely large, to be fair to it.

[r1_pi_plaque]
A brass plaque: IN CASE OF EMERGENCY, PULL.

It doesn't say what.

[r1_pi_bell]
A bell pull, tied in a knot. That's somebody saying no.

[r1_pi_banner2]
A blue banner, shredded to the rod. It hangs the way a sleeve hangs when the arm has left it.

[r1_pi_hamper]
A picnic hamper tied with a ribbon, tucked into the corner where the pillars throw their shadow. A card on the lid:

OPEN ON ARRIVAL. (IT'S FINE. NOBODY MINDS.)

You open it. The flask inside is still sealed.

[r1_pi_column]
A pillar that lay down for a rest a long time ago. The floor has grown round it, a little.

[r1_pi_rubble]
Some of the ceiling, down on the floor. It doesn't look sorry.

[r1_pi_stump]
Another broken pillar. They'll all be like this one day. The tall ones know it. You can tell by how they stand.

[r1_pi_helm]
An old helmet, a daisy growing out of it. Somebody has left it standing up straight, as if it might still be wanted.

[r1_pi_lost]
A box marked LOST. Under it, a second label: FOUND. Under that, a third: NOT MINE.

Inside: one glove, a spoon, and an apple core that has become a fossil.

[r1_pi_candles]
Candle stubs in a saucer, burning in the corner of a very empty room. Somebody likes to leave a light on for whoever's next.

[r1_pi_candles2]
A saucer of stubs, lit. The wax has run down into a little stalagmite, which is the closest thing to a pet this room has.

[r1_pi_books]
A short stack of books with a bookmark in the top one. The bookmark is a pressed daisy, and it's the only thing in here that's been loved.

[r1_pi_bench]
A stone bench. Someone has carved a tiny arrow into the seat, pointing at the gate, and underneath: YES, THAT WAY.

[r1_pi_toad]
Toadstools, in a ring, at the foot of a pillar. They're very proud of the pillar.

[r1_pi_flower]
A flower in a crack. Whoever put it there picked the one place a pillar's shadow wouldn't kill it.

# flower_room

[r1_fl_ivy]
Ivy right up the wall, in a room with no window. It found a way. It generally does.

[r1_fl_sign]
A little sign pushed into the soil:

MOONBELLS: LOOK, DON'T PICK. THEY'VE HAD A LONG DAY.

Plant tags in the soil. MOONBELL. MOONBELL (SULKING). MOONBELL (DON'T TELL THE OTHER ONE). NOT A WEED (WEED).

[r1_fl_tags2]
More tags. LATE. LATER. LATEST. It looks as though she ran out of words for "wait".

[r1_fl_can]
A second watering can, left by the beds, nearly full. A bee is using it as a hot tub.

[r1_fl_trug]
A trug of gloves and a trowel. The gloves are so worn they've taken the shape of a hand holding a flower.

[r1_fl_gnome]
A clay gnome in a red cap with a wick sticking out of the top of it. A tag round his neck says NORBERT.

You don't ask. He looks as if he's been asked.

[r1_fl_barrow]
A wheelbarrow of pots. One wheel, and a great deal of faith.

[r1_fl_barrel]
A barrel brim-full of rainwater, which is odd indoors. You let it be odd.

[r1_fl_cloche]
A glass jar over a seedling. The seedling presses its face to the glass to look at you.

[r1_fl_cloche2]
Another jar, another seedling. This one's turned its back. You've offended it somehow.

[r1_fl_beans]
Bean canes hung with beans as long as your arm. Taper's pride, you'd guess.

[r1_fl_pot]
A pot of moonbells with their faces closed for the day. All but one, which is watching you.

[r1_fl_pot2]
A pot of moonbells, glowing a very faint blue. They ring, a bit, if you stand close and are quiet.

[r1_fl_bench]
A stone bench facing the beds, a cushion on it with a knitted flower. It's the best seat in the house, and she knows it.

[r1_fl_toad]
A ring of toadstools by the barrow. Even the weeds in here are tidy.

[r1_fl_button]
A small brass button, lying at the edge of the pool, shining like it wants to be found.
