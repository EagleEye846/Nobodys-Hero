# --- THE CHIMNEY (glimmer_chimney): a ring climb up to a chest ---

[chimney_sign]

Underneath, smaller: DON'T LOOK DOWN. (THE PINK ONES ARE DOWN.)

[chimney_face]
The chimney's wall goes up and up into the crystal.

Old iron rings are hammered into it, one above another, all the way to a shelf at the top.

[chimney_face_ves]
{bored}Someone climbed that for fun. Someone with no friends.

[chimney_pink]

[chimney_chest]
A chest on the shelf, at the top of the chimney.

Whoever left it here wanted it kept dry and very hard to get to. It worked.

# --- THE LIFE PASS: things to look at (set dressing) ---

[gp_bug]
A glowbug, lamp end up, going about its business. It lets you look for a moment, then remembers an appointment.

[gp_bug_ves]
{smile}It has a better light than we do and it's using it to commute.

[gp_bug 2]
It turns its lamp towards you, very slowly, to see who you are. Then it turns it away again. Not interested.

[gp_bug_ves 2]
{smile}Rude. But I respect a creature that knows when to stop looking.

[gp_chime]
A thin crystal stalk hung with little crystal bells. When the draught comes round it they tick together, very faintly, like someone counting under their breath.

[gp_chime_ves]
{thoughtful}Wind chimes. In a cave. Somebody's been decorating, and not for us.

[gp_chime 2]
One small bell swings, and the rest answer it, one after another, down the stalk. Then quiet again.

[gp_chime_ves 2]
{smile}Lovely note. I'm going to pretend that was me.

[gp_crumbs]
A heap of crystal crumbs, swept into a corner. Somebody tidies the Glimmering, and then never takes the dustpan anywhere.

[gp_crumbs_ves]
{bored}I respect the sweeping. I question the destination.

[gp_crumbs 2]
The pile is faintly warm and smells of sugar. Crystal sugar, probably. Probably.

[gp_crumbs_ves 2]
{bored}Don't lick it. I can see you thinking about it.

[gp_gem]
A little crystal, pink at the heart, standing on its own. It gives a faint tick when the light moves across it, as if it's thinking about chiming.

[gp_gem_ves]
{smile}A very small crystal with very big opinions. I can tell.

[gp_gem 2]
It ticks again. You get the feeling it was about to say something.

[gp_gem_ves 2]
{smile}Give it time. They always wait for the end of the room.

[gp_geode]
A geode, cracked clean in half and left where it fell. The inside is packed with crystals so tight they look like they're gossiping.

[gp_geode_ves]
{smile}Half a geode. The other half is somebody's very expensive doorstop.

[gp_geode 2]
You hold your hand up beside it. The geode is winning.

[gp_geode_ves 2]
{bored}Stop sizing it up. It's not for sale and you couldn't afford it.

[gp_puddle]
A puddle so still it holds the whole ceiling: every crystal upside down, and twice as bright.

[gp_puddle_ves]
{thoughtful}Down there it's another cave. Better lit, honestly.

[gp_puddle 2]
You lean over. Your reflection leans over. The cave in the puddle has more crystals than the one you're standing in.

[gp_puddle_ves 2]
{thoughtful}Don't fall in. It's deeper than it looks. Everything here is.

[gp_ribbon]
A scrap of red ribbon snagged on a crystal spike, so thin you can see the glow through it. Whoever lost it didn't come back.

[gp_ribbon_ves]
{bored}Somebody lost a ribbon and kept walking. Priorities.

[gp_ribbon 2]
The ribbon moves in a breeze you can't feel. It's the only thing in the cave that's moving at all.

[gp_ribbon_ves 2]
{thoughtful}Red doesn't fade down here. Nothing does.

[gp_shell]
A gleamsnail's old shell, empty. The crystals inside went on growing with nobody home to mind them.

[gp_shell_ves]
{bored}They moved out and left the house standing. That's the most snail thing I've ever heard.

[gp_shell 2]
Something has been round the inside with a tiny brush. The crystals are cleaner than anything else in the cave.

[gp_shell_ves 2]
{surprised}Somebody's still looking after it. A snail with a mortgage.

[gp_web]
A small web strung between two crystals, beaded with dew. The spider's out. It'll be back, and it'll be smug about it.

[gp_web_ves]
{surprised}Don't touch it. It's the only thing down here that's actually handmade.

[gp_web 2]
A drop of dew slides down a thread, reaches the crystal, and rings it, just barely.

[gp_web_ves 2]
{surprised}Even the spider has better timing than I do.

[lp_nibble]
A glowing mushroom with a neat bite out of it. Whoever it was left the stem, which is the polite way.

[lp_nibble_ves]
{smile}Don't look at me. I'd have taken the whole thing.

[lp_daisy]
A daisy. It came down the hole with you, landed on a moss tuft, and is now the only thing in the cave that looks pleased to be here.

[lp_daisy_ves]
{bored}It's tougher than it looks. I'm not saying that's a recommendation.

[lp_land_nook]
A twist of glowing sugar wedged in behind the crystal spires, with a glowbug asleep on top of it. It shuffles over, grudgingly, and lets you have it.

[lp_fell_tally]

[lp_fell_tally_ves]
{bored}Encouraging. At least they kept count.
