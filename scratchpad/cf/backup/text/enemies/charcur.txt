[intro]
A dog comes down the road toward you, all ribs and long legs, black as a burnt log, its tail smoking. It stops. It looks past you, down the road, and then at you. A CHARCUR!

CHARCUR lies down on the road, then gets up again at once, in case it missed them.

# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the

[talk: Inspect]
CHARCUR - HP 6000
A stray of charcoal that keeps the old road, waiting for someone to come back along it. Walls of bones with a gap, a howl in arcs, cinders dug up, and a game of fetch it plays very hard.

[talk: Hold out a hand]
You crouch and hold out the back of your hand, the way you do with a dog you don't know. CHARCUR sniffs it for a long time. It isn't the hand it wanted, but it's a hand.

[talk: Throw a stick]
You pick up a burnt stick and throw it down the road. CHARCUR goes after it like a shot and brings it back, and drops it at your feet, and looks at you, and then down the road again.

[talk: Wait with it]
You sit down at the side of the road and look the way it's looking. Nobody comes. Nobody comes for a long time. CHARCUR sits down next to you, close enough to lean.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
CHARCUR lies down in the middle of the road with its head toward the town. The glow goes out of it slowly, from the tail up. Its collar is lying in the ash.

[pacified]
CHARCUR licks your hand once, quickly, as if it might get into trouble for it. Then it trots off down the road, tail smoking, to go on waiting somewhere you can't see.
