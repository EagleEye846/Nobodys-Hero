# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/bunting.js.
[talk: Inspect]
BUNTING - HP 800
A little round bird wearing a string of party bunting. Throws the party at you: poppers, balloons, streamers, a cake, and musical chairs. Every one is a bit slower than it looks.

[talk: Wish it well]
You wish BUNTING a very happy birthday. It's overcome. It says nobody has said that all year, and then it says it's not even its birthday, and then it cries a little with joy anyway.

[talk: Join the party]
You clap along. BUNTING is thrilled. It starts a conga line of one, and invites you to be the second one. The line goes round a bush, and comes back, and it's still thrilled.

[talk: Fix the flags]
You straighten its flags, so they hang evenly. BUNTING holds very still, like a bird being painted. When you're done it checks every flag twice, and hugs the string.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
BUNTING flops over with its flags in a heap. A few last bits of confetti come down, slowly, on the grass. It's a very quiet party.

[pacified]
BUNTING flaps up into the air, scattering confetti, and does a lap of honour above your head. Then it points you cheerfully down the path, and goes to find someone else's birthday.
