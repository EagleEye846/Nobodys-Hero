[intro]
The milestone at the side of the road stands up on two square stone feet and steps into your way. The number carved on it says three. A MILESTONE!

# Shown at the start of each of your turns, in order, then repeating.
[flavor]
MILESTONE stands in the road the way milestones stand at the side of it: as if it has always been there.

The number carved on MILESTONE says three. You are sure it said four a moment ago.

MILESTONE shifts its weight. Somewhere under the ash, the road shifts with it.

A little soot runs down MILESTONE's face like a tear. It is only soot.

Smells of wet stone and old moss, burnt.

[flavor: spareable]
MILESTONE is leaning back toward the side of the road, where it used to stand.

# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/milestone.js.
[talk: Inspect]
MILESTONE - HP 6400
An old stone that counted the miles to the buried town, and got up to count them nearer. Throws its numbers, sends chips down the road, drops little stones out of the sky and points the way, hard.

[talk: Trace the number]
You run your finger round the number carved in MILESTONE's face, slowly, the whole way round. It is a three. MILESTONE stands very still, as if being read was the thing it was made for.

[talk: Scrape off the soot]
You rub the soot off MILESTONE's face with your sleeve. Under it the stone is pale and the letters are sharp. MILESTONE leans closer to let you get at the top.

[talk: Walk the mile with it]
You fall in beside MILESTONE and walk with it a little way along the road, step for step. It is very slow. You don't hurry it. When you stop, the number on it says two.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
MILESTONE topples over onto its back in the road with a long, grinding thump. The number on it says nought. A chip has come off the corner.

[pacified]
MILESTONE steps back to the side of the road, turns round to face it, and settles into the ash until it's standing where a milestone ought to. The number says three. It will wait.
