[intro]
Water slops over a bank, and a green foal climbs out of it on legs that don't match. It sees you, rears up as high as it can, and falls over. Then it gets up and does it again. A KELPIE FOAL!

# Shown at the start of each of your turns, in order, then repeating.
[flavor]
KELPIE FOAL paws the ground, one hoof, then another. It has lost track of which it started with.

Weed drips from KELPIE FOAL's mane. It shakes its head, throws water everywhere, and looks, for a moment, very much like a proper kelpie.

KELPIE FOAL tries a frightening whinny. It comes out as a squeak. It clears its throat and tries again, lower.

KELPIE FOAL gallops in a circle and stops, panting, to check you were watching.

Smells like pond weed, wet horse, and a morning in spring.

# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/kelpie_foal.js.
[talk: Inspect]
KELPIE FOAL - HP 1900
A young water-horse, practising being frightening. Gallops laps of the box (kick on the fourth beat), leaps where only its shadow shows, and tugs you toward its kelp.

[talk: Pretend to be frightened]
You gasp, and stagger back, and hold your hands up. KELPIE FOAL stares. Then it rears, as high as it can, throws its head back, and lets out a neigh that is only a little squeaky. It trots in a very pleased circle. You've made its whole week.

[talk: Offer a sugar lump]
You hold out a sugar lump on your flat hand. KELPIE FOAL snorts at it, suspicious. It sniffs again. A warm, whiskery muzzle takes it from you, and its ears go all the way forward, and then it leans its whole wet head against your shoulder.

[talk: Hold still and let it sniff]
You stand quite still while it sniffs you over, hair, hands, bag. A lot of water ends up on your coat. KELPIE FOAL sneezes, once, and decides you smell like a safe place. It folds its legs under it and sits down. Its legs don't match, even then.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
KELPIE FOAL folds up on the bank with a long wet sigh, a heap of weed with a muzzle. After a moment the weed goes still, and the pond goes quiet, and something small and green slips away under the surface.

[pacified]
KELPIE FOAL gives you a last, wet, whiskery nudge. Then it wheels round, bolts for the water, and dives in with a splash that soaks everyone, itself included. A green ripple goes away across the pond, whinnying.
