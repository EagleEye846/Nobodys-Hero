# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/fiddler.js.
[talk: Inspect]
FIDDLER - HP 1200
A blind cave cricket that fiddles with its leg. It hears you, not sees you. Strings, feelers and phrases, and it plays your own footsteps back at you. Move on, and don't double back.

[talk: Applaud]
You clap, softly, three times. FIDDLER freezes. Then it bows so low its feelers touch the floor. It says nobody has ever clapped. They mostly ran. It plays an encore, with its whole heart.

[talk: Hum along]
You hum along with the tune. It's not the right note. FIDDLER tilts its head, and adjusts, ever so slightly, and then you're in harmony, and neither of you knows quite how it happened.

[talk: Keep very quiet]
You stand perfectly still, and don't breathe. FIDDLER sweeps its feelers across you, and finds nothing. It says "...Hello?" very gently, to the dark. Then, quietly, it relaxes. It isn't alone, and it knows.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
FIDDLER lies down, folds its long legs under it, and plays one last, soft note that fades out. The cave is a lot quieter now than you remember.

[pacified]
FIDDLER plays a slow, lovely tune just for you, and it sounds like every place you've been. Then it bows, and feels its way off into the dark, still playing.
