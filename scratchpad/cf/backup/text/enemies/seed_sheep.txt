# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/seed_sheep.js.
[talk: Inspect]
SEED SHEEP - HP 1850
A sheep with a dandelion clock for a fleece. Each baa turns the wind, its flock hops the fence (harmless in the air), and its wish clocks burst when you come near.

[talk: Scratch behind the ears]
You scratch SEED SHEEP behind both ears, slowly. Its eyes close. Its whole fleece loosens by half an inch and about forty seeds sigh off into the air. It leans into your hand with all its weight.

[talk: Count its seeds]
You start to count its seeds, one finger at a time. SEED SHEEP waits politely until you lose your place around three hundred, and then starts again from the top, to be helpful. You both give up at the same time.

[talk: Make a wish]
You hold a seed up and make a wish. SEED SHEEP watches you very seriously. You blow, and the seed goes up past the hedge, and the sheep gives a small, satisfied baa, as if it were the one who'd asked.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
SEED SHEEP sits down in the grass with a soft whump. Its seeds all go at once, a slow white cloud lifting off the field. What is left is small and round and rather surprised, and very pink.

[pacified]
SEED SHEEP fluffs back up to twice its size, shakes itself, and sends a whole sky of seeds up over your head. It gives you a slow, sleepy baa, and wanders off, a little less round and rather happier.
