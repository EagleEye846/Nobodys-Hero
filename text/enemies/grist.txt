GRIST keeps glancing at the door. Just checking where it is.

Smells like old flour and wet wood.

[talk: Inspect]
GRIST - HP 460
A rat who moved into the empty mill and gave itself a job. It's very proud of it.

[talk: Admire the mill]
You look round the mill and nod, impressed. GRIST puffs up so much flour falls off.

"Built it myself! ...Well. Kept it. Keeping's harder."

[talk: Call it Miller]
You call it Miller. GRIST stops. Its whiskers twitch.

"Nobody's ever called me that. Except me. Say it again?"

[talk: Say "boo"]
You lean in and go "boo". GRIST jumps clean out of its apron.

It climbs back in very slowly, like that was on purpose.
