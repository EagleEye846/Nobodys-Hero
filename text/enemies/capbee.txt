[intro]
A plump bumblebee drifts into your path, in a tweed flat cap and a very put-upon expression. He looks you up and down like someone who has seen better clover. A FLATCAP BEE!

# Shown at the start of each of your turns, in order, then repeating.
[flavor]
FLATCAP BEE hovers, arms folded, and sniffs. The cap does not move. The cap has never moved.

FLATCAP BEE says that in his day clover was clover. He does not say what it is now.

FLATCAP BEE straightens his cap, then checks the clover for signs of enthusiasm. There are some. He is displeased.

A little honey falls from FLATCAP BEE onto the grass. He tuts and wipes it off with his sleeve, which is also honey.

Smells like clover, warm wax, and old tweed.

# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/capbee.js.
[talk: Inspect]
FLATCAP BEE - HP 1800
A bumblebee in a flat cap. His mates dance across in weaving lines, he lets honey drip from above, and he throws his cap like a boomerang. It comes back the way it went.

[talk: Ask about the clover]
You ask FLATCAP BEE about the clover. His whole body unclenches. He tells you about the red, and the white, and the one patch by the wall that flowers late. He is still going when you stop listening and, a long while after, when you start again.

[talk: Admire the cap]
You say it is a very fine cap. FLATCAP BEE takes it off and shows you the inside. There is a name sewn in it. It is not his. He says it is his father's, and he is in no hurry to find out whose it was before.

[talk: Compliment the honey]
You say the honey smells wonderful. FLATCAP BEE tries to look as if he hasn't heard, and fails completely. He offers you a taste on the end of one finger. It is, by a distance, the best thing you have eaten all week.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
FLATCAP BEE drops, spinning, and lands on his back in the clover with his legs in the air and the cap over his face. Under it he says that it is only a flesh wound, and that he would like to be left here a bit.

[pacified]
FLATCAP BEE takes off his cap, holds it to his chest, and bows from the middle. He buzzes straight up over the hedge, trailing a thin gold line of honey, towards a patch of clover that is, for once, perfectly all right.
