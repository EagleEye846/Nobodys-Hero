# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/wisp.js.
[talk: Inspect]
WISP - HP 540
A marsh light that copies the lanterns to lead people off the path. It just wants company.

[talk: Keep it company]
You stay where you are and let the WISP float beside you for a while. It doesn't say anything. It glows a little warmer.

[talk: Wave back]
You wave at the WISP. It wobbles, astonished, then tries to wave back. It doesn't have hands. It waves its whole self.

[talk: Say it glows nicely]
You tell the WISP it glows very nicely. It goes so bright with pride its lid nearly falls off.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
WISP flickers, smaller and smaller, and goes out. Out in the fog, another light comes on. It might be the same one.

[pacified]
WISP circles you twice, happily. Then it drifts off into the fog, looking back the whole way, as if you might still follow.
