# THE WARDEN OF THE GATE: the colossal stone statue of the Hero before the
# Hero's Gate, built by the old city to greet him. It has waited three
# hundred years, and it has decided you are not him.
# Numbers (HP, damage, its phases) live in src/data/enemies/warden.js.
# It can't be spared: nothing here builds MERCY. It has to break.
#
# It speaks in capitals, slowly and formally, like something carved. It
# isn't cruel. It's doing what it was made to do.
#
# A section with ": welcome", ": judgement" or ": ruin" after its name is
# used in that phase of its fight instead of the plain one ([flavor: ruin]
# instead of [flavor]). [phase: ...] sections are what's said as a phase
# starts: plain ones in the battle panel, "vesper" ones by Ves in her box,
# "foe" ones by it in speech bubbles.

# Said in speech bubbles during its entrance, after its title card. Lines written
# together (no blank line between) share a bubble; a line starting with
# ... waits for a bubble of its own, and words that won't fit run on into
# the next.
[boss_intro]
I WAS RAISED TO WELCOME HIM.
I WILL KNOW HIM WHEN I SEE HIM.

YOU ARE NOT HIM.

THEN I WILL DO
THE OTHER THING I WAS RAISED FOR.

NONE PASS BUT THE HERO.

[intro]
The WARDEN OF THE GATE lifts its foot from its plinth. The whole court shakes.

# Shown at the start of each of your turns, in order, then repeating.
[flavor]
The WARDEN holds its stone sword high, the way it has for three hundred years.

Moss falls from the WARDEN's shoulders every time it moves.

The WARDEN's eyes are pale, and steady, and fixed on you.

Carved flowers hang round the WARDEN's neck. Someone painted them pink, a long time ago.

Ves leans on her crutch behind you, and doesn't look away from it.

[flavor: judgement]
The WARDEN looks you up and down, and up and down again.

A crack runs across the WARDEN's chest, through the carved sun.

The WARDEN's garland has come half away. The petals lie all round your feet.

The WARDEN holds out its open hand, measuring. You come up to its knee.

[flavor: ruin]
The WARDEN's sword is broken off at the hilt. It's still holding it up.

Stones fall from the Gate behind it, one at a time, like a slow clock.

One of the WARDEN's eyes has gone dark.

The WARDEN is coming apart, and it still won't move out of the way.

Dust pours from the WARDEN's cracks every time it breathes. It doesn't breathe. It's stone.

# What happens as each phase starts (see `beat` in warden.js).
[phase: judgement]
A crack runs up the WARDEN, from its plinth to its chest, with a sound like ice on a pond.

The WARDEN stoops, slowly, and looks at you properly for the first time.

[phase: judgement vesper]
{determined}It's measuring you. Like a tailor.

{grin}Bad news, statue. They don't come in hero size.

[phase: judgement foe]
I WILL MEASURE YOU
AGAINST THE ONE I WAIT FOR.

[phase: ruin]
The WARDEN's sword snaps at the hilt, and the blade comes down across the court.

Behind it, the Hero's Gate groans. Stones begin to fall from the arch.

[phase: ruin vesper]
{scared}The Gate! It's coming down with it!

{resolute}...Then let it. We'll climb over the bits. Keep going!

[phase: ruin foe]
I AM BREAKING.
I WILL STAND
UNTIL I AM BROKEN.

# Ves's turn prompt, in each phase. She's hurt: her turn is COVER and CALL OUT.
[vesper_turn: welcome]
Ves grips her crutch and squints up at the WARDEN. "It's so big." Her turn.

Ves keeps right behind you, where its sword can't reach her. Her turn.

[vesper_turn: judgement]
Ves glares up at the WARDEN, like she's daring it to measure her too. Her turn.

Ves shifts her bad leg. "Nobody's the right size for a statue." Her turn.

[vesper_turn: ruin]
Ves covers her head as dust comes down. "Nearly! It's nearly done!" Her turn.

Ves holds on to your shoulder. She's shaking, but she's grinning. Her turn.

"WE got here," she says. "Nobody else did."
The WARDEN is quiet. A stone falls from its shoulder.
