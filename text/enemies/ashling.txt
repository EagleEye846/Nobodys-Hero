# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/ashling.js.
[talk: Inspect]
ASHLING - HP 5300
What the fire left, packed together and trying to be somebody. Throws flakes, balls of ash and its own ember eyes, and crumbles in walls. Mind where a ball is marked to land.

[talk: Pat it into shape]
You scoop up what has fallen off it and pat it back on, round the middle, firm, the way you would a snowman. ASHLING stands quite still and lets you. When you step back it is the best shape it has ever been.

[talk: Give it a hat]
You find a burnt pot lid by the road and set it on ASHLING's head. It is too big. ASHLING tips its head back to see out from under it, and keeps it on.

[talk: Stand very still]
You stand as still as it does. For a while there are two of you at the side of the road, not moving, while the ash comes down. ASHLING seems to find this very companionable.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
ASHLING slumps into a heap. The two embers glow on in it a little while, and then they don't. A button is lying in the ash where its middle was.

[pacified]
ASHLING bows, very carefully, holding on to its head. Then it shuffles off along the road, shedding a little, to stand somewhere somebody might see it.
