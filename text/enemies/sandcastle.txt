[intro]
Three towers and a keep, turned out of a bucket and crowned with a lolly-stick flag, shuffle across the sand toward you. The door opens like a mouth. A SANDCASTLE!

# Shown at the start of each of your turns, in order, then repeating.
[flavor]
SANDCASTLE trickles a little from its top turret and tidies itself, grain by grain.

The flag snaps in the wind. SANDCASTLE straightens it with a creak.

A shell in the wall falls out and is put back, with great dignity.

SANDCASTLE looks out to sea, and does not see a thing wrong with it.

Smells of sun cream, wet sand and a bucket.

# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/sandcastle.js.
[talk: Inspect]
SANDCASTLE - HP 2000
A sandcastle that has come to life and is sure it will last. Fires sand from its turrets, sweeps its flag round like a gate, and sends its moat out along the floor in a wave. Always a gap in the wave. Don't count on the tide.

[talk: Admire the towers]
You walk slowly round it, looking up, and nod at every tower in turn. SANDCASTLE swells until a little sand runs off its keep. It has never been so pleased in its life. It will last, it thinks, forever.

[talk: Pat the walls]
You pat the wall, gently, to firm it up, the way you'd set a sandcastle. SANDCASTLE holds perfectly still. Nobody has ever done this on purpose. A little trickle of sand runs out of its doorway, which is how it cries.

[talk: Point at the sea]
You point at the sea, and then at SANDCASTLE. SANDCASTLE says the tide is not coming in. You point again, and make a little wave with your hand. SANDCASTLE says, firmly, that it is not, and then looks out to sea for a long time. Its flag droops. It is going to be very brave.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
The towers go first, one by one, and then the keep, with the flag still on it, and then a tide of its own sand runs out into the moat. All that's left is a low round heap with a lolly stick in it. It looks like it has been very happy.

[pacified]
SANDCASTLE straightens its flag, puts its shells in a neat row, and bows from the keep. Then it lets you pass, shuffling over, and holds the door for you, as if there might be a party.
