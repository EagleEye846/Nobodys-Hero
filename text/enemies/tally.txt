# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the TALK options in
# src/data/enemies/tally.js.
[talk: Check]
QUIRE - HP 17500
The Welcome Register of the Dead City, with a splinter of star in its binding. It is counting everyone who promised to come. It is trying to get the number to come out right.

[talk: Check: late_fees]
QUIRE - HP 17500
It keeps losing its place. Each time it starts again, the sums come out angrier.

[talk: Check: margins]
QUIRE - HP 17500
It's all coming off the page. There is no list any more, only counting.

[talk: Stand in line]
You stand very straight, at the end of an invisible queue. QUIRE stops. It looks along it, and finds you, and can't think why you weren't there before.

[talk: Raise a hand]
You raise your hand, the way you would for a register. QUIRE's quill hangs in the air. "...Present?" it whispers, as if it had been afraid to ask.

[talk: Keep very quiet]
You keep so quiet that the ink stops dripping. QUIRE holds its breath, which it doesn't have. For the first time in a hundred years it can hear itself think.

[talk: Sign the book]
QUIRE holds out its great pages, and a quill. You write one word. It takes a long time to read it out. "...No-bod-y. Nobody." A pause. "...Present."

# Ves's own kind word (TALK): she does it for you.
[vesper_talk]
Ves says she'll sign the book too, if it likes. It writes her name down, and gets the spelling right, which makes it sob like a printing press.

Ves tells QUIRE it can stop counting the ones who aren't here. The ones who are here are a nice number. It tries it out loud.

Ves says the Hero was never going to come, and nobody put it on the list that they'd be sorry. QUIRE writes "sorry" in the margin and underlines it.

# Said when you try to RUN.
[no_escape]
The pages have closed round you like a door. The room is the book now.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
QUIRE goes up in a gust of loose pages, all its figures spilling out of it. Nobody on its list came to see it end.

[pacified]
QUIRE closes itself gently, like a book at the end of a long day. The splinter in its binding comes loose and drifts out into your hand. "...Everyone present," it says. "Everyone. Present."
