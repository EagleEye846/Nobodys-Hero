[intro]
A knitted cap rises out of the fog on the deck, and under it a striped jersey, and under that nothing but mist. In one hand is a swab. In the other, held out to you, is a letter. A DECKHAND!

# Shown at the start of each of your turns, in order, then repeating.
[flavor]
DECKHAND hums something under his breath. It sounds like it had words once, and they were about a girl.

He holds the letter out to you. The seal is still red. It has been waiting a long time for someone to take it.

The swab goes round and round the same boards. They are the cleanest boards in the world, and have been for a hundred years.

He looks at you, politely, and then past you, at the door. Nobody ever comes through it.

Smells of wet rope, lye soap and an old, old Sunday.

[flavor: low]
DECKHAND is wearing thin. You can see the boards through his jersey, and he is still holding out the letter.

[flavor: spareable]
DECKHAND has put down the swab. He is holding the letter with both hands now, as if he'd like you to have it, and is not sure how to say so.

# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/ghost_deckhand.js.
[talk: Inspect]
DECKHAND - HP 12400
A boy of the Fair Return's crew, still on watch. Pumps the bilge out of the next scupper along, slings a bucket on every stroke of the bell, and his letters open where they land. Means no harm.

[talk: Ask the day]
You point at your wrist, then at him: what day is it? DECKHAND thinks about it hard. He says it was a Tuesday. It was definitely a Tuesday, and he'd be paid on Friday, and then he'd go home. He is quiet for a while, and the swab stops.

[talk: Take the letter]
You step in and take the letter from his hand. It weighs nothing at all. DECKHAND looks at his empty hand, and then at you, and something in his face lets go. He says thank you, very quietly, as if he had been wanting to say it all this time.

[talk: Hum along]
You pick up the tune he has been humming and hum it, a note behind. DECKHAND stops. Then he starts again, from the beginning, slower, with you, and for a verse and a half the two of you get all the way to the part about the girl.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
DECKHAND sets the letter down very carefully on the deck, as if it were the last thing he had to do. Then he comes apart into mist, a cap and a jersey's worth, and the swab clatters on the boards.

[pacified]
DECKHAND puts his letter in your hand and closes your fingers over it. He says it's all right, he knows how long the post takes, and he tips his knitted cap to you, to the ship, and to a girl who isn't there, and fades, cap first, into the fog.
