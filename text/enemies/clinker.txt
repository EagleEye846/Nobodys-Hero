# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
CLINKER pulls itself all the way into its mug. The mug rolls away down the drain, clanking crossly.

[pacified]
CLINKER gives the mug one last proud CLANK, for you this time. Then it waddles off to bang on something else.
