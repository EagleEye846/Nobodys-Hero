[intro]
A heap of old line on the floor lifts itself off the stone, and the heap was a rope, and the rope has a weight on the end of it. A grey lead, stamped with a number, with two pale slits for eyes. It starts to swing. A PLUMB LEAD!

# Shown at the start of each of your turns, in order, then repeating.
[flavor]
PLUMB LEAD swings, and murmurs a number, and swings. The number is never quite the same twice.

The number on PLUMB LEAD's belly is 7. Then 8. Then, a little crossly, 7.

PLUMB LEAD lowers itself an inch on its line and listens, as though the floor might say something.

A loose fibre of PLUMB LEAD's line lifts in the draught and points down. It always points down.

Smells like wet rope, tallow and a very old and very patient job.

[flavor: spareable]

# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/plumb_lead.js.
[talk: Inspect]
PLUMB LEAD - HP 8400
A sounder's lead on a frayed line, still taking soundings. Swings its line across the box like a clock, rains tags stamped with depths (the bigger the number, the faster it falls), and hauls the floor of the box up from below.

[talk: Call out a depth]
You call out a depth, as the old sounders did: "By the mark, twelve!" PLUMB LEAD goes quite still on its line. It says "twelve" back, very carefully, and looks at its own belly, and its slits go wide. It has not had a reading confirmed in a hundred years.

[talk: Hold the line taut]
You take hold of the loose line and hold it, just taut, so the lead hangs true. PLUMB LEAD stops its swinging. It lets out a long breath of old rope. It says that is how it is meant to be held. It says nobody has held it right since the Board.

[talk: Tie off the loose end]
You find the frayed end and tie it off with a small neat knot, the kind a sounder uses. PLUMB LEAD swings once, to see the difference. It says it feels like being on a boat again. It says it does not remember what a boat was, but the knot is right.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
PLUMB LEAD's line parts at the top, quite gently, and the weight goes down the way it was always meant to, and does not stop swinging until the floor. It lies there cracked, with a number on it that has worn off. It looks like a weight.

[pacified]
PLUMB LEAD hangs dead straight and the number on its belly stops at 1. It lowers itself, loop by loop, into a neat coil on the stone, and lies there, and does not swing any more. It has been told the bottom. That was all it was ever asking.
