The plume stirs in the wind. LIMPET KNIGHT does not stir.

LIMPET KNIGHT raises its visor a crack, looks at you, and lowers it again with a click.

Somewhere inside the armour, a very small voice recites regulations.

Smells of rock pool, polish and duty.

# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/limpet_knight.js.
[talk: Inspect]
LIMPET KNIGHT - HP 2250
A limpet in tiny armour, very serious. Bashes with a wide plate, sucks you toward its rock, and rolls stones down it. Everything it does is meant to stop you moving. So keep moving.

[talk: Salute]
You put your hand to your brow, properly, with your elbow up. LIMPET KNIGHT freezes. Then it gives the smallest, sharpest salute you have ever seen, and the plume trembles a little with feeling.

[talk: Ask to pass]
You ask, very formally, whether you might pass. LIMPET KNIGHT considers it for a long moment. Nobody has ever asked. They've only gone round. It is not sure what the form is.

[talk: Admire the shield]
You look at the shield for a good while and say it's a fine piece of work. The limpet on it has the same face as the knight. LIMPET KNIGHT turns it very slightly in the light. That is the one it likes best.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
The shell slides off with a scrape and the soft little foot underneath is only a limpet after all. The sword rattles down. The shield rolls in a slow circle and lies flat. The knight stays exactly where it is, which is the only thing it ever wanted to do.

[pacified]
LIMPET KNIGHT lowers its sword, bows to the ground with its whole shell, and stands aside as if it had meant to all along. The plume ruffles. It holds the rock still, but it lets you by.
