[intro]
You trip over a stump. The stump says "OW." A KNOTLING!

# Shown at the start of each of your turns, in order, then repeating.
[flavor]
KNOTLING glares out of its knot-hole. It's been glaring since before you were born.

KNOTLING creaks. It might be its roots. It might be a very long sigh.

A woodpecker lands on KNOTLING, takes one look at its face, and leaves.

KNOTLING's little red shoot is trying very hard to grow. KNOTLING pretends not to notice it.

Smells like sap and sawdust. KNOTLING would rather you didn't mention the sawdust.

# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/knotling.js.
[talk: Inspect]
KNOTLING - HP 420
A cross old stump. Cut down once, and it has never, ever let it go.

[talk: Count its rings]
You count KNOTLING's rings. It puffs up proudly. You lose count at ninety. It tells you the rest. All of them.

[talk: Say sorry]
You say sorry for the woodcutters, and the saw, and all of it. KNOTLING goes quiet. "...Nobody ever says sorry."

[talk: Sit on it]
You sit on KNOTLING. It's appalled. You get a splinter. Then it sighs. Nobody's sat on it in years. It missed this.
