# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/blindfish.js.
[talk: Inspect]
BLINDFISH - HP 1150
A pale cave fish with no eyes and a very good idea of where you are. It hunts the ripples you make, and it can hear you stop.

[talk: Stay perfectly still]
You hold absolutely still. The water goes flat. BLINDFISH circles, listening, and can't find you. It looks quite lost, for something with no eyes. It seems to relax a little.

[talk: Ripple the water]
You trail one finger through the water, slow and gentle. BLINDFISH turns its whole body toward the ripple, and follows it, and follows it, and forgets for a moment what it came up for.

[talk: Drop a pebble for it]
You drop a pebble in. Plink. BLINDFISH goes after the ring like a dog after a ball, comes back with nothing, and is thrilled anyway.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
BLINDFISH goes belly-up and sinks into the black water without a ripple. For once, nothing hears it go.

[pacified]
BLINDFISH sinks, slow and calm, and the rings it leaves go out one by one until the lake is flat. It lets you by.
