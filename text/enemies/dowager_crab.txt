# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/dowager_crab.js.
[talk: Inspect]
DOWAGER CRAB - HP 7600
A hermit crab in a bathtub, in pearls. Blows bubbles, tolls her bell, snips her claws and shies ducks. When she loses patience the bath water comes out too. She is not a bully. She is a hostess whose house has been entered. Manners work on her.

[talk: Wipe your feet]
You drag one boot across the shingle, then the other, and then both again, slowly, where she can see. DOWAGER CRAB leans out of the tub to check. "Thank you," she says, as if it hurt. The monocle drops a little.

[talk: Admire the tub]
You walk round the bathtub once and nod at the taps, the blue band, the iron feet. DOWAGER CRAB goes pink under the pearls. "Clawfoot," she says. "Admiralty issue. They don't make the enamel any more."

[talk: Point at the bell]
You point at the ship's bell on her tub and raise your eyebrows. "That," says DOWAGER CRAB, "is my doorbell. The sea sent it in, one winter. Nobody has used it in years." She seems to be waiting for you to ring it. You don't. She approves.

[talk: Curtsey]
You bow as low as you can without falling over. DOWAGER CRAB, after a moment, dips the whole tub back at you, and the bell rings once. It is the first time in a long time someone has done it properly.

[talk: Apologise for the sand]
You hold up one boot and let a little sand trickle out of it onto the shingle. You say sorry with your face. DOWAGER CRAB stares at the sand, and at you. "It is a beach," she says slowly. "Sand is, I suppose, to be expected."

# Said when you try to RUN.
[no_escape]
DOWAGER CRAB is standing in the only gap in the rocks, and she does not look as if she moves for anyone.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
DOWAGER CRAB folds into her tub with a long, offended "Well!". The bell on top gives one short clang, to say it is not sorry either.

[pacified]
DOWAGER CRAB looks at your feet for a long moment. Then she dips her whole tub to you, and the bell rings a thin, polite note. "You may go through," she says. "Take the bell, dear. It was only ever a doorbell. And do shut the gate."
