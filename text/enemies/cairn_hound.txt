A dog of stacked stones, guarding the old city's dead. Heavy. Loyal. Hits like rocks.

[talk: Call it a good dog]
You tell CAIRN HOUND it's a good dog. The best dog. CAIRN HOUND's tail goes clack clack clack. It's been a long time since anyone said.

[talk: Add a stone to it]

[talk: Throw a stick]
You throw a stick. CAIRN HOUND watches it go. It looks at you. It looks at the stones it's guarding. It doesn't go. But it really, really wants to.
