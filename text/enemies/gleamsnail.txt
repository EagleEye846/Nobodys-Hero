# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/gleamsnail.js.
[talk: Inspect]
GLEAMSNAIL - HP 950
A snail with a cracked geode for a shell. Carries its house, and keeps it spotless. Slow, but its crystals ring and its light bends. Mind the slime.

[talk: Admire the house]
You tell GLEAMSNAIL it's a lovely house. A truly fine one. GLEAMSNAIL's eyestalks rise as high as they'll go. It points out the chimney. And the door. And the doormat.

[talk: Wipe your feet]
You stop, and wipe your feet on its mat, carefully, one then the other. GLEAMSNAIL watches every bit of it. Then it has to go inside for a moment, to be alone with its feelings.

[talk: Offer to dust]
You offer to dust the crystals. GLEAMSNAIL considers this for a long time, with its whole body. Then it lets you, one crystal at a time, and says "Not THAT one" about four times.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
GLEAMSNAIL draws into its shell and the door clicks shut. Nothing comes out. After a while you notice the little sign on the door says BACK SOON. You think it means it.

[pacified]
GLEAMSNAIL lifts its head, rings its crystals once, and slides aside to let you by. The doormat has been swept. For you.
