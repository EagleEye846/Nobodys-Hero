[intro]
A gull the size of a sheepdog lands in front of you, squares up, and puts both taped wing-tips under its chin. Behind it is a very full sack. A GULLION!

# Shown at the start of each of your turns, in order, then repeating.
[flavor]
GULLION bounces on its toes, fists up, and glares at you over its dented tin cap. The sack on its back clinks.

GULLION eyes your pockets. Then your hands. Then your pockets again. It is not subtle and has never needed to be.

A spoon slides out of the sack and falls on the ground. GULLION steps on it and looks at the sky.

GULLION throws a one-two at the air, and the air, which is fast, gets out of the way.

Smells like fish and chips, and cold salt air.

# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/gullion.js.
[talk: Inspect]
GULLION - HP 1850
A brawler gull with a swag sack. Dives at you and drops chips as he goes, throws off feathers, lobs the junk from his sack in arcs, and fights with two jabs and a haymaker. Mind the line he dives along.

[talk: Offer a chip]
You hold out a chip, flat on your palm. GULLION goes absolutely still. Then he snatches it, drops his fists, and swallows it whole with his eyes shut. A very small, very happy noise comes out of him, and he looks about to cry.

[talk: Check your pockets]
You turn out your pockets, to show him there's nothing worth taking. GULLION looks into every one. Then, quietly, he puts something back in your hand: a button. He says he was going to give it back anyway.

[talk: Admire the sack]
You heft the sack, very impressed, and give him a slow nod of respect. GULLION puffs out his chest. He starts to show you things. Four teaspoons. A kettle lid. A boot for a left foot. He says it is a collection, not stealing.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
GULLION goes down on his back with his feet in the air and the sack bursting. Out roll the spoons, the boots, the buttons, a lot of chips. He sits up and starts gathering them again, a little dazed.

[pacified]
GULLION sits on his sack, cracks a chip in half, and gives you the bigger bit. Then he gives you your own button, a spoon you didn't know you'd lost, and a very dignified nod. He flaps off, a lighter bird.
