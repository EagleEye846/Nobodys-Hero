[intro]
The black water in the well goes still, then goes up. It stands, taller than the rim, with the outline of somebody's head and shoulders. A TIDEWIGHT! There are small, steady lamps hanging in it.

# Shown at the start of each of your turns, in order, then repeating.
[flavor]
TIDEWIGHT sways on a swell that isn't there. The little lamps inside it sway a beat late, as though they were on ropes.

TIDEWIGHT's arms hang a little too far. Water runs off its fingers, up, and back into them.

The light inside TIDEWIGHT is the old, steady amber of a work lamp. It doesn't flicker. It has been lit a very long time.

TIDEWIGHT turns its head toward the sound of the sea in the rock, and then, slowly, toward you, like a thing that has learned to wait.

Smells like the bottom of a very deep well.

# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/tidewight.js.
[talk: Inspect]
TIDEWIGHT - HP 8800
A shape of black water from the deepest well. It rises in the box and slows you below it: stay above the surface. It opens whirlpools that pull you in, and drifts old sounders' work lamps through; the middle one flares. It only stands at high tide.

[talk: Call out a depth]
You call out a depth, the way the sounders did: "By the mark, three!" TIDEWIGHT goes very still. Then, in a voice like a bucket being let down a long way, it answers: "And a half." Its lamps all brighten, together.

[talk: Lower a lamp on a line]
You hold your lamp out over the water, as if on a line, and let the light go down. TIDEWIGHT leans over it, all that black, and watches the little flame. It has seen a great many lamps go down. It hasn't seen one come back up in a long time, and it waits to be sure yours does.

[talk: Sit by the edge]
You sit down by the edge of the well and put your boots in the shallows. TIDEWIGHT lowers itself, bit by bit, until its face is level with yours. It doesn't do anything. It has never had anyone sit with it. It seems to find out what that is, slowly.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
TIDEWIGHT sinks back into its well all at once, with a long, hollow slosh. The little lamps go down one at a time, still lit, until the dark takes them. The water is only water again.

[pacified]
TIDEWIGHT lets itself down, level, quiet, until it is only a dark circle in the floor with a few amber lights going down in it, and then it is only the circle. The surface closes over, smooth as a lid.
