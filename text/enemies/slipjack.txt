# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/slipjack.js.
[talk: Inspect]
SLIPJACK - HP 470
An eel that rides the culvert currents for fun. The fastest thing in the drains. It'll tell you.

[talk: Clap]
You clap. SLIPJACK does a little loop of pure joy and nearly swims into a wall.

[talk: Ask to race]
You point down the culvert. SLIPJACK's eyes go very wide. A race? With it? It's already won. It goes again anyway.

[talk: Count how fast]
You count on your fingers while it does a lap. SLIPJACK waits to hear the number. Any number. It's sure it's a record.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
SLIPJACK goes limp as a wet sock and lets the current carry it off round the bend. "Wasn't even trying," it says, very quietly.

[pacified]
SLIPJACK does one last lap, a lap of honour, then shoots off down the culvert. Far away, you hear it go "wheee".
