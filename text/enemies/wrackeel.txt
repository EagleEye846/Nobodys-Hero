# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the

[talk: Inspect]
WRACKEEL - HP 2050
A teal eel wired wrong. Darts in zigzags, links its sparks into a chain, closes in rings with a gap that turns, and lights up whole rows. Keep moving, and keep off the wet.

[talk: Offer a rubber boot]
You set a rubber boot down in the shallows. WRACKEEL sniffs it, loops round it twice, and gives a long, happy fizz. It is the first thing in years that didn't hurt to touch.

[talk: Hum a low note]
You hum something low and steady. WRACKEEL's sparks drop into time with it, blink, blink, blink, like a harbour light. It sways. For once it isn't doing anything at all.

[talk: Look away politely]
You turn your head and study the clouds. WRACKEEL goes very still, then lets out a breath that sizzles. It says no one has ever looked away before. They always stare at the sparks.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
WRACKEEL slumps in the shallows with a last, soft crackle. The water goes dark and ordinary. Something small in the lane starts to breathe again.

[pacified]
WRACKEEL curls into a loose, glowing ring, bows its blunt head, and slips away under the water. The puddles it leaves behind are warm for a moment.
