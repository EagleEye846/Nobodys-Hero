# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/old_gulper.js.
[talk: Inspect]
OLD GULPER - HP 13000
A moray eel older than the town, living in its fallen bell. Lunges, blows bubbles, whips his tail, sucks the water in and rings the bell. He is not wicked. He is in pain, and he has not slept properly in a very long time.

[talk: Look in his mouth]
You lean in, as close as you dare, and look into the mouth. Teeth, teeth, teeth, and at the front one tooth sticking out sideways, red round the root. It wobbles every time he breathes. OLD GULPER sees you see it, and goes quiet.

[talk: Hum]
You hum, low and slow, like the bell does when it's nearly stopped. OLD GULPER's gills slow down. The ringing in the tower fades away to nothing. For a moment he looks very sleepy.

[talk: Pat the bell]
You give the great bell a pat on its green bronze side, the way you'd pat a horse. It gives back the smallest note. OLD GULPER watches you do it with one eye, and lets it go.

[talk: Wiggle the tooth]
You take hold of the crooked tooth with both hands and wiggle it. OLD GULPER goes rigid. Then a noise comes out of him like a kettle going off, and the tooth swings loose, hanging by a thread. His whole long body sags with relief.

# Said when you try to RUN.
[no_escape]
The stairs are under water, and OLD GULPER is between you and the door. He is also, in a way, the floor.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
OLD GULPER sinks back into his bell, slowly, until only the end of his tail is hanging over the rim.

[pacified]
OLD GULPER lowers his head and rests his chin on the rim of the bell. He opens his mouth wide, and waits. You reach in and take hold of the crooked tooth.
