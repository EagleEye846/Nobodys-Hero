SLAGLING - HP 1050
Leftover from the furnaces. Crusty outside, molten in the middle, and lonely, by the look of it. Spits sparks. Slow, but it sticks.

[talk: Warm your hands]
You hold your hands out to SLAGLING, the way you would to a fire. It goes very still. Then it goes brighter, and brighter, until you have to lean back a bit. It's showing off.

[talk: Sit by the vent with it]
You sit down on the old vent next to SLAGLING. It sits, in its way. Neither of you says anything. It ticks, very quietly, like a clock being careful.

[talk: Pat its crust]
You pat SLAGLING with your sleeve. It's hot. You pat it anyway. It tries to purr and spits a small spark instead, and looks mortified.
