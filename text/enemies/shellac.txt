# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/shellac.js.
[talk: Inspect]
SHELLAC - HP 1950
A hermit crab in a teapot, and extremely house-proud. Snaps its claw in fans, tosses cups that bounce, spins the whole pot in rings of china, and lets off steam. Stay off the flat of the floor.

[talk: Wipe your feet]
You scrape one boot on the sand, slowly, and then the other one, for a good long while. SHELLAC watches every scrape. Its claw drops a little. Nobody has wiped their feet for it in years.

[talk: Admire the pattern]
You lean in to look at the blue willow pattern, the little bridge, the two birds. SHELLAC turns so you can see the other side. It had forgotten there were birds on it.

[talk: Mime a cup of tea]
You mime a teacup, very politely, little finger out, and look hopefully at the spout. SHELLAC is stunned. It tips the lid. There isn't. There hasn't been for years. It looks at you as if you're the first person to ask.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
The lid pops off with a ping and SHELLAC rolls out of its teapot onto its back, claws up, deeply offended. The pot rocks to a stop. You are not sure which of them is more cracked.

[pacified]
SHELLAC settles its lid with great dignity and tips a claw to you, the way a person nods at a gate. It lets you by, and watches you all the way down the beach to make sure you wipe your feet.
