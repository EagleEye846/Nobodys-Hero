[intro]
A hay bale at the roadside rolls itself upright, straightens its hat, and beams. A HAYBOB!

# Shown at the start of each of your turns, in order, then repeating.
[flavor]
HAYBOB rocks gently on its base. It has the air of a bale who has Seen Things, mostly fields.

HAYBOB tips its hat at you. The hat tips back, a little late.

Something small squeaks inside HAYBOB. HAYBOB says nothing. Neither of them has been consulted.

HAYBOB sheds a bit of straw and kicks it under itself, hoping you didn't see.

Smells like warm hay and a very good afternoon.

# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/haybob.js.
[talk: Inspect]
HAYBOB - HP 800
A hay bale that got loose and likes it. Rolls a lot. Has a pitchfork it didn't ask for, and a mouse it doesn't mention. Easy, if you keep moving.

[talk: Compliment the hat]
You tell HAYBOB it's a very fine hat. HAYBOB goes pink in the places where hay can. It adjusts the brim. It was, in fact, already perfect.

[talk: Ask about the mouse]
You ask HAYBOB about the mouse. HAYBOB goes very still. The mouse goes very still. Then, from somewhere in the bale, a very small voice says "Hello." HAYBOB looks astonished.

[talk: Roll along with it]
You lie down and roll a little way beside HAYBOB, down the lane. It's much more fun than it looks. HAYBOB is so pleased it nearly comes undone.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
HAYBOB comes unwound in a long, golden spill, with the hat on top. A tiny mouse crawls out, looks around, and says "Finally." It goes home to the field.

[pacified]
HAYBOB tips its hat to you, and the mouse tips its ear. It rolls aside, gently, to let you past. It leaves a trail of straw, in case you want to follow.
