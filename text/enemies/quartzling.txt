# Shown at the start of each of your turns, in order, then repeating.
[flavor]
QUARTZLING stands stock still. It is being a crystal. It is being it very hard.

A glint runs down QUARTZLING's side. It didn't do that on purpose. Probably.

QUARTZLING's eyes drift to the left, to see if you've bought it. You have not.

All the other crystals in the room are perfectly still. QUARTZLING is the one not blinking the hardest.

Smells like a cold cellar and a very still afternoon.

# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/quartzling.js.
[talk: Inspect]
QUARTZLING - HP 950
A small crystal creature that stands among crystals and pretends to be one. Any of the crystals round you might be one too. Shards split. Some of them glint.

[talk: Say what a fine crystal it is]
You tell QUARTZLING it's the finest crystal in the whole cave. It says nothing, in a way that means a great deal. A slow blush spreads through its facets. It is still being a crystal. It's just a pink one.

[talk: Stand still beside it]
You stand next to QUARTZLING and don't move. It doesn't move. Neither of you moves for a very long time. You are, briefly, the best of friends.

[talk: Pretend not to notice]
You walk past QUARTZLING, remarking loudly on what a lovely cluster this is. Just crystals. QUARTZLING's whole body trembles with the effort of not saying thank you.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
QUARTZLING cracks and comes apart in a ringing of little bells. When the sound dies, the shards on the floor are, very clearly, just crystals.

[pacified]
QUARTZLING breathes out. All its facets go pink. It steps aside, still stiff as a post, and waves, once, very small, so no other crystal will see.
