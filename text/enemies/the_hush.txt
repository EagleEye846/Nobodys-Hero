# Ves's kind word (TALK) once she has her umbrella back: her working out what
# it is. {foe} is its name.
[vesper_talk]
Ves lowers her umbrella, so the HUSH can see her face. It stops dead, like nobody's ever done that.

Ves asks the HUSH how long it's been on its own out here. It lies down on the stones, as if that's an answer.

Ves says it didn't take her umbrella to keep it. It took it because she let it go. It was the first thing anyone ever left it.

Ves tells the HUSH it can come closer. It does, one hoof at a time, and stops when she flinches, and waits. The HUSH's ears come round to her.

Ves says she's sorry about its hundred years. She means it. The HUSH makes a small noise, like a dog in its sleep.
