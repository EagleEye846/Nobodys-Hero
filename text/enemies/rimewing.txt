[talk: Inspect]
RIMEWING - HP 260
A lantern moth, grown huge in the cold. It can't keep away from a light. It has been trying.

[talk: Shade the lamp]
You cup your hands round your light. RIMEWING slows, blinks, and drifts a little lower.

[talk: Hum]
You hum something soft. RIMEWING's wings slow to the tune, the way they would by a warm window.

[talk: Mention the Keeper]
You mention the old Keeper and his lantern. RIMEWING goes very still.
For a moment, it doesn't look at any light at all.
