[intro]
Something up in the dark lets go of the rock with a wet little click. Two pale green eyes open, upside down, and the dark flaps at you. A BARNACLE BAT!

# Shown at the start of each of your turns, in order, then repeating.
[flavor]
BARNACLE BAT hangs from its knob of rock, wrapped up tight in its wings, and watches you upside down.

A drop of sea water gathers on the end of BARNACLE BAT's ear, and falls, and lands on your boot. It looks pleased about that.

BARNACLE BAT squeaks. A moment later the squeak comes back from every wall in the cave, a little smaller each time.

BARNACLE BAT tries to fold its wings neatly. The barnacles get in the way. It's been trying for years.

Smells like wet rock, old rope, and the inside of a shell.

# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/barnacle_bat.js.
[talk: Inspect]
BARNACLE BAT - HP 1850
A cave bat crusted with barnacles. Its echo marks you and it swoops through the mark; its cousins drop when their eyes open; its barnacles comb at you on every squeak. Move once the echo finds you.

[talk: Hang your head upside down]
You bend right over and look at BARNACLE BAT between your knees, so the cave is the wrong way up and it's the right way up. It goes very still. Then it squeaks, softly, the friendly sort of squeak. Nobody has ever met it halfway before.

[talk: Keep very quiet]
You stand quite still in the dark and don't make a sound. BARNACLE BAT squeaks at you to find out where you went, and the echo comes back around you, and it seems to decide you're a sort of rock. A nice rock. It settles.

[talk: Pick off a barnacle]
You reach up and work loose the one barnacle on the middle of its back, the one it can never reach. BARNACLE BAT shivers all over. Then it stretches its wings out, both of them, all the way, for what seems to be the first time in years.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
BARNACLE BAT flutters down onto the wet floor in a little heap of wing and barnacle. It gives one small squeak, and the echo of it goes round the cave and doesn't come back.

[pacified]
BARNACLE BAT flaps up into the dark, finds its knob of rock first time, and folds itself up. Two pale green eyes watch you go, upside down, and then shut.
