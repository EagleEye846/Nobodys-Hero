# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the

[talk: Inspect]
STONE GOLEM - HP 300
A heap of cave stone that got up. Slow, heavy, and in no hurry. Its ice took centuries to grow.

[talk: Stand very still]
You stand as still as a stone. STONE GOLEM looks at you for a long, long time, and slowly nods. It respects that.

[talk: Admire crystals]
You admire the crystals on STONE GOLEM's shoulders. It turns, ever so slowly, so you can see the best one.

[talk: Knock politely]
You knock on STONE GOLEM, twice. After a very long pause, something deep inside it knocks back.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
STONE GOLEM crumbles back into a heap of rubble. One crystal is still glowing on top.

[pacified]
STONE GOLEM curls back up into a boulder with a sigh like a rockfall, and goes back to being a rock. It seems relieved.
