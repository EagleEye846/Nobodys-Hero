[talk: Inspect]
GLOAMWORM - HP 440
A glowworm of the Underroot. It fishes with lights. Lonely, and a bit sticky.

[talk: Hold up your candle]
You hold up your candle. GLOAMWORM glows back, very bright. It's so happy to meet another glowworm.

[talk: Tell it about the sky]
You try to tell it about the sky. A roof so high you can't see it, with lights all over. GLOAMWORM thinks that sounds lovely. It doesn't believe you.

[talk: Stay very still]
You stand very still. GLOAMWORM creeps down its thread for a closer look. It's shy, close up.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
GLOAMWORM's light goes dim. It winds itself up its thread into the roots and goes very quiet.

[pacified]
GLOAMWORM leaves one little light hanging by the path, so you won't get lost. Then it goes back up into the dark.
