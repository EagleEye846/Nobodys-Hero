# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/dunlin_dancer.js.
[talk: Inspect]
DUNLIN DANCER - HP 1900
A flock of sandpipers that acts as one bird. Runs at you in rows, pecks down in diagonals, and drops shells from the sky. There is always a gap in the row. Find it early.

[talk: Match their step]
You hop when they hop, and turn when they turn. You are a beat behind, then half a beat, then a beat ahead. All five of them look at you. Then they do something new, and look to see whether you'll keep up.

[talk: Stand very still]
You stand so still that a bird lands on your boot. The rest of the flock does not know what to make of it. It watches, with five heads, for a long time, and slowly stops running.

[talk: Scatter some crumbs]
You break up a biscuit and throw the crumbs in a long line. The flock runs the line as one, picking them up in time. It has never, in its life, been fed in a row.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
The five of them go up at once, in a whirl of wings. They come down a long way off. One feather lands by your boot, and stays there.

[pacified]
The flock bows to you in a wave, head after head, and then runs off along the tide in a long, neat line. It leaves a trail of small footprints that look like writing.
