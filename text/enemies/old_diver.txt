[intro]
A heavy clank, then another. A brass helmet turns toward you on a rusted collar, and the porthole is dark. Somebody is still working in there. Nobody is. An OLD DIVER!

# Shown at the start of each of your turns, in order, then repeating.
[flavor]
OLD DIVER stands very straight. Water runs out of its cuffs. It has been walking for a very long time and is, plainly, not done.

The air hose lifts, sways, and lies down again, like a sleeping snake that's heard its name.

Something faint and green glimmers in the porthole, and goes out. OLD DIVER taps its own helmet, as if checking a gauge.

OLD DIVER pats its breastplate for a pocket. It has no pockets. It pats again, to be sure.

Smells like wet brass, canvas, and a lot of years.

# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/old_diver.js.
[talk: Inspect]
OLD DIVER - HP 2300
An empty diving suit that still thinks it has a job. Throws a harpoon on a line and reels it back, hunts with the loose air hose, lets its last air up in bubbles, and drops an anchor on you. Watch the line and the floor.

[talk: Knock on the helmet]
You knock on the helmet, twice, politely. A long, low note rings out of it, and rolls round the inside like a bell in a well. OLD DIVER holds quite still while it fades, as if listening for somebody to knock back.

[talk: Salute]
You salute. OLD DIVER stops. Slowly, with a groan of every joint, it salutes back. For a moment the porthole is full of a steady, kind green light, and you could swear someone was in there, saluting.

[talk: Bow your head]
You bow your head and hold a hand to your chest. OLD DIVER's own head tips. It has seen that done before, for other divers, on a day that wasn't a good one.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
OLD DIVER's helmet comes off with a clang and rolls across the planks. The suit folds up on itself, empty, with a long sigh of old air. It was never anybody's, any more.

[pacified]
OLD DIVER sets its harpoon down against a wall, very carefully, the way you'd put down something you won't need. It nods once, and walks off into the rain, boots ringing, towards a job that isn't there.
