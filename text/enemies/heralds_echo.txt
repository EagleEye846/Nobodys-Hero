# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the

[talk: Inspect]
HERALD'S ECHO - HP 680
The ghost of a town crier, still crying the Hero's coming. Its words hurt. Literally.

[talk: Listen to the news]
You stand and listen to the whole proclamation, all the way through. HERALD'S ECHO looks surprised. Nobody has listened to the end in a very long time.

[talk: Applaud]
You clap. HERALD'S ECHO bows, very low, and nearly loses its hat. The street claps back, faintly. That's just the echo.

[talk: Ring its bell for it]
HERALD'S ECHO lets you ring its bell. The sound goes all the way down the empty street. It seems to like hearing it from out here.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
HERALD'S ECHO fades away mid-shout. A moment later, from far off down the street, you hear it start again: "Hear ye..."

[pacified]
HERALD'S ECHO rolls up its scroll and tips its hat to you. It drifts off down the street, ringing its bell, a little softer than before.
