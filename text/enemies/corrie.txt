# CORRIE, THE TIDECALLER: chapter 5's last boss, at the top of the Last Light.
# A boy of about eight in an oilskin far too big for him. He went out to stop
# the sea with a spell, and the spell became the storm. He's still trying to
# get it right ("WHEN I GET IT RIGHT HE WILL COME BACK", on the gallery wall),
# and the bigger it got, the more he hoped somebody would see it and come.
# Across the four phases: a proud, lonely boy showing off his spells; the
# storm taking him over; dragged round in the whirl; then a tired child.
# He speaks like the child he is, in short bits. The hero never speaks.
# Ves and Bramble only say what they can see; they're frightened, and they
# shout. (draft: every line here, rewritten 2026-11-15; the old ones are in
# notes/text-backup-2026-11-15/corrie/)
# Numbers (HP, damage, his four phases) live in src/data/enemies/corrie.js.
# He can't be spared: the storm answers every kindness ([no_mercy]).
#

# Shown at the start of each of your turns, in order, then repeating.
[flavor]
CORRIE floats over nothing. His coat is far too big for him. The sleeves hang right past his hands.

The glow in the fork of his staff flickers like a candle in a draught.

Drops of sea go round and round him in a ring, and never fall.

He holds the staff the way you'd hold something you were told not to touch.

[flavor: storm]
The storm pours out of him. Somewhere in the middle of it, a small boy is shouting.

The tide is coming up. Every turn, a little higher.

Rain lashes the lamp room's glass. The lightning finds the water first.

TIP: Haddock's bell, rung by you and Bramble together, sends the water back down.

[flavor: whirlpool]
The water under him is turning. Planks and oars go round in it, and the bits of old boats.

A ship's figurehead goes by, with salt in her hair. Round and round.

Below the waist, CORRIE isn't there any more. Only the whirl.

The pull's in your bones now. Don't let it take you to the middle.

[flavor: lamp]
The great lamp turns, slow. Each time the beam crosses him, he flinches.

He's only a boy now, mostly. A tired one. The staff is too heavy for him.

The water on his face might be the sea. It might not.

Hide behind the pillars when the beam comes round.

# One section per TALK option (the names must match corrie.js).
[talk: Check]
CORRIE - HP 36000
A boy in a coat far too big for him, and the storm all round him. Darts, boats, a spell circle, a song. Words don't get through the storm to him.
His bullets come too thick to feed on: grazing them gives little SP. Spend it well.

[talk: Ring Haddock's bell]
You and Bramble take Haddock's bell by its cord together and ring it, hard. Bramble yells over it: "HADDOCK! We're UP here!"
The note goes out over the water. CORRIE looks round at it, and for a moment the storm holds its breath.
The sea pulls back. The next attack will be gentler.

[talk: Wave to him]
You wave to him across the water, both arms, the way you'd wave a boat in.

[talk: Hold out a hand]
You hold out a hand.

[no_mercy]
You try to reach him. The wind gets there first, tears it up, and throws it into the sea.

If he heard any of it, the storm didn't let him answer.

[no_mercy_again]
The rain on the glass is too loud. Nothing gets through to him.

He looks your way for a moment. Then the wind turns him round again.

[no_mercy_again: storm]

A wave comes over, and he's gone behind it.

[no_mercy_again: whirlpool]

[no_mercy_again: lamp]

# Ves's and Bramble's turns, in each part of the fight (instead of their usual
# lines). What they shout is only what they can see.
[vesper_turn: boy]
Ves stares at the boy. "Corrie? ...It can't be." Her turn.

Ves watches the paper boats go by. "He made those. Same as downstairs." Her turn.

Ves grips her umbrella. "That's a CHILD. Floating." Her turn.

Ves wipes the spray off her face. "Fine. Stop him first. Questions after." Her turn.

[vesper_turn: storm]
Ves shouts over the thunder. "Keep OUT of the water!" Her turn.

Ves looks down. "No, no, no. It's still coming up." Her turn.

Ves can't hear herself. "Did he DO that? On purpose?" Her turn.

Ves wipes the rain out of her eyes. "I can't SEE!" Her turn.

[vesper_turn: whirlpool]
Ves digs her umbrella into the boards. "Hold ON!" Her turn.

Ves, white in the face: "Don't let it pull you in. Don't you DARE." Her turn.

Ves watches the boy go round with it. "He can't get out either." Her turn.

[vesper_turn: lamp]
Ves ducks behind a pillar as the beam goes by. "Down! DOWN!" Her turn.

Ves looks at the boy, then away. "He's crying." Her turn.

Ves keeps her voice low. "Nearly done. Nearly." Her turn.

[bramble_turn: boy]
Bramble waves his hatchet at the boy. "Are you Corrie? HADDOCK'S Corrie?" His turn.

Bramble, to the boy: "Oi! Put the stick DOWN! You'll have somebody's eye out!" His turn.

Bramble eyes the water on the floor and steps round it. His turn.

Bramble, to the boy: "Your coat's too big! You'll TRIP!" His turn.

[bramble_turn: storm]
Bramble stands on tiptoe, out of the water. "I'm fine. I'm FINE." His turn.

Bramble shouts down the stair. "HADDOCK! It's raining INSIDE!" His turn.

Bramble yells at the storm. "Leave him ALONE!" His turn.

Bramble's teeth are chattering. He grips his hatchet anyway. His turn.

[bramble_turn: whirlpool]
Bramble flattens himself on the boards. "I'm not scared. My LEGS are scared." His turn.

Bramble, to the boy: "Corrie! Grab hold of something! ANYTHING!" His turn.

Bramble yells at the water. "Let him GO, you stupid sea!" His turn.

[bramble_turn: lamp]
Bramble, to the boy: "We came up all the stairs, Corrie! Somebody CAME!" His turn.

Bramble wipes his face. It's only rain. Probably. His turn.

Bramble crouches behind a pillar. "Beam! BEAM!" His turn.
