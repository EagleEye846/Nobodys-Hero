[intro]
A sou'wester rises out of the surf, and under it a long grey coat, and under that nothing but water, running. Two pale lights turn toward you. A SALT SHADE!

# Shown at the start of each of your turns, in order, then repeating.
[flavor]
SALT SHADE stands with its face to the sea. It has been waiting a long time to see a sail.

Water runs down it, and out of it, and does not make a puddle.

SALT SHADE coils its rope over an arm, a turn at a time, the way you would if you'd done it ten thousand times.

It looks at you. It is not sure what you are, and is far too polite to ask.

Smells of cold brine, tar and a very long wait.

# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/salt_shade.js.
[talk: Inspect]
SALT SHADE - HP 2200
The ghost of a drowned sailor, still waiting for a ship. Signals in dots and dashes, his brine puddles dry into salt spikes, and he heaves the lead and hauls it home.

[talk: Tap your wrist]
You tap your wrist and point at his post: how long? SALT SHADE counts on its fingers, and loses its count, and starts again. He asks, politely, whether the war is still on. You do not have the heart.

[talk: Take the rope]
You step in and take the end of the rope from his hand. For a moment you are both pulling the same line, gently, and the water in him goes still. He nods, as if a watch had been handed over.

[talk: Point at the tide]
You point out to sea, where the water has begun, very slowly, to slide back off the posts. SALT SHADE lifts its head. All its life, and a good while after it, he has been waiting for someone to show him the tide turning, and the lights in his eyes go from pale to warm.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
SALT SHADE comes apart into bubbles, a coat's worth of them, each with a small lit window in it. They rise. The sou'wester drifts down and settles on the water, and floats away on a tide that wasn't running a moment ago.

[pacified]
SALT SHADE takes off its sou'wester and holds it to its chest. The water in it goes quiet. It bows low to you, to the sea, to a ship that isn't there, and fades, hat first, into the sound of the surf. The way is clear.
