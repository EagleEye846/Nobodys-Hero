SAPLING - HP 380
A young tree spirit that only moves when nobody's looking. Bad at staying put.

[talk: Water it]
You pour a little water at its roots. SAPLING's leaves go glossy. It grows about a hair. It's delighted.

[talk: Look away]
You look away. When you look back, SAPLING is right next to you, very pleased, pretending it's always been there.

[talk: Stand like a tree]
You stand very still with your arms out like branches. SAPLING looks you up and down. You pass.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
SAPLING drops its leaves all at once, and the next time you look it's a stump. Then it isn't anywhere.

[pacified]
SAPLING shuffles off between the big trees, but only while you're looking the other way.
