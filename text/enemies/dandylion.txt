# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the

[talk: Inspect]
DANDY LION - HP 800
A lion whose mane is a dandelion clock. Vain about it. Blows apart when it roars, puffs back together when it sulks. Easy, if you stay out of the fluff.

[talk: Admire the mane]
You tell DANDY LION its mane is the roundest you have ever seen. It tries to look modest. It looks, instead, like a very proud cloud.

[talk: Make a wish]
You close your eyes and hold your breath. DANDY LION goes rigid with delight. You wish it would stop fighting. It sneezes a little, and a seed floats away, and you feel the wish go.

[talk: Hold still in the breeze]
You stand perfectly still while the seeds blow past. DANDY LION is moved. Nobody has ever just stood and let its mane be a landscape.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
DANDY LION blows apart in one last, enormous puff. When the seeds settle, there is a small, bald, very embarrassed lion in the middle of them. It runs.

[pacified]
DANDY LION fluffs itself up, big and bright, and bows. Then it trots off, tail first, so you can see the flower on the end. It lets you by.
