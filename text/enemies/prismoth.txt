# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/prismoth.js.
[talk: Inspect]
PRISMOTH - HP 950
A fuzzy moth whose wings are panes of coloured glass. It bends every bit of light into beams and rainbows, and can't keep away from a lamp. Only the glass that matches the light is really there.

[talk: Hold up a glass]
You hold up a bit of crystal, so the light goes through it, and PRISMOTH sees itself in the rainbow on the floor. It stares for a long time. It's the most beautiful thing it has ever seen.

[talk: Admire the colours]
You name every colour on its wings, out loud, in order. PRISMOTH corrects you on the third one, kindly. It says that's indigo, and that most people miss it.

[talk: Turn down the light]
You shade your eyes, and ask politely if it might dim a little. PRISMOTH is mortified. It folds its wings in at once, glowing a faint, apologetic pink, and perches on your shoulder, ever so softly, to say sorry.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
PRISMOTH's wings go grey, one colour at a time, until they're just glass. It folds them up and sinks down onto the stone. There's still a very small rainbow in the crystal beside it.

[pacified]
PRISMOTH spreads its wings so the light goes through them, and the whole cave turns every colour at once, slowly, for you. Then it flutters up and off, leaving a faint trail of glitter.
