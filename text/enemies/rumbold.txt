[talk: Hold out a hand to the rafters]

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
His strings snap, and KING PEPTUP comes apart across the boards, a joint at a time. His crown rolls across the stage and spins to a stop. Up in the rafters, TUCK is untouched, both paws still on a bar that isn't holding anything.

[pacified]
TUCK lets go of the bar. The strings go slack, and KING PEPTUP folds up on the boards and stays there. The show is over. Nobody claps. Then somebody does.
