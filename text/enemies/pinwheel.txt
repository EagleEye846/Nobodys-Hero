[intro]
Something round and violet rolls up the shingle, stops, and puts out every spine at once. A PINWHEEL!

# Shown at the start of each of your turns, in order, then repeating.
[flavor]
PINWHEEL turns its spines a notch, like a dial. Click. It wants you to know it can do that.

PINWHEEL is scowling at a pebble. The pebble has not done anything. PINWHEEL is making sure it never does.

A drop of gold roe beads on the tip of one spine. PINWHEEL sees you looking and rolls it round to the back.

PINWHEEL tries to look big. It's already big. It tries to look bigger. A spine falls off.

Smells like wet rock and something sweet and expensive.

# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/pinwheel.js.
[talk: Inspect]
PINWHEEL - HP 2050
A sea urchin with a temper. Throws fans of spines, rolls across the box shedding more, rains them down and lays little mines that burst. Mind the roll and the sky.

[talk: Offer it a smooth stone]
You set a smooth, flat stone in front of it. PINWHEEL pokes it with one spine. Then another. Then it leans on it, spines drooping, like a creature that has never once sat on something soft.

[talk: Tell it it is very round]
You tell PINWHEEL it is the roundest thing on the whole shore. It stops spinning. It turns slowly to look at its own side. It goes a very deep, proud pink.

[talk: Sit down nearby]
You sit down on the shingle, a good way off, and look at the water. PINWHEEL watches you for a long time. Then it rolls a little closer, and then a little closer, and then it sits too.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
PINWHEEL's spines drop off, one by one, ticking on the shingle like rain. It sits in the middle of them, small and plain and violet, and rolls slowly off.

[pacified]
PINWHEEL lets its spines go slack and soft. It rolls once, all the way round, so you can see there's nothing in it but a very small, very pleased urchin. Then it rolls off down the beach.
