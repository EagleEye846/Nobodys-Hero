# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/magpiemole.js.
[talk: Inspect]
MAGPIE MOLE - HP 1050
A mole with a sack of everything shiny. Throws it, swings it, and pops up through the floor wherever you stand. Don't stay put; it's quick, and so is the sack.

[talk: Describe a lost thing]
You describe something you've lost: a brass button, a bit bent. MAGPIE MOLE rummages for a long, long time, and produces a button that is nothing like it. It insists it's the same one. You say thank you.

[talk: Hand over a button]
You hold out a spare button. MAGPIE MOLE trembles. Nobody has ever just given it one. It holds it up to the light, and then it puts it in a special pocket, at the very back of the sack.

[talk: Fill in a form]
You ask for a form. MAGPIE MOLE has one. It's mostly dirt, but it has a box on it for NAME, and a box for ITEM, and a box for HOW YOU FEEL ABOUT IT. It's the most official thing it has ever done.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
MAGPIE MOLE slumps over its sack, with its pink hands over the top, to keep everything safe. Something inside goes "tink", once, quietly, and doesn't go again.

[pacified]
MAGPIE MOLE stands, and gives a small, solemn bow. It pulls something shiny out of the sack, a ring-pull from a very old tin, and presses it into your hand. "Found it," it says. "Keep it. It was lost."
