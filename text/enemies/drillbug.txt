# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the

[talk: Inspect]
DRILLBUG - HP 1050
A beetle with a drill for a nose and a lamp on its hat. Proud of every tunnel. Digs where you stand, so don't stand still. Rocks come down when it gets excited.

[talk: Praise a tunnel]
You point to a tunnel, any tunnel, and tell DRILLBUG it's the straightest you've ever seen. It's not straight at all. DRILLBUG's antennae stand bolt upright. It scratches the date into the wall beside it, so the tunnel will remember the day.

[talk: Ask for directions]
You ask DRILLBUG the way to the next cave. It lights up. It draws a map in the dirt, with a great deal of detail. All of it goes through one wall. It's very sure about the wall.

[talk: Admire the drill]
You ask to see the drill up close. DRILLBUG presents it, reverently, like a sword. It is very shiny. It has a tiny engraving on it that says WORLD'S BEST DRILLBUG. It engraved it itself.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
DRILLBUG rolls onto its back with all six legs in the air. The drill stutters, spins down, and stops. Its helmet lamp stays on a little longer, then dims.

[pacified]
DRILLBUG takes off its helmet and holds it to its chest. It points you down the tunnel it's actually sure about, this time. It's the right one.
