[intro]
The big white rock beside you unfolds, and unfolds, and keeps on unfolding. A BLIND GROTTO CRAB! It has no eyes at all. Its feelers have already found you.

# Shown at the start of each of your turns, in order, then repeating.
[flavor]
BLIND GROTTO CRAB's feelers fan out and wave, reading the air. They are reading you. They seem to find you interesting, and slightly damp.

BLIND GROTTO CRAB taps the floor with one claw. Tap. Tap. It listens for the answer. The cave answers, a bit late.

A feeler touches your sleeve, lets go, and touches it again, as though checking it hadn't changed.

BLIND GROTTO CRAB turns its smooth, eyeless face to the left of you, and then, politely, to the right of you.

Smells like a wet cave and a very clean shell.

[flavor: spareable]
BLIND GROTTO CRAB has folded its claws. Its feelers rest lightly on your boot, the way you'd rest a hand on a door to feel if the room beyond was warm.

# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/blind_crab.js.
[talk: Inspect]
BLIND GROTTO CRAB - HP 8400
A huge white cave crab with no eyes. Its feelers follow the path you walked a moment ago, so keep moving and don't double back over yourself. It spits pearls, and it knocks the roof down: watch for the shadows.

[talk: Stand perfectly still]
You stand perfectly still. A feeler comes in, touches your sleeve, your hand, the very end of your nose, and goes away satisfied. It was only asking who you were. BLIND GROTTO CRAB does a small, pleased click.

[talk: Tap a slow rhythm]
You tap a slow rhythm on the rock. The feelers all turn to it, one by one, like flowers to a window. BLIND GROTTO CRAB taps it back, a little off. You fix it. It copies you. Neither of you hurries.

[talk: Offer your hand, palm up]
You hold out your hand, palm up, and wait. A single feeler lowers itself onto it. Then a pearl, small and cold, is set in your palm, and taken back, and set there again. It is the nicest thing anyone has done in this cave.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
BLIND GROTTO CRAB folds in on itself with a long, hollow click. Its feelers lie down, one by one. The cave is much quieter without something listening to it.

[pacified]
BLIND GROTTO CRAB gathers its feelers in, taps the floor twice, and sidles off into the dark with a whole lot of dignity. It leaves a pearl behind, in case you came back.
