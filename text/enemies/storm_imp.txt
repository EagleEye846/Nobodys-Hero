# One section per TALK option. The names after "talk:" must match the
# TALK options in src/data/enemies/storm_imp.js.
[talk: Inspect]
STORM IMP - HP 2200
A bit of the storm with a mind of its own and a very long attention span for one joke. Forks of lightning (each flickers first), rain, gusts that shove, and balls of lightning that bounce. Mind the flicker.

[talk: Play along]
You jump over a puddle it points at, and then another, and then you pretend to be hit by a very small bolt. STORM IMP howls with laughter and rolls over in the air. It has never had anybody play before. Everyone just ran.

[talk: Hold up an umbrella]
You hold your coat over your head and walk, very slowly, through its rain. STORM IMP trails after you, a little way above, drizzling politely so that it all falls exactly where you are not.

[talk: Tell it a joke]
You tell it a joke about a lighthouse. It doesn't understand a word, but it understands you're trying, and it laughs so hard that thunder comes out of its ears. It tells you to do it again. You do not.

# How the fight ends. The rewards line is added automatically.
[victory]
STORM IMP lets out a long, thin wail, and rains itself out, all at once, on the planks. A small, warm puff of static is left floating where it was. It doesn't look like a threat. It looks like a toy.

[pacified]
STORM IMP tumbles over in the air, giggling, and throws up a tiny, harmless flash for you, like fireworks. Then it zips away over the sea with its tail flickering, to see what else it can spoil. Only a little.
