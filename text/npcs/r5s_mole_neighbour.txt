# R5S_MOLE_NEIGHBOUR: Burdock's neighbour on Dandelion Hill, up out of his own molehill two along from
# Burdock's, wearing Burdock's lost spectacles and delighted with them. The first talk he shows them off;
# then he hands them over in the same talk (take them to Burdock). (draft)

[first]
Hello! Have a look at these. Found them in the grass. Cool glasses. Everything's enormous. You're enormous. That daisy is the size of a cart.

[repeat]
I can see every single bit of the hill. I didn't know the hill had bits.

[show]
He pushes the spectacles up his nose with one claw, very slowly, so you can see how well they fit. They don't.

[show_ves]
{bored}...Those are round.

[show_bramble]
{surprised}The mole right next to him lost round spectacles!

[give]
He pushes the spectacles up his nose with one claw, very slowly, so you can see how well they fit. They don't.

"His? Burdock's?" He looks two holes along, where Burdock is squinting hard at a stone. "...Oh. He did say."

He takes the spectacles off. The hill goes small and soft again, and he blinks at it with some relief. "Here. Everything was a bit much, to be honest."

[give_ves]
{smile}Nicely done. Nobody even had to fight anybody.

[give_bramble]
{glee}Go on, give them back to him! Quick, before he tries them on again!

[after]
He's sitting by his hill with his eyes shut. "Resting them," he says. "They saw a lot."
