# DUNSTAN: the bell-ringer, up on the bell platform. Ear muffs, a rope round
# one arm, and a hundred years of one ring a day.

[first]
Oh! A visitor. Up here! Mind the rope. Mind the... yes, that's the bell. Please don't.

I'm Dunstan. I ring it. Once, at dawn, so the Hero knows which way is up. That's the whole job. It's a good job.

Have I ever missed one? No. Not one dawn in a hundred years, counting my father's and his father's. Same rope, nearly. Same bell.

You can ring it if you like. Just once. It's daytime, so it's allowed. ...Go on. I can see you want to.

[repeat]
Once, at dawn. Never after dark. You've heard about the goat.

Some mornings, when the wind is right, the mountain rings back. Low, from inside. The Gallery, they say. I say it's keeping me company.

When the Hero comes, I'm to ring it three times. I've never rung it three times. I've practised in my head. One, two, three. It'll be loud.
