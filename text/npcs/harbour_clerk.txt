# THE HARBOUR CLERK (at his desk in the harbour office, saltcombe_harbour_office): a puffin in a
# green eyeshade and round spectacles, ink on his cuffs. Fussy and fond of his books. Trail 4 is the
# ledger on his desk; he says nothing about that line, except that the book falls open there.

[first]
{stern}Name? Vessel? Purpose of visit?

{flustered}Oh. On foot. Off the Wrack. On FOOT. Well, there's no column for that. I'll have to rule one.

The ledger? You may look. Don't lick your fingers. It falls open at the same page, I'm afraid. Always has. Some books are like that.

[repeat]
Everything that leaves this harbour goes in the book, and everything that comes back. Mostly they come back.

{stern}Mostly.
