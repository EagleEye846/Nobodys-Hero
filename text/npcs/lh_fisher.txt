# THE EEL-FISHER: a woman in waders with a basket, who goes out into the
# Lantern Marsh every morning and comes back every evening, and knows its
# paths better than anyone living.

[first]
You came across the fen? On the boards? In the mist?

Huh. Most people don't. Most people follow the wrong lights, out there.

[repeat]
Eels. Fresh this morning. Well. This afternoon. They don't mind.

[first after ves_on_her_feet]
I go out on the fen every day. I know every board of it.

Your friend came across it alone, hurt, in the dark? Then she's tougher than I am. Tell her I said so.

[repeat after ves_on_her_feet]
Stay on the boards. Follow the ones on poles. Don't follow anything that moves.
