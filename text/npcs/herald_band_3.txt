# Third trumpet: Nib, a kid with a wooden trumpet, who is only in the band
# because somebody has to be.

[first]
I'm in the band. I'm the third trumpet. There's only three of us so I'm the last trumpet.

Mine's wooden. It goes "fweep". Ada says it adds texture.

Are you the Hero? If you are, can I be in the story? Even just a bit? "And a small herald went fweep." That'd be enough.

[repeat]
Fweep.

Ada says when the Hero comes I'll get a brass one. A kid in the square says it isn't coming. I say fweep.
