# A SNOW HARE in the square, by the stores. White as the drifts, one ear
# down. It doesn't talk, exactly.

[first]
The snow hare looks at you. One ear goes up. The other stays down.

It sniffs your boots very thoroughly, decides something, and goes back to eating a frozen cabbage leaf.

[repeat]
The snow hare is eating the cabbage leaf. It's going to take all day. It has all day.

Its nose twitches at you in what is probably a greeting.
