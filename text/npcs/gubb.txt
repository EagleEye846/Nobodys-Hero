# GUBB: the old cave frog who ferries folk across the Stillwater. Slow, kind,
# fat, sings the same verse over and over. You can knock on the jetty post
# to hear him from the water without taking a ride (the sign says "please
# knock"); the ride itself is the scene gubb_crossing.

[first]
(You knock on the post. Out on the black water, the humming stops.)

Hm? Someone knocking? That's nice. Nobody knocks.

Whenever you're ready, dear, stand out at the end of the boards. I'll hear you. I hear everything on the lake. It's the only thing that carries.

[repeat]
Take your time. The lake isn't going anywhere. I've checked.

If you've a song, I'll take it. I'm stuck on a verse.

[first after gubb_crossing]
(You knock on the post. The humming stops.)

Back already? Or still? Time's slippery out here. It all goes round like the punt.

Stand at the end when you want a ride, either shore. Same fare as before. A sweet, if you've one, but I've plenty.

[repeat after gubb_crossing]
Mind the edges, dear. Things live in the edges.

Hm-hmm... hm-hm-hmmm... Where was I?

[first after the_light_goes_up]
(You knock on the post. The humming stops, then starts again, a little higher.)

Did you feel that, dear? The whole lake went warm from underneath, like a bath someone's just run.

Stand at the end of the boards when you want a ride. Either shore. Same fare.

[repeat after the_light_goes_up]
Warm water and a bit of light. I've found the next verse. Don't ask me what it is. It's about a bath.
