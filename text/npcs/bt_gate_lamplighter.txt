# BT_GATE_LAMPLIGHTER: A glow-worm lamplighter with a hooked pole, who lights the gate's lamps every dusk and has noticed they take more lighting lately.

[first]
Evening! I mean, afternoon. I mean, who can tell, with the Sunstone like it is.

I light these lamps by hand. They used to stay lit all night. Lately I'm going round twice.

[repeat]
My tail's the lamp, my pole's the lighter. It's a family business. Not much of a family.

Our king's a human, you know. Came down the stair with the Hero! Nobody else down here has got a human.

Mind you, nobody else down here has got a town.

[first after the_kings_welcome]
All the way to the bottom? Oh, that's brave. Take a little glow with you, in case. I won't miss it.

[repeat after the_kings_welcome]
It's good that someone's going. It was getting to be a lot of lamp.

[first after the_light_goes_up]

[repeat after the_light_goes_up]
