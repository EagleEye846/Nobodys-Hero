# MRS. TANSY (on the quay, saltcombe_quay): a stubby ginger tabby in a navy coat and a flat
# cap, Saltcombe's lamplighter, with a pole as tall as two of her. She does the quay. Only the
# quay. Lantern 3: the lamps up the hill are lit too, and she didn't light them. She has a plain
# excuse and is not at all curious. Nobody says who, and nobody goes to look.

[first]
{smug}Evening. Mind the ladder.

{smug}Every lamp on this quay is mine. Trimmed, filled, lit, every night, rain or worse. And it's always worse.

{smug}A hundred years of worse, now. Never once gone out on my watch. Nor my mum's.

{huffy}And before you ask: the ones up the hill aren't mine. Must be someone else. The quay's plenty.
