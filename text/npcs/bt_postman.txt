# BT_POSTMAN: The beetle postman, in a blue cap, with a satchel of letters nobody collects. He walks the same route every day.

[first]
Post! Any post? Anyone expecting post? No? No one? Right.

I deliver to the Hero, you know. Every year. I leave it at the statue. They've never written back.

[repeat]
My route's twelve doors. Eleven don't answer. The twelfth answers 'GO AWAY'. It's a friendly house.

[first after the_kings_welcome]
A quest, eh? I'll pop a letter in your pocket. For the Diggings. If you see a mole called Mattock.

[repeat after the_kings_welcome]
Remember: Mattock. A mole. At the pithead. He has a hat.

[first after the_light_goes_up]
Post! So much post! Letters to everyone! It's like the old days!

[repeat after the_light_goes_up]
I've run out of satchel! I'm delighted! Don't tell the union!
