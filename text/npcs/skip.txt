# SKIP: a cave-frog kid on the Far Shore who launched a paper boat at the Reeds and has been waiting
# for it ever since. A tiny errand: the boat is in the Reeds, bring it back, get a skipping stone.

[first]
Gubb brought me over for the afternoon. I made a boat out of the back of my brother's homework and I launched it off the end of the Reeds and it just went. It didn't even look back.

Now I've got a flag and no boat.

[repeat]
It was a very small boat, with a charcoal flag on. If you see something small and optimistic, that's it.

[boat_back]
You hold out the paper boat. Skip stares at it, then at you, then at it.

IT CAME BACK! It's dry! Mostly! Nothing ever comes back!

He tucks it inside his jacket, very carefully, and presses a flat grey stone into your hand.

That's my best. Seven skips, once, with witnesses. You can have it. I'll make another stone. I'm much better at boats.

[boat_after]
I'm going to launch it again. With a rope this time. That's called learning.
