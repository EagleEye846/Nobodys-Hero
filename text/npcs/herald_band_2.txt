# Second trumpet: Ollo, who would rather be playing something else.

[first]
Psst. You want to hear something that isn't the fanfare?

Ollo glances at Ada, then plays six quick notes, very softly. It's a dance tune. It's good.

I wrote that. Ada says it's not in the book. The book has one tune in it and it goes BRAAP.

[repeat]
A hundred years. One tune. I love this village, but if I hear ba-da-da-DAAA one more time I'm taking up the drum.

If you ever find a lost trumpet, hide it. Its owner plays the DAAA flat.
