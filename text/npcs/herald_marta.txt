# MARTA: a herald who knits, on the bench in the square. Her scarf is longer
# than the square. She has no opinions about the Hero, and will tell you so.

[first]
Mm. Hello, dear. Mind the scarf.

You'll be the one from the stairs. Everyone's very excited. I'm knitting.

Are you the Hero? I've no opinion. I've knitted for six so far and not one of them wanted a scarf. Big heroic types. Cold necks, every one.

Here. Marta measures your neck with her eyes. Mm. Give me a day.

Have you met Ves yet? The girl in the chapel, with the umbrella. Go and say hello, dear.

[repeat]
Knit one, purl one, a hundred years. You get a rhythm.

If you go up past the Lookout, dear, take mittens. The Hero's meant to be above all that. You are not, no offence.

That scarf of mine? Goes round the square twice and into the inn. The innkeeper uses the end for the soup pot handle.

[first after ves_in_the_chapel]
Mm. Hello, dears. Mind the scarf.

So you found Ves. Good. She's sat in that chapel long enough to take root.

Marta measures your neck with her eyes, then looks at Ves's scarf and nods. Her mum's work. Good wool.

Yours is next, dear. Give me a day.
