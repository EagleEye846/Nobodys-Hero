Who are YOU? Where'd you come from? Is that a real sword? Can I hold it? Can I hold HER?

Are you staying? Are you staying for the bean harvest? Everybody has to help with the bean harvest. It's the law. It isn't really the law.

# --- SIDE QUEST: Biscuit the cat (she's in the healer's, where there isn't a cat) ---

[cat_ask]
Wait. WAIT. Don't go. This is important.

Wren grabs your sleeve with both hands.

Biscuit's GONE. She's a cat. She's brown. She sleeps in the hollow in the rug at the inn, every day, since the cart brought her in the spring, and today the rug's EMPTY.

Mum says we don't have a cat. Mabb says it's a rug. Bryony says there isn't a cat. But she's MY cat. I just haven't told her yet.

Find her? Please? She doesn't like being picked up. She'll let you do it once. She decides.

[cat_wait]
Biscuit! Brown! Rude! If you can't find her, go where somebody keeps saying there isn't a cat. That's where I'd hide, if I was a cat.

[cat_done]
Wren takes Biscuit out of your arms so fast you almost don't see it happen.

BISCUIT. Where WERE you? ...The HEALER'S? Bryony said there wasn't a cat! Bryony LIED!

She buries her face in the fur. Biscuit looks at you over Wren's shoulder with total contempt, which is how you know she's fine.

...Okay. Okay. I owe you. I've only got one thing. It's the best thing in Lasthome and I've never given it to anyone, so don't make it weird.

It's the bean medal. I won it last year. I won't win it this year, I'll be eleven by the harvest. So. It's yours. It's a bean.

[cat_after]
Biscuit is sitting on Wren's head. Wren is pretending not to notice. Biscuit is pretending to be a hat.
