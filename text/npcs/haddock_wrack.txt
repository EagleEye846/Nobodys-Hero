# HADDOCK (on the shingle at the Wrack landing, wrack_landing), beside the Bother. He is only here until the scene the_dowager_crab; after that he has gone round by the water to Saltcombe.

[first]
{determined}This is the Wrack. I put in here, and I don't go further. The bay's no place for a boat that wants to stay a boat.

{proud}Follow the shore east. Up the boards at the end is Saltcombe. I'll go round by the water and see ye there.

{smile}And wipe yer feet where it asks. Most places don't ask. Here, somebody does.

[repeat]
{proud}East, and up the boards. I'll see ye in Saltcombe.
