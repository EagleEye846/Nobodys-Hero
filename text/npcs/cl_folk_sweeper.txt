# The square's sweeper: a marmot with a long broom. Part of the life pass, round 2. (draft)

[first]

The leaves come in through the roof. Always have. It isn't a job. It's a conversation. They say 'here we are' and I say 'no you're not'.

[repeat]
Sweep, sweep. They'll be back by supper. They always are.
