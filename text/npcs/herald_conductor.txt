# The conductor of the fanfare band: Maestro Pell, with a baton (a knitting
# needle of Marta's) and immense dignity.

[first]
Ah. An audience. At last. Sit, sit. No, not in that chair. Any chair but that chair.

Pell. Maestro Pell. I conduct the Fanfare of Welcome, the Fanfare of Welcome (short) and, in the regrettable event, the Lament.

We rehearse daily. We have rehearsed daily for one hundred years. We are, I say without pride, the most rehearsed three trumpets in the world.

[repeat]
The Lament is beautiful. Nobody has ever heard it but us. We'd all rather keep it that way.

A conductor's secret: the baton is a needle of Marta's, the knitter. She wants it back. She's wanted it back for forty years.

Ba-da-da-DAAA. Feel the DAAA. Don't rush the DAAA. You'd think it was hard.
