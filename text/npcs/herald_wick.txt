# WICK: the oldest herald, by the Pottles' gate, wrapped in a blanket. He was
# a kid when the second climber came through, and remembers.

[first]
Hm? Oh. Another one. Sit down, sit down, you're making the square look untidy.

I'm Wick. Oldest herald. Not the wisest, the Head Herald will tell you, but the oldest, and that's harder.

I was knee-high when the scholar came through. Number two, in the book. Lantern, long coat, kind face. Went up past the Lookout like he was going for a walk.

Came back down a week later. Different. Quiet. Wouldn't say what was up there. Just kept the lantern lit and went on down the stair, and that was that.

If it's company you want for the climb, try the chapel. Ves. Little thing with an umbrella. Prays more than the rest of us put together.

[repeat]
That scholar. I think about him. Everyone else in the book went up and didn't come back, or turned round early. He's the only one who came back from the top and kept walking.

Kept his lantern lit the whole way down. Day and night. Like he was afraid of what'd happen if it went out.

You'll be going up past the Lookout, I expect. They all do. Just... come back down past me, afterwards. I'd like to see what the mountain does to a kind face.

[first after ves_in_the_chapel]
Hm? Oh. It's you. And you found Ves. Good. Sit down, the pair of you, you're making the square look untidy.

Wick. Oldest herald. I've watched a lot of folk go up past the Lookout on their own. Nicer, seeing two.

Mind she eats. And mind you both come back down past me, afterwards.
