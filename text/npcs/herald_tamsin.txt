# TAMSIN: a herald by the inn's fire, boots off, who went up past the
# Lookout once, got as far as the second cairn, and came back.

[first]
Don't mind me. I'm defrosting.

Tamsin. I went up. Past the Lookout, I mean. Furthest anyone from the village has been, which is the second cairn, which is not very far.

It's not that it's hard, up there. It's that it's quiet. So quiet you can hear yourself thinking, and I didn't much like what I was thinking.

Anyway. I came back. Greta made soup. Here I am. Toes nearly working.

[repeat]
If you go up past the second cairn, do me a favour and look back once. I want to know what the village looks like from up there.

The quiet up there... it's not empty, is it. It's listening. That's what got me. Something up there is listening.

Right. Toes. Back to work.
