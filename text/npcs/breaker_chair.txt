# THE EMPTY CHAIR (Chapter 5, the life pass): a breaker's chair on the Wreck Yard's Hull (wreck_hull), with a note pinned to the blanket. Duncan's clay pipe is in a corner of the Bilge Pumps (wreck_pumps). (draft)

[first]
An empty chair, with a plaid blanket over it and a note pinned to the blanket in capital letters:

BACK IN FIVE MINUTES. - DUNCAN

The paper has gone the colour of tea. Whoever Duncan was, he has been five minutes for a very long time.

[repeat]
BACK IN FIVE MINUTES. The ink is faded, but the pin is bright, as if somebody changes it.

There's a dent in the seat the exact shape of a man who has just stood up.

# Duncan's pipe is in your pack.
[pipe_done]
You set the pipe on the arm of the chair, where the wood is worn pale.

Nothing happens. The wind drops, just for a moment, as if it were holding its breath.

Under the note, in fresh yellow chalk, a new line has appeared: THANKS. HAD IT ALL ALONG. BACK IN FIVE.

There is a stub of chalk on the seat. It wasn't there before.

[pipe_after]
BACK IN FIVE MINUTES. The pipe is on the arm of the chair, and the blanket is, very slightly, warm.
