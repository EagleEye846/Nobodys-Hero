# HADDOCK: the coast's lightman, an old walrus in an oilskin and a sou'wester, on his rock on Gullrest's beach (gullrest_beach). He rings a brass handbell at nothing, and is a bit deaf. The meeting is the scene meet_haddock.

[first]
{proud}Morning! Haddock. Lightman of this coast, and all its tides.

[first after meet_haddock]
{proud}Lifeboat house is the big red doors, up the beach. Kettle's on. Tide table's on the wall; read it, don't rub it.

{smile}Cove's past that, and the jetty past the cove. Mind the castles, miss. They'll tell you the tide's not coming in.

{eh}It is.

[repeat after meet_haddock]
{eh}Eh? Oh, it's you. Aye, still here. Somebody's got to ring it.

{proud}Twice a day the sea comes up this beach and goes back down. Manners, mostly.
