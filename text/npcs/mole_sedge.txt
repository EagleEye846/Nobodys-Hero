# SEDGE: a grey mole in a yellow hard hat, the shift foreman at the pithead.
# Forty years on the job, and he still thinks the shift is on. Brisk, kind,
# and not easily shaken, except by the dark between the lamps.

[first]
Mind the rope, mind the edge and mind your head, in that order. Sedge. Foreman. Of a shift. Of two.

...Ah. You'll be the two the king sent. News gets round fast down here. There's nobody left to hold it up.

[repeat]
The lamps still burn the same, you'll have noticed. It's the dark between them that's got heavier. You feel it on the back of your neck.

I've stopped sending anyone below the old seam. Somebody ought to see to it. Somebody, I'm told, is you.

...Right. Well. Mind the carts. They're free, and they're awful.

And if you're in the Old Seam, leave the lever that says BROKEN alone. That's an order. ...From before my time. I've never been sure who it was for.

[first after the_light_goes_up]
Well. Would you look at that. The dark between the lamps has gone off somewhere. I can feel the back of my neck again.

I'd say the shift is over. Forty years, and it's over. ...Don't tell Mattock. He'll want to go home.

[repeat after the_light_goes_up]
Shift's over, officially. I'm going to stand here a bit longer anyway. Somebody ought to.
