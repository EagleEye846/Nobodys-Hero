# R5S_BURDOCK: a mole on Dandelion Hill who has lost his spectacles. An errand: his neighbour, two holes along, found
# them and is wearing them (r5s_mole_neighbour.txt). Burdock cannot see to notice. The life pass, round 2.

[first]
Who's that? Don't tell me. A boot. A boot and a half. Two boots and a smaller boot. Hello! I'm Burdock. I'd shake your hand but I've got no idea where it is.

[repeat]
It's very bright up here. I think. I can't tell. It's all one big yellow smudge, and I think the smudge is the hill.

[ask]
I had spectacles. Round ones. Very good. I took them off to look at something small, and I put them down, and then I couldn't see where I'd put them, which is the trouble with spectacles. They can't have gone far. Nobody round here would just pick up a man's spectacles and wear them.

[ask_ves]
{bored}...Round spectacles. We'll look. We won't have to look far.

[ask_bramble]
{determined}Don't worry! We'll find them. We're very good at finding things. Mostly we find them by accident.

[found]
You put the spectacles into Burdock's paws. He turns them over, finds the arms, finds his face, and puts them on, in that order.

The whole hill arrives. Burdock turns slowly in a circle, looking at everything, and then looks at you, and his whiskers go quite stiff.

"Oh," he says. "You're much smaller than you sounded." He rummages in the earth and comes up with a big brass button. "Here. It came off a coat. A much bigger coat than I've ever owned. I don't know whose. It's very valuable. It's a button."

[found_ves]
{smile}Somebody's got a very good eye for treasure.

[found_bramble]
{glee}A button! A real one! It's brilliant!

[after]
Burdock sits in the middle of the hill with his spectacles on, staring at a daisy. He has stared at it for some time. He seems to be getting something out of it.

[repeat 2]
Everything's so sharp. The daisies have edges. I never knew they had edges.
