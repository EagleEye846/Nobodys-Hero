# MOTHER TAPER: the oldest waxling, a tall candle in a shawl. She finds you in
# the Flower Room (src/data/scenes/meetTaper.js), then waits at her home up
# the stairs (src/data/rooms/taperHome.js).
# Each entry (separated by a blank line) is one page of the textbox.

# Said the first time you talk to her at home.
[first]
There you are. Boots off, sprout, I've only just swept. Sit by the fire and thaw out.

Stay near the lanterns out there. Most of them, anyway.

The waxlings will have a go at you. They're mostly drip and bluster. You needn't snuff them.

If you come back scorched, I've wax for that. Warm wax mends most things.

# Said every time after that.
[repeat]
Back again? Hold out your hands. ...Ten fingers. Good.

Hero or not, you track mud in like one.

The Keeper's lantern... I don't like how it leans toward people. Do be careful, sprout.

# Said the first time you talk to her at home after sparing her at the way out
# (src/data/scenes/taperSpared.js).
[first after taper_spared]
{surprised}...Sprout? Is that you?

{surprised}You came back up. Nothing comes back up those steps.

{sad}I was sure that door had swallowed you, like all the others.

{sad}I keep going over it. I gave you the worst frost I had, and you stood in it.

{crying}...I only ever got to watch them go. I never got to see one come back.

{glee}Well! Boots off. The soup's on, and there's far too much of it.

{glee}There always is. Old habit.

# Said every time after that.
[repeat after taper_spared]
{glee}Still coming home to visit. I'll never get used to it, sprout.

{worried}Mind that light out there. And eat something green now and then.
