# HADDOCK (in his parlour, haddock_light_parlour): at home in his armchair after he has told it. The first time is the scene haddock_tells_it; these are what he says afterwards. (draft).

[first]
{determined}Boat's at the bottom when ye're ready. No rush. The sea's waited this long.
{eh}And I'm not climbin' that tower with you, afore ye ask. Stairs and me fell out years ago.

[repeat]
{eh}Eh? No, I'm not comin' up. Stairs and me fell out years ago.
