# The herald on watch at the Lookout: Sedge, who has the best job in the
# village and knows it.

[first]
Hush. Watching.

...Sorry. Habit. Sedge. Lookout duty, dawn to dusk. Best shift there is. You get the whole sky.

I watch the stairs up, for the Hero coming down, and the stairs down, for the Hero coming up, and in between I watch the clouds do whatever clouds do.

Today somebody came up. That's you. That's the first interesting thing in... I'd have to check the log.

[repeat]
The stairs go on from here, north, into the cloud. Nobody from the village has come back from past the second cairn. One of ours got that far and came back with her face all wrong. The lamplighter went further. We don't talk about that.

Some days the cloud parts, right at the top, and you can see... I don't know what. The light's a funny colour up there. Not sunrise. Not quite.

Go up if you're going. I'll watch you as far as I can. That's the job.
