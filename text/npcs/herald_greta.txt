# GRETA: keeps the inn, the Soft Beds. Apron, ladle, flour on the fur, and
# the kind of welcome that doesn't ask questions until you've eaten.

[first]
In you come, in you come. Boots by the door. Soup's on, it's always on.

Greta ladles something into a bowl without asking and puts it in your hands. It's hot. It's very good.

Hero? Don't care. You're cold and you've come up the stairs, and that's a bed and a bowl in this house.

Beds are through there. Sleep in any of them. Yes, even that one. It's been turned down every night for a hundred years; it'd be a kindness to crease it.

And if you see Ves, the girl in the chapel, tell her there's soup. She forgets.

[repeat]
Sleep when you need to, love. The mountain doesn't care how tired you are, but I do.

A night in a proper bed puts you back together. HP and all. And you'll dream, like as not. Everyone dreams up here. It's the thin air.

Second helpings are free. Thirds are free. Fourths, I start to worry about you.

[first after ves_in_the_chapel]

You've got her, then. Good. Somebody's got to make sure she eats.
