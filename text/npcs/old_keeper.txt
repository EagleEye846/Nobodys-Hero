# OLD KEEPER: a hooded figure with a lantern, in the west room of the Ruins.
# He introduces himself in a cutscene when you arrive
# (src/data/scenes/meetingTheKeeper.js), so these pick up from there.
# Each entry (separated by a blank line) is one page of the textbox.

# Said the first time you talk to them.
[first]
Ah, you came back to the light. Good. A lantern gets lonely, you know.

That dummy in the east room was built to train heroes. Now it trains itself to hate them.

If it attacks, don't just run from the bullets. Brushing close past them fills your spell power.

The floating crystals are older than me. Touch one, and the Ruins will remember you.

And up the hall, past me, is Moss.

He is one of many merchants with a shop full of things meant for the Hero.

{sly}Tell him the Keeper sent you. He may even give you a fair price.

# Said every time after that.
[repeat]
The lantern still burns. That is enough for today.

The others will say you are not the Hero. Let them talk.

They have had a long time to decide what a Hero looks like.

North of here is the Lantern Hall. Lately a moth has taken a liking to the light. A big one.

# In Heralds' Rest, once he's met you on the Shoulder
# (src/data/scenes/keeperOnTheShoulder.js): by the frozen fountain.
[first after keeper_on_the_shoulder]
There you are. You made it up the ice.

{glee}I told them you would. They didn't believe me; they've been let down before.

{sly}Lovely people, the heralds. A hundred years of practising a welcome. Don't tell them you're tired of it before they've finished.

The old goat, Nan, remembers me. Don't let that worry you. I remember her too.

The stairs go on from the Lookout. I'd rest first, if I were you. A proper bed, a proper sleep. The mountain will keep.

{worried}And whatever you dream up here... it's only the thin air. Don't go looking for it when you wake.

# Chapter 3: by the woodcutters' fire in the Pinefall, after he's told you
# where the wind took Ves (src/data/scenes/keeperAtTheFire.js).
[first after keeper_at_the_fire]
The fire's warm. Sit as long as you like.

South, through the turning wood. {sly}Mind the brambles. They're more patient than they look.

[repeat after keeper_at_the_fire]
{glee}Stay where the light can find you, little one. Under all this red, it's easy to lose.

[repeat after keeper_on_the_shoulder]
The fountain's lovely, isn't it. Frozen in the middle of giving.

{sly}I know how it feels.

Sleep at the inn before you go on. The soup, the beds. You won't find either above the clouds.

{glee}Stay where the light can find you, little one. Up here, that's harder than it sounds.

# On the Old Street in the dead city, by the lamp he lit
# (src/data/scenes/keeperLightsALamp.js).
[first after keeper_lights_a_lamp]
Mind the boards in the forum. They'll take her weight, but not yours.

{thoughtful}Strange place, this. Built for a Hero, and every lamp lit for him the night they thought he'd come.

{sad}They burned all night. And the next. Then one by one they went out, and nobody had the heart to light them again.

[repeat after keeper_lights_a_lamp]
{glee}One more lit. It's slow work, but I've the time.

Go on. Get her umbrella back.
