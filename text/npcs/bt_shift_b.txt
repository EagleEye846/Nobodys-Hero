# BT_SHIFT_B: A brown mole coming home off the Diggings shift with a hat full of grit and a fine opinion of it.

[first]
Home from the Diggings! Nothing found. Plenty of dust. A very good day, on balance.

[repeat]
Tell my feet they're home soon. They won't believe me.

[first after the_kings_welcome]
Heard about the show. I missed it. I was underground, doing my bit. Was it loud? It sounded loud.

[repeat after the_kings_welcome]
I'd like to sit. I'll sit when I'm dead. Or at the inn. Whichever's nearer.

[first after the_light_goes_up]
You did it! I can tell by the shine on my hat. Bless you, whoever you are.

[repeat after the_light_goes_up]
Look at me, home in daylight. Well. Sunstone-light. Still counts.
