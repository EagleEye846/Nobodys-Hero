# BRAM: a herald in the square who has lost his trumpet. Again. He is very
# good at the fanfare and very bad at keeping hold of things.

[first]
Oh! Hello! Have you seen a trumpet? Brass. About this long. Goes "BRAAAP".

No? Right. Right. It'll turn up. They always turn up. Usually in the snow. Once in the soup.

I'm Bram. Second trumpet. Well. First trumpet, until I lost the first trumpet.

Are you the Hero? Don't tell me, it's more fun not knowing. If you are, I should probably find the trumpet.

[repeat]
Still no trumpet. I've started whistling the fanfare instead. The Head Herald says it doesn't carry.

If you go up the Lookout stair, could you keep an eye out? I may have left it on a cairn. Or in a cairn. Or built a cairn on it.

The fanfare goes BRAAP, BRAAP, ba-da-da-DAAA. The DAAA is the hard bit. A hundred years and I still rush it.
