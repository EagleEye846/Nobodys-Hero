# HADDOCK (on the slip at the far side of the Wreck Yard, wreck_slip), beside the Bother. (The Fair Return's old fog bell: he
# mentions it in passing, never her name, and never where it went.) Walk down to the end of the slip and he rows you home to Saltcombe (the scene wreck_slip_home, any time). The way here is over the mast; the star-shard that used to be up it is somewhere out in the fog, and he has a few things to say about that.

[first]
{proud}There ye are. Thought the yard had kept you.

{smile}End o' the slip when ye want fetchin'. I'll not go deaf on you. Not more than I am.

[repeat]
{smile}End o' the slip, and I'll row ye home.

[first after wreck_star_shard]
{proud}There ye are! Look at that sword. Pullin' like a dog on a lead, and all the one way.

{determined}Star-iron, that is. Only one smith on this coast would know what to do with it: Orla Cobb, out on the Beacon Isles. Big grey seal. Bad temper. Best hands I ever saw. She'll want what it's missin', mind.

# (draft) The Fair Return, in passing: he won't say her name.
{eh}See that post out on the point? There were a fog bell on it once. Me gran said they rang it for a ship that never come in, and one foggy night summat out there rang back. Took it down after, they did, and good riddance. Leave the point be.

{smile}End o' the slip when ye want fetchin'.

[repeat after wreck_star_shard]
{smile}End o' the slip, and I'll row ye home. Orla's on the Isles, when ye're ready for her. And leave that point alone.

# (draft) Turns.
[repeat 2 after wreck_star_shard]
{smile}Orla'll grumble. Let her. It's how she says hello.

# (draft) The Fair Return, in passing.
[repeat 3 after wreck_star_shard]
{eh}Fair Return? Where'd ye hear that? ...Me gran's gran waited on that ship. Wouldn't have her name said in the house after.

{smile}Ye'll hear singin' off the water, out here, some fogs. Don't sing along. It only encourages 'em.
