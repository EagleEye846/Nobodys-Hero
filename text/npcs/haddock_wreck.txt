# HADDOCK (on the slip at the far side of the Wreck Yard, wreck_slip), beside the Bother. Walk down to the end of the slip and he rows you home to Saltcombe (the scene wreck_slip_home, any time). The way here is over the mast; the star-shard that used to be up it is somewhere out in the fog, and he has a few things to say about that.

[first]
{proud}There you are. Thought the yard had kept you.

{smile}End of the slip when you want fetching. I'll not go deaf on you. Not more than I am.

[repeat]
{smile}End of the slip, and I'll row you home.

[first after wreck_star_shard]
{proud}There you are! Look at that sword. Pulling like a dog on a lead, and all the one way.

{determined}Star-iron, that is. Only one smith on this coast would know what to do with it: Orla Cobb, out on the Beacon Isles. Big grey seal. Bad temper. Best hands I ever saw. She'll want what it's missing, mind.

# (draft)
{eh}Right out at the far end of the cove, behind my old upturned boat, there's the fog bell. I'd leave it be, if I were you. It rings back.

{smile}End of the slip when you want fetching.

[repeat after wreck_star_shard]
{smile}End of the slip, and I'll row you home. Orla's on the Isles, when you're ready for her. And leave the bell alone.

# (draft) Turns.
[repeat 2 after wreck_star_shard]
{smile}Orla'll grumble. Let her. It's how she says hello.
