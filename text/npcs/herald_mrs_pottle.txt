# MRS POTTLE: Pottle's mother, in her kitchen, baking. She has made a
# welcome cake every week for a hundred years and is, by now, extremely good at it.

[first]
Boots! Oh, you've wiped them. Good. Sit. No, sit. There's cake.

I'm Mrs Pottle. You'll have met my Pottle, I expect. He argues with people. He gets it from his father, who got it from me.

Hero or not, you'll eat. The cake's for the Hero's welcome, but there's always another cake, so that's never really been a reason not to cut it.

[repeat]
A hundred years of practice cakes. The first few were terrible, my grandmother said. By year ten they were good. By year fifty they were famous as far as the Shoulder.

If my Pottle gives you any cheek, you tell him his mother said the Hero would have better manners. That usually works. Once.

Take a slice for the road. Take two. The road up from here is long and nobody's measured it.
