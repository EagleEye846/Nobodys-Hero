# Taper's candle-making notebook, on its stand by the stairs (it has a "!" over
# it until you've read it). Each entry is a page. The enemies are riddles.
[candle_book]
A well-thumbed notebook, open on a little stand. The handwriting is round and careful.

HOW TO MAKE A CANDLE, by T.
Every candle needs Wax, and Thread for its wick. The rest is just for flavour.

WAX: shed by the little candles that wander about on their own.

THREAD: pulled from a sack of fluff, come to life.

MOONBELL PETALS: carried off by the dusty, long-eared things that nibble my flowers.

GLOW DUST: left behind by the winged things that can't stay away from a flame.

SHADOW ASH: what's left when something from the dark between the lanterns fades away.

PLAIN: 2 Wax, 1 Thread. Nothing fancy.
HEARTH: 4 Wax, 2 Thread. Warms you right through.

MOONBELL: add 2 petals. Its scent calms even angry things.

GLOW: add 2 Glow Dust. Bright and long, and it makes you braver.

ASH: add 2 Shadow Ash. More smoke than light, and the smoke hides you.

Light a candle when you need it most.
And never follow a light you didn't make yourself.

(You can make candles from CANDLES in your menu. Press C.)
