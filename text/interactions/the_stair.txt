# THE LONG STAIR: things you can read and look at on the mountain (Chapter 2).

# What a save point says when you save here.
[save_point]
The crystal's light is pale in the daylight, but warm all the same.
Your progress is saved.

[save_failed]
The crystal flickers, but nothing happens...
(Your browser isn't letting the game save.)

# The sign at the bottom of the stair.
[stair_tally]
Carved into the bottom step: six tally marks, worn almost smooth by the weather.

Beneath them, in fresh chalk, someone has added a seventh.
And a question mark.

# The old pack on the ledge (it gives you a Climber's Ration).
[stair_pack]
A weathered pack, wedged up on the ledge long ago. Someone left in a hurry.

Inside, still sealed in waxed paper: a climber's ration.

[stair_rockslide]
A rockslide has buried the stair here. Boulders, packed tight.

Hoofprints lead off east, along the foot of the cliff.

[stair_boulder]
A boulder, mossy on top. It looks like it's been here longer than the stair.

[stair_pine]
A mountain pine. It smells sharp and clean, nothing like the Ruins.

[stair_cliff]
Sheer rock, layered like the pages of a very old book.

[stair_block]
A fallen block of the stair, about knee-high. You could jump up onto it.

[stair_tall_block]
A tall block of the old stair, too high to jump onto from the grass. Something lower might help.

# --- THE GOAT TRACK ---

[goat_rockslide]
The rockslide, piled higher than your head. Something with hooves has been over it, somehow.

[goat_fallen_stair]
A whole run of the stair came down with the slide and landed on top of it. You could climb onto it, a block at a time.

# The old pack on the end of the fallen stair (it gives you a Climber's Jacket).
[goat_pack]
Another pack, wedged between the blocks. The same make as the one at the bottom.

Folded inside: a padded jacket, patched at the elbows. Whoever wore it meant to go a long way.

[goat_cairn]
Six flat stones stacked on the boulder, one on top of another.

There's room on top for one more.

# --- THE START OF THE CLIMB ---

# The herald's waymark at the top of the lower stair.
[climb_waymark]
WELCOME, HERO, TO MOUNT VIGIL!

The Long Stair climbs from here to the summit, where the sun first touches the world.

Halfway up waits HERALDS' REST: hot soup, soft beds, and a fanfare in your honour.
(Please do not ring the bell after dark.)

Underneath, scratched into the post in a different hand:
"stair's gone above here. use the rings."

[climb_tent]
A small tent, patched in three colours. The flap is tied shut from outside.

Whoever pitched it meant to come back.

[climb_campfire]
A ring of stones round a fire that went out a long time ago. The ash is cold all the way through.

# The pack by the tent (it gives you the Climbing Rope).
[climb_pack]
A third pack, the same make as the others. This one was left on purpose, propped against the tent.

Inside, coiled neatly: a long rope with an iron hook. A single tally mark is scratched into the hook.

[climb_pack_empty]
The empty pack. Someone packed it for a long climb, and then didn't take it.

[climb_broken_stair]
The great stair just stops here, snapped off at the foot of the cliff. The rest of it is somewhere up above.

[climb_cliff]
Sheer rock, too smooth to climb by hand.

[climb_stair_onward]
The stair winds on up the mountain. Somewhere above, a bell rings once.

# --- THE SHEER FACE ---

# The notice at the foot of the face (how swinging works).
[face_notice]
CLIMBERS, TAKE NOTE!
This face is too high for one throw. Hook the lowest ring and swing.

Hold left or right along with your swing to swing higher. The higher you swing, the farther you can reach.

Press Z at the right moment to catch the next ring.

For a ring off to the side, wait for the top of your swing. For one straight above, catch it as you pass the bottom.

Miss, and you'll be starting over from the grass. (X lets go, if you'd rather.)

[face_rock]
A sheer wall of rock. Iron rings are hammered into it here and there, all the way up.
