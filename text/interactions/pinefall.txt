# THE PINEFALL: things you can read and look at in the scarlet forest below
# the clouds (Chapter 3).

[save_failed]

# --- Shared, across the forest ---

[scarlet_tree]
A tall tree in full autumn. Every leaf on it is red.

Every few seconds, one lets go.

[bramble]
A wall of bramble, thick as a hedge and twice as rude about it.

There are berries in it. They look like they'd bite back.

# --- WHERE YOU FELL ---

# Ves's mitten (it gives you the mitten, then the [got_ves_mitten] words).
[landing_mitten]
Something mustard-yellow in the red leaves.

A mitten. Hand-knitted, a little lopsided. Still a bit warm.

[landing_great_tree]
The great tree you came down through. Its crown is gone, snapped off and
lying in a heap.

The branches broke your fall. Most of them, anyway.

[landing_crown]
The tree's snapped crown, in a tangle of red branches.

You can see the hole you made in it.

# --- THE BRAMBLE GATE ---

[gate_gate]
An old gate of woven branches, tied shut with a rope that runs off to three
levers in the leaves.

[gate_open]
The rope goes taut, and the gate creaks open.

[gate_reset]
Clunk. The levers all drop back, and the rope goes slack.

That wasn't the order.

[gate_sign]
A board nailed to a tree, the letters burnt in:

WOODCUTTERS' GATE. PULL AS THE SUN GOES.

Underneath, smaller: "it comes up in the east. at noon it stands in the
south. it goes down in the west. honestly, it's not hard."

[gate_lever]
A wooden lever, worn smooth by a lot of hands.

# --- THE DEADFALL ---

# Ves's ribbon, caught on a branch up on the fallen trunks.
[deadfall_ribbon]
A coral ribbon, snagged on a branch where the wind dropped it.

You've seen it in her hair the whole way up the mountain.

[deadfall_pack]
An old pack under a tree, half buried in leaves. Inside, a bag of pine
candy, still sealed.

[deadfall_pack_empty]
The old pack. Empty now, apart from leaves.

[deadfall_trunk]
A fallen giant, lying where it came down. Far too tall to climb straight up.

A stump might help.

[deadfall_stump]
A stump, about knee high. A good step up.

# --- THE COLD BROOK ---

[brook_sign]
A sign by the water:

NO BRIDGE DOWN HERE. NEVER WAS. USE THE ONE UP TOP.
(YES, YOU HAVE TO CLIMB. NO, WE'RE NOT SORRY.)

[brook_water]
Snowmelt off the mountain, fast and clear and very, very cold.

It's not something you'd want to wade.

[brook_cliff]
A shelf of grey rock. There's a ring hammered in at the top, for a rope.

[brook_bridge]
A plank bridge across the brook. It sways, but it holds.

# --- WOODCUTTERS' CAMP ---

[camp_fire]
A campfire. Someone's been feeding it a stick at a time, just enough to keep
it going.

[camp_bench]
A bench made of half a log. The bark's been worn off where people sit.

[camp_tent]
A canvas tent, patched in three colours. The flap's tied shut.

[camp_table]
A long table, set for eight. Seven bowls are clean.

The eighth has had a very small amount of porridge in it for a while.

[camp_stall]
A little stall, made from crates and a door laid flat. On it: pinecones,
painted red. A jar of something sticky. A carved wooden owl.

None of it has a price. All of it has a little card that says "ask".

[camp_stall_sign]
A hand-painted sign, very cheerful:

THE GREAT PINEFALL SALE!!
EVERYTHING MUST GO!!
(NOTHING HAS GONE YET.)

[camp_crates]
Crates of split wood, stacked neat. Each one is labelled with a name.

[camp_barrel]
A rain barrel. There's a ladle tied to it, and a cup with a chip in it.

[camp_notices]
A notice board. Most of the notes are about firewood.

One, newer than the rest, in big letters: "SOMETHING BIG IN THE WOOD
AGAIN. GO ROUND BY THE CAMP. DON'T GO TO THE CLEARING."

[camp_fence]
A rail fence. It keeps the camp in rather than anything out.

# --- THE TURNING WOOD ---

[maze_tree]
A scarlet tree, taller than the brambles. You've seen this one before.

Haven't you?

[maze_pack]
An old pack, tucked in the brambles where nobody would find it. Somebody
did. Inside, a climber's ration, wrapped in wax paper.

[maze_pack_empty]
The old pack. Empty now. Whoever hid it was good at hiding.

# --- THE TORN CLEARING ---

[tatters_scrap]
A scrap of evening-blue wool, caught on a branch.

It's the blue of her coat.

[tatters_scrap_2]
Another scrap of blue wool, torn at the edges.

The leaves round it are trampled flat, in a circle, like something big went
round and round here.

[tatters_scrap_3]
A last scrap of blue, and beside it, in the soft ground, a single footprint
going south. Small. Running.

She got away from whatever it was.

[tatters_claws]
Claw marks in the bark, four deep grooves, one above the other.

The sap in them is still wet.

[tatters_claws_high]
More claw marks, higher up the trunk than you could reach on tiptoe.

You decide not to think about that.

[tatters_brush]
A thicket, crushed flat on one side, as if something heavy pushed straight
through it.

# --- THE LONG DROP ---

[ravine_cliff]
The ravine wall, grey rock in shelves. Each shelf has a ring at its edge.

Someone has done this climb before. A lot of someones.

[ravine_end]
A post at the bottom of the ravine, with a sign hung off it:

THE LANTERN MARSH. KEEP TO THE LIGHTS.

Somebody has scratched underneath: "which ones?"

[ravine_mist]
Below the last of the trees, the ground goes flat and grey, and then it
stops being ground at all. Mist, as far as you can see.

There are lights out in it. Small ones, swinging.

(The way on isn't open yet.)
