# --- Things every room shares ---

[sc_rock]
Dark rock overhead, glazed on its underside to a dull blue-grey. It looks like something that has been very hot and then thought better of it.

[sc_face]
Rock face, run to glass. Where the heat was, it's smooth as a bottle. Where it wasn't, it's the ordinary banded brown of the mine, and the line between them is as clean as a ruled one.

[sc_ore]
A knuckle of ore the glass has run round and left alone.

[sc_glass]
A crystal the colour of a dull sky, grown straight out of the glaze. It's cold, and it gives off a thin light that has no business being there. When the sword gets near, it hums.

[sc_stalag]
A stalagmite of glass, thick as a leg, with a drip frozen halfway down it.

[sc_lamp]
A miner's lamp, trimmed and lit by somebody a long time ago. It's not gone out. It has just been very, very patient.

[sc_shoring]
A pit prop, wedged in tight against the wall. The glass has crept up it and stopped at the cap beam, as if it had been told not to go further.

[sc_sacks]
Sacks of ore, tied at the neck and stacked just so. Whoever stacked them would have hated how long they've been left.

[sc_spoil]
A heap of spoil with a shovel in it, the glaze run down one side like icing.
