# --- THE TOWN'S WALLS AND LAMPS (every room) ---

[town_wall]
Old carved stone, older than the town. Somebody has patched the worst bits with planks, and somebody else has painted the planks to match the stone, not quite.

[town_rock]
Bare cavern rock, high above. Somewhere up there is the roof of the world.

[town_lamps_wall]
A string of glow-worm lamps running along the wall, each one with a worm in it, glowing quietly and looking a bit tired.

[town_banner_wall]
A red-and-gold banner, hung up for the king's last show. It has a little mushroom stitched in the corner, which is the town's mark.

[town_pillar]
A carved stone pillar with a glow-worm lantern hung from it. The carving is of a lot of little hands, all holding something up.

[town_broken_pillar]
A pillar snapped off short, with toadstools growing out of the top. Nobody has the heart to tidy it.

[town_barrel]
A barrel. It says SPORES on it, in the sort of letters you use when you want people to be careful.

[town_house]
A round-windowed house with a roof of moss and little toadstools. Someone is cooking inside.

[town_house_door]
A round door, shut. There's a welcome mat on the step that says GO AWAY, WELCOME.

[town_plank]
A creature-built wall of lashed planks, tight as a drum, with a mushroom-cap roof over it. Warm light comes through the window.

[town_toadstools]
A bed of toadstools with a stone edge. A sign pushed into the soil says PLEASE DON'T, and then, underneath, in smaller letters, SERIOUSLY.

# --- THE MARKET ---

[market_stall_mole]
A stall of cheese. Rock-hard cheese, in fact. A sign says TASTES BETTER THAN IT LOOKS, and below it, in a different hand, IT'D HAVE TO.

[market_stall_pebble]
Pebble's stall: toadstool pasties under a glass dome, honey in jars with the lamp-light caught in them, toffee, candles for the dark, and a rail of mossweave cloaks that look warm enough to live in.

[market_stall_frog]
A frog's stall of marbles and fishing lines. All the marbles are slightly slimy. There is no explaining it.

[market_stall_snail]
A snail's stall, selling very small, very slow-to-eat sweets. A sign says BACK IN A MOMENT. The sign has been up a while.

[market_stall_snail_ves]

[market_stall_beetle]

[market_burrow_door]

[market_lamp]
A glow-worm lamp. The worm is humming to itself, a little flat.

[market_barrels]
Barrels, stacked and tied. One of them is warm and is making a quiet noise like a snore.

[market_crates]

[market_noticeboard]

[market_chest]

# --- THE SQUARE ---

[square_well]
A well with a little stone roof. The bucket comes up full of water and, once, a very small frog. He says he's fine.

[square_statue]
A statue of the Hero, half-crumbled, standing in the plaza. It was carved a long time ago, by someone who had not seen them. The head is the size of a barrel, the sword is the size of a door, and the feet are missing.

Underneath there's a plaque: NINE FEET TALL, AND WORTH EVERY INCH.

[square_statue_ves]
{bored}Nine feet. {smile}Somebody was either very excited, or very short.

[square_noticeboard]

THE SUNSTONE IS NOT DIM. IT IS "RESTING". -- HIS MAJESTY

LOST: ONE LAMPLIGHTER'S POLE. IT WAS HERE A MOMENT AGO.

CHOIR PRACTICE: CANCELLED (THE SUNSTONE). ALSO CANCELLED: THE CHOIR.

[square_signpost]

[square_bench]
A bench, polished by a lot of waiting.

[square_lamp]
A glow-worm lamp. Its worm waves, a bit shyly.

[square_barrels]

[square_pouch]
A purse in the corner behind the toadstool beds. The toadstools aren't telling.

# --- HOBNAIL'S FORGE ---

[forge_vent]
A great iron grate over a crack in the rock, with heat pouring up out of it and a glow like a small sunrise. The air above it wobbles. The floor's warm through your boots.

[forge_coal]
A heap of coal, black and shining. A chest is half-buried behind it, as if it had been shoved there in a hurry.

[forge_anvil]
An anvil as big as a wheelbarrow, scarred all over. One dent in it is the exact shape of Hobnail's hammer. It was there before the apprentice was born.

[forge_quench]
A quenching tub. The water in it is black and, if you listen, hisses a little at you.

[forge_grindstone]
A grindstone, still turning slowly by itself. Nobody's near it. It is better not to ask.

[forge_barrel]
A barrel of horseshoe nails. Wait, there aren't any horses. They're for something else.

[forge_crates]
Crates of ore, labelled by a hand that only uses one word: ORE. ORE. ORE. MORE ORE.

[forge_sign]
A sign hammered into the wall in letters like crowbars:

NO TOUCHING.
NO ASKING.
NO.
-- H.

[forge_sign_ves]
{bored}Three rules and a signature. I respect anyone who gets to the point.

[forge_chest]

# --- THE MOSSY PILLOW ---

[inn_wall]
Panelling the colour of strong tea, with a row of little coat hooks. Most of the hooks are holding a hat that has not moved in years.

[inn_rug]
A round rug, woven in mushroom rings. It's frayed at the edge where someone's been practising their dancing.

[inn_fire]
A hearth with a fire that never goes out, and a sign over it: THE MOSSY PILLOW. WARM, DRY, AND ONLY MOSTLY MOSSY.

[inn_counter]
The innkeeper's counter, scrubbed bare. Behind it hang a hundred keys, none of which are for any door in the building.

[inn_pot]
A pot of toadstool stew, bubbling quietly on the stove. There's one pasty left in a cloth beside it.

[inn_pot_empty]
An empty pot with a spoon in it and a sign: BACK TOMORROW.

[inn_table]
A long table with mugs, a half-played game of toadstool-and-ladders, and the great dent where a guest once fell asleep face-first.

[inn_bench]
A bench, worn smooth. There's a pillow at one end, very mossy, and a sign: DON'T.

[inn_bed]
A real bed, with a patched quilt and a pillow that looks, frankly, a bit damp, but in a friendly way. You could sleep here.

[inn_barrel]
A barrel of ginger moss-beer. A sign says IT'S NOT FOR YOU, and somebody has added, THE BAT.

[inn_rules]

[hall_wall]
A long carved wall, covered in little painted panels. Damp has got into most of them.

[hall_bench]
A bench for sitting and looking at the mural. A tiny sign says PLEASE LOOK RESPECTFULLY. Somebody has scratched in underneath it: AT AN ANGLE.

[hall_noticeboard]
The curator's notices:

THE HERO WENT BACK UP IT (WE ARE FAIRLY SURE). THE STAIR FELL IN SOON AFTER. NOT OUR FAULT.
RELICS: SEE THE KING. DON'T ASK.
THE DAMP: SORRY.

[hall_sign]
HALL OF THE VISIT. Admission free. Opinions free. A damp patch by the door is free but please do not take it with you.

[hall_lamp]
A glow-worm lamp. The worm is looking up at the mural as if it had never seen it before.

[hall_chest]
A chest in the corner, half hidden behind the barrels, with a note on it: DONATIONS. Underneath: IT'S MOSTLY SWEETS.

[hall_barrels]
Barrels of paste and whitewash. The curator keeps patching the mural, and the damp keeps winning.

[throne_carpet]
