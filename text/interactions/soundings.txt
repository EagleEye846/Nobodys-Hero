# --- THE MOUTH ----------------------------------------------------------------------

[mouth_chart]
A chart under a lid of glass, gone green at the edges: THE SOUNDINGS, SURVEYED FOR THE SALTCOMBE HARBOUR BOARD. The caves are drawn as rooms joined by passages, with the depths written small.
THE MOUTH, here. East: the ECHO HALL, a great cave with its own sea. "Floods. Its sluice is worked from the top of the TIDE STAIR."
Up the stair: the THREE WELLS. "Three windlasses. Not a job for one." From the wells, a dotted line straight back down to the hall: "chute (one way)".
South of the hall, under the water: the DROWNED CHAPEL, "drained from the wells", and under that the WEIGHT ROOM, ringed in red: "deepest sounding. The weight is kept here."
North of the hall: the WAY UP, to the Isles. "Gate weighted. Weight kept below."
