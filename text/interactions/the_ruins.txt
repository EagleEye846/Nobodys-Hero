# --- Lantern Hall puzzle: light the lanterns in the old order ---
[hall_riddle]
The lanterns of this hall were lit in the old way:
First the west, where the sun goes to sleep.
Then the east, where it wakes.
And last of all, the heart.

[hall_lantern]
You light the lantern. Its flame is small, but steady.

[hall_gate]
An iron gate. Words are carved above it:
LIGHT THE WAY.

[hall_gate_open]
The last flame catches... and with a long groan, the gate swings open.

[hall_gate_reset]
The flames gutter and go out, one by one.
That wasn't the old way.

# --- Pillar Room puzzle: two levers, one hidden ---
[pillar_lever]

[pillar_gate]
A wide iron gate. Two lever-shaped marks are carved into the stone beside it.

One lever must be hiding somewhere. The other is up high, out of easy reach.

[pillar_gate_open]
