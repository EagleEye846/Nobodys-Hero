# --- Lantern Hall puzzle: light the lanterns in the old order ---
[hall_riddle]
The lanterns of this hall were lit in the old way:
First the east, where the sun wakes.
Then the heart.
And last of all, the west, where it goes to sleep.

Scrawled underneath, in a different hand:
...or something, I don't know.

[hall_lantern]
You light the lantern. Its flame is small, but steady.

[hall_gate]
An iron gate. Words are carved above it:
LIGHT THE WAY.

[hall_gate_open]
The last flame catches... and with a long groan, the gate swings open.

[hall_gate_reset]
The flames gutter and go out, one by one.
That wasn't the old way.

# --- Pillar Room puzzle: two levers, one hidden ---
[pillar_lever]
You pull the lever. Stone grinds somewhere nearby.

[pillar_gate]
A wide iron gate. Two lever-shaped marks are carved into the stone beside it.

One lever must be hiding somewhere. The other is up high, out of easy reach.

[pillar_gate_open]
Clunk. The gate rattles open.

# ruins_entrance

[r1_en_column]
A fallen column, snapped in two. Someone has used it as a bench, and someone else as a bed for moss. The moss won.

[r1_en_urn]
A cracked urn with a fern in it. The fern is delighted about the crack.

[r1_en_bench]
A stone bench, worn into a hollow by a great many people sitting down to think about it.

[r1_en_stash]
A mouse's treasury behind a heap of rubble: bottle caps, shirt buttons, a bent spoon and one real coin, all sorted by size.

You take only the coin. A very small voice, somewhere, says nothing, loudly.

# lantern_hall

[r1_lh_stump]
The stump of a pillar, broken off at the knee. Ivy has moved into it as if it was always going to.

[r1_lh_column]
A fallen pillar, lying where it fell. Somebody has swept carefully round it, every day, for years.

[r1_pi_hamper]
A picnic hamper tied with a ribbon, tucked into the corner where the pillars throw their shadow. A card on the lid:

OPEN ON ARRIVAL. (IT'S FINE. NOBODY MINDS.)

You open it. The flask inside is still sealed.

[r1_pi_column]
A pillar that lay down for a rest a long time ago. The floor has grown round it, a little.

[r1_pi_stump]
Another broken pillar. They'll all be like this one day. The tall ones know it. You can tell by how they stand.

[r1_pi_helm]
An old helmet, a daisy growing out of it. Somebody has left it standing up straight, as if it might still be wanted.

[r1_pi_candles]
Candle stubs in a saucer, burning in the corner of a very empty room. Somebody likes to leave a light on for whoever's next.

[r1_fl_can]
A second watering can, left by the beds, nearly full. A bee is using it as a hot tub.

[r1_fl_trug]
A trug of gloves and a trowel. The gloves are so worn they've taken the shape of a hand holding a flower.

[r1_fl_barrel]
A barrel brim-full of rainwater, which is odd indoors. You let it be odd.

[r1_fl_button]
A small brass button, lying at the edge of the pool, shining like it wants to be found.

# way_out

[r1_wo_cups]
Tin cups lined up on a ledge, three to a row. Each one has a dried ring of soup in the bottom.

[r1_wo_bench]
A stone bench halfway along, for people who needed to think about it. The seat is worn in two places.

[r1_wo_stump]
A pillar stub, broken off short. It keeps the corridor honest.

[r1_en_stash_gone]
The treasury, minus one coin. The rest is arranged with great dignity.

[r1_pi_hamper_gone]
The hamper, empty, with its card still on the lid. It looks pleased to have been opened.
