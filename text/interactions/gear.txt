# Gear: things for getting around that you keep for good (src/data/gear.js).
# [got_<id>] is what you see when you get it, after the words of whatever it
# was in. The other sections are shared by every room.

[got_climbing_rope]
You got the CLIMBING ROPE!

Face a cliff with an iron ring at the top and press Z to climb up to it.

Stand on a ring and face down the cliff to climb back down.

On a face too tall for one throw, hook the lowest ring and swing.

Hold left or right with the swing to swing higher and reach farther.

Press Z at the right moment to catch the next ring. X lets go.

# Facing a cliff with a ring at the top, before you have the rope.
[ring_no_rope]
High above, an iron ring is hammered into the top of the cliff.

With a rope, you could get up there.

# Facing a steep face with rings hammered into it, before you have the rope.
[swing_no_rope]
Iron rings are hammered into the rock above you, one after another, all the way up.

With a rope, you could swing from one to the next.
