# (draft) THE REGISTRY'S QUEST (chapter 3): three torn leaves of the Welcome Register, in three hidden places. All three and the sword open the sealed cleft off the Old Street.

[got_register_leaf_names]
You got the LEAF OF NAMES.

A page torn out at the binding, ruled in columns and crowded with names in a hundred different hands, every one of them ticked. In the corner, a numeral: I. It is warm, the way paper is after a hundred years in a sunny window.

[about_register_leaf_names]
Page I of the Welcome Register: the Names, every one ticked. There are two more leaves out there, torn out the same night. A clerk would have kept the second where the fen ends, the toll being a clerk's business.

[got_register_leaf_hours]
You got the LEAF OF HOURS.

A page ruled in thin rows, one for each hour from dusk to dawn, every row ticked. The corner is marked II. It has dried stiff in the shape of the hand that held it, and a fish hook is still through the top.

[about_register_leaf_hours]
Page II of the Welcome Register: the Hours, called one by one all night. One leaf left, the third. A page like that would go where there was a fire to wedge a door against: somewhere in the old city that kept one burning day and night, and kept the water warm.

[got_register_leaf_seal]
You got the LEAF OF THE SEAL.

A page with almost nothing on it but a great round seal, printed in red ink, and under it a line of tiny columns. The corner says III. One edge is scorched brown, where a furnace door was held shut on it.

[about_register_leaf_seal]
Page III of the Welcome Register: the Seal. That's all three leaves. The panel over the cleft off the Old Street is ruled with three blank columns, and these are the pages that go in them.
