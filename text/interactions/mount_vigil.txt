# MOUNT VIGIL: things you can read and look at on the spire and in its caves
# (Chapter 2, above the Long Stair). The spire rooms wrap round the mountain.

# What a save point says when you save here.
[save_point]
The crystal glows against the snow. The wind drops for a moment.
Your progress is saved.

[save_failed]
The crystal flickers, but nothing happens...
(Your browser isn't letting the game save.)

# What an emptied chest says.
[chest_empty]
An empty chest. The snow is already getting into it.

# --- Shared, across the mountain ---

[vigil_pine]
A mountain pine, leaning away from the wind. All of them lean the same way.

[vigil_snow_pine]
A pine bowed under snow. Now and then a branch lets go of its load with a soft whump.

[vigil_rock]
The spire's rock, cold and grey. Iron rings are hammered into it here and there, wherever the heralds' route goes up.

[vigil_ice_rock]
Rock veined with ice. Touch it and your fingers stick for a second.

[vigil_boulder]
A boulder that rolled this far round the mountain and no further.

[vigil_snow_boulder]
A boulder wearing a hat of snow. It looks quite pleased about it.

[vigil_bridge]
Planks slung across a gap on two ropes. They creak. The drop under them goes all the way down to the valley.

[vigil_drop]
The ledge fell away here. A long way down, the clouds are thinking about weather.

[vigil_block]
Fallen blocks of the stairs, heaped where they landed. Jump up them.

[vigil_first_snow]
A patch of snow, the first you've seen. It hasn't melted. It isn't going to.

[vigil_snow]
Snow, deep and dry and squeaking under your boots.

# --- THE RIDGE ---

[ridge_waymark]
HERO! The stairs pause here, but the mountain does not.

Round and up: the heralds' route follows the rings. Trust the rings.

Scratched underneath: "and don't look down. or do. it's quite something."

[ridge_notice]
TO ALL CLIMBERS: a ledge goes round the mountain, not across it. Keep walking and you'll come back to where you started, a little dizzy.

[ridge_cairn]
Six stones, stacked. Someone's added a seventh, smaller, on top. It wobbles when you look at it.

[ridge_chough]
A chough, glossy black with a red beak. It looks at you sideways, says "chee-ow", and goes back to looking at the view.

[ridge_gap]
The ledge gave way here once. Planks cross it now, roped at both ends by someone who knew knots.

# --- THE SPIRAL STAIR ---

[spiral_stair]
The great stair again: wide steps winding up and round the spire. Whoever built it carved the mountain to fit.

[spiral_tally]
A standing stone by the stairs. Six tally marks, deep and old. Under them, in chalk that's almost gone: "7?"

Under that, in different chalk: "8".

[spiral_sign]
LAST STAIR BEFORE HERALDS' REST (not counting the other stairs).

[spiral_gap]
A run of the stairs sheared off and went down the mountain. The heralds bridged it with planks and a prayer.

[spiral_chest]
A chest wedged on a shelf above the ledge. Getting up here took some jumping. The cloak inside is folded with care.

[spiral_waymark]
HERO! Round once more. The way goes into the mountain here; the heralds dug it when the outside got too steep.

"Mind the drips." has been added, and underlined twice.

# --- THE HOLLOW ---

[hollow_sign]
THE HOLLOW. The route goes inside from here. It is dark, it drips, and it hums. The humming is normal.

[hollow_mouth]
A cave mouth in the face of the spire, with icicles for teeth. Cold air breathes out of it.

[hollow_chest]
A chest tucked on a spur of rock, reached by one throw of the rope. There's frost on the inside of the lid.

[hollow_waymark]
HERO! Through the mountain and out on the Shoulder. Soup at the Shoulder. Keep going.

[hollow_fire]
A fire ring, long cold. Someone sat here a while, facing the cave mouth, deciding.

[hollow_pack]
A pack propped against the rock, the same make as the ones on the stairs below. Inside, wrapped in cloth: honeycake, somehow still soft.

# --- THE ICE CAVES and THE SINGING GALLERY ---

[cave_sign]
Chalked on the wall, in a herald's neat hand: "FOLLOW THE BLUE LIGHT. Crystals are friendly. Stalactites are not."

[cave_wall]
Cave rock, cold and wet. Threads of ice run through it like silver in a vein.

[cave_stalactite]
A stalactite, as long as your arm, pointing straight down at the top of your head. A drop of water gathers at its tip.

[cave_stalagmite]
A stalagmite, as tall as you, built up one drip at a time over longer than anyone's been counting.

[cave_crystals]
A cluster of ice crystals, glowing a soft blue from somewhere inside. Warm to look at. Cold to touch.

[cave_pool]
A pool frozen clear as glass. Far down under the ice, something pale and round. A stone, probably.

[cave_chest]
A heavy chest beyond the frozen pool. Inside it: mail, forged for someone the Hero's size, carried this far and set down.

[cave_pack]
A pack left against the wall. A mug inside, with a lid. Still warm? No. But it will be.

[cave_cairn]
A cairn in the middle of the passage, so nobody takes the wrong fork. Both forks go the same way, but it's the thought.

[gallery_sign]
THE SINGING GALLERY. When the wind is right, the mountain sings here. Climbers have mistaken it for the bell. The bell is louder.

[gallery_lake]
A frozen lake under the mountain. Your footsteps ring on it like a drum.

[gallery_chest]
A chest in a walled alcove, up on fallen blocks. An ice pick inside, its grip worn to the shape of a hand.

[gallery_book]
THE BOOK OF ARRIVALS (travelling copy). A list of everyone who came up the stairs, kept by the heralds.

"1. A knight. Very tall. Left at the Shoulder, said the air was thin."
"2. A scholar with a lantern. Went up further than any. Came back down alone, and very quiet."

"3. Two sisters. Only one went on above the ice. She didn't say why."
"4. A goat. We're not sure the goat counts."

"5. A singer. Sang in the Gallery for three days. The mountain sang back."
"6. A child, no older than the Hero was meant to be. Left her rope by her tent and walked back down the stairs."

"7."

[gallery_fire]
A fire ring under the mountain. The smoke went up a crack in the roof. The crack is still black with it.

[gallery_pack]
A pack by the cold fire, with a cloth bundle in it. Fudge. A hundred years is nothing to fudge.

[gallery_cairn]
A cairn built on the lake shore out of stones carried in from outside, since all the stones in here are stuck to the floor.

# --- THE SHOULDER ---

[shoulder_mouth]
The cave mouth you came out of. From here it just looks like a shadow in the rock.

[shoulder_tent]
A good tent, pitched tight, with a herald's red pennant on it. The heralds keep it for climbers. Nobody has slept in it for a very long time.

[shoulder_fire]
A fire that doesn't go out. One of the heralds comes down every few days to feed it, in case.

[shoulder_mug]
A mug of cocoa sitting on a stone by the fire, with a lid on it and a note under it: "FOR WHOEVER. drink it while it's hot."

[shoulder_mug_empty]
The mug, empty. You've left the note where it was, for the next whoever.

[shoulder_cairn]
A cairn with a trumpet carved into its top stone. The heralds' mark. From here on, the route is theirs.

[shoulder_waymark]
HERO! You are on the Shoulder. Above: the Ice Face, the Knife Edge, the Cloud Walk, and then us. Nearly there. (A hundred years of nearly there.)

[shoulder_snowman]
A snowman with a trumpet of its own. Whoever built it gave it a very determined expression.

[shoulder_chest]
A chest beyond the bridge, on the far end of the ledge. Inside, folded small, a hood lined with soft white fur.

[shoulder_sign]
ICE ABOVE. Swing high, catch late, and don't let go for anything but a ring.

# --- THE ICE FACE and THE KNIFE EDGE ---

[iceface_sign]
THE ICE FACE. Three pitches. The third one goes over the top. Don't stop at the top; there isn't one.

[iceface_chest]
A chest at the end of the high ledge, nearly buried. Mittens inside, thick and warm and useless for swords.

[knife_sign]
THE KNIFE EDGE. Named by someone who'd just climbed it. From here it's mostly walking. Mostly.

[knife_waymark]
HERO! Above the clouds now. If you can hear the bell, you're close. If you can't, you're closer than you think; it only rings at dawn.

[knife_cairn]
A cairn so tall someone must have stood on another cairn to finish it.

[knife_tally]
A stone with the tally. Six marks. Then, carved very recently and very neatly: a seventh.

You didn't carve it. Somebody up here is counting you.

# --- THE CLOUD WALK ---

[cloud_waymark]
HERO! Welcome to the Cloud Walk. Everything below you is weather. Everything above you is us.

[cloud_sign]
Someone has written "YOU'RE DOING GREAT" on this sign in paint, and then, smaller, "sorry about the ice face".

[cloud_snowman]
A snowman in a herald's tabard. The tabard is real. The snowman seems very proud of it.

[cloud_stair]
Stairs swept clear of snow, between lamps. Somebody sweeps them every morning. Somebody has swept them every morning for a hundred years.

[cloud_lamp]
An iron lamp, lit. The heralds light them at dusk and put them out at dawn, so the Hero never arrives in the dark.

[cloud_banner]
A red banner with a golden trumpet on it, snapping in the wind. The gold is a little faded. The red is not.

# --- After dark ---

[cloud_lamp_night]
A lamp, lit. Above the clouds, under the stars, its light looks very small and very brave.

# A second chest on the Cloud Walk, at the top of the stairs by the bridge.
[cloud_vane_chest]
A chest at the top of the stairs, half buried. Inside, wrapped in sacking: an iron weathercock on a short handle, the kind that might fit an umbrella.
