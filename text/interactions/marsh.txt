# THE LANTERN MARSH (Chapter 3): the fen below the Outfall, where the old
# conduit's water spreads out under the mist. Old lanterns on poles mark the
# one safe way. Where Ves came down. Things you can read and look at. See
# notes/docs/chapter-3-marsh.md.
#
# A section with `_bramble` on the end of its name is what Bramble says
# after the section of that name, if he's with you; `_ves` is what Ves says
# after that, once she's with you again (see OverworldScene.say).

# What a save point says when you save here.
[save_point]
The crystal's light goes out a long way into the mist. Further than the lanterns'.
Your progress is saved.

[save_failed]
The crystal flickers, but nothing happens...
(Your browser isn't letting the game save.)

# --- Things all over the marsh ---

[reeds]
Reeds and bulrushes, taller than you, packed so close you can't see through.

[reeds_ves]
{worried}Anything could be standing in there. Don't look. I'm not looking.

[bog]
Black mud, wet and shining. It looks like you could walk on it.

You couldn't.

[bog_bramble]
{scared}That's the kind that keeps your boots. And then the rest of you.

[fen_water]
Still, dark water. You can't see the bottom. You can't see if there is one.

[duckboards]
Old planks laid across the fen on sunk posts, grey and soft and green at the edges. Somebody laid these, a long time ago, and somebody's mended them since.

[marsh_lantern]
An old lantern hung on a crooked pole, still burning. Its glass is clean.

They go on across the fen in a line, one after another, as far as the mist lets you see.

[marsh_lantern_bramble]
{thinking}Who keeps these lit, all the way out here?

{bored}...Probably nobody. Probably they just burn.

[marsh_lantern_out]
A lantern on a pole, gone out. It's cold. The wick's burnt right down.

[marsh_lantern_out_ves]
{worried}Stay where the lit ones are. That's the rule out here. I worked that out the hard way.

[drag_print]

Then another boot print.

[drag_print_bramble]
{worried}That's her. One foot walking. One foot... not.

[drag_print_2]

[drag_print_2_bramble]
{determined}She's still going, Sword. Hurt, and she's still going.

[drag_print_3]

[reed_gap]

[chest_empty]
