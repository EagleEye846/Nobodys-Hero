# What a save point says when you save here.

The crystal rings softly, like a glass someone's run a finger round. Your progress is saved.

[save_failed]

(Your browser isn't letting the game save.)

# --- THE LANDING ---

[landing_cap]
The giant mushroom, glowing violet: the size of a house, and about as soft as a very good bed. There's a deep dent in the top of the cap, and a faint cloud of spores still going up out of it.

[landing_cap_ves]
{bored}We don't speak of the mushroom.

{smile}...It was very comfortable, though.

[landing_sign]
A hand-painted sign, nailed to a crystal: MIND THE PINK ONES. THEY BITE.

And underneath, in a smaller and much shakier hand: (sorry).

[landing_sign_ves]
{bored}Pink ones bite. Noted. {smile}Apology accepted.

[landing_bloom]
A great bloom of crystals in every colour, all growing from a single point, like the world's most expensive bouquet.

[landing_pool]
A little pool of glowing water, no deeper than a plate, full of tiny drifting lights. Your reflection in it is a bit brighter than you are.

[landing_crystal]
A crystal, humming faintly, warm to the touch and one clear colour all the way through.

[landing_trio]
Three little glowing mushrooms in a row, teal, violet and pink, standing close together like a family waiting to be let in.

[landing_small]
A small pink mushroom. It's very cute, and you don't touch it. You have a feeling about it.

[landing_pouch]
A purse wedged between the crystal spires. Somebody dropped it in a hurry, or hid it with great care. Either way, it's yours now.

[landing_rock]
The cavern roof, high overhead. You can't see where it ends.

[landing_wall]
Rock, laced with veins of crystal that pulse very slowly, as if the wall were breathing.

[landing_vein]
A fat vein of crystal running up the wall, thick as your arm, glowing from the inside.

[landing_moss_wall]
Glowing moss hanging down the rock in long strands. They sway, though there's no breeze.

[landing_spires]
Tall crystal spires, three colours, standing very straight, like they were told to wait there and say something important.

[landing_spike]
A crystal spike, sharp as a pin. The kind of thing you walk around.

[landing_mushrooms]
Small glowing mushrooms, close together, whispering.

[landing_thicket]
A thicket of spiky crystal. Nothing's getting through that.
