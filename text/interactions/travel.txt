# The first time you find one, with Ves along: she knows what it is.
[found]
A warm green updraft curls up out of the ground. It smells of grass, here of all places.

# Then Ves, with her portrait.
[found_ves]
{surprised}Oh! A green wind. Mum used to say those always find their way back to anywhere they've been.

{smile}It'll remember this place now. Next time we rest at a save point, we can ride the wind back here.

# The first time you find one, before Ves has joined.
[found_alone]
A warm green updraft curls up out of the ground. It tugs at your sleeves, as if it wants to take you somewhere.

You've nothing to catch it with. Still, it feels like it will remember you.

# Pressing Z on one later, without Ves.
[no_ves]
The green updraft tugs at your sleeves. You'd need something to catch it with.

# Pressing Z on one you've already found, with Ves.
[from_saves]
The green wind curls round you, and settles. It remembers this place. You can ride it back here from any save point.
