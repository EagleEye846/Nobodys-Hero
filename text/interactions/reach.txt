# --- THE REEF ----------------------------------------------------------------------

[reef_sign]
A plank with a tide wheel painted on it, and an arrow: TURN FOR LOW. TURN AGAIN FOR HIGH. MIND THE CUT.

[reef_wheel]
A tide wheel bolted to the rock, green with weed, its brass rings scoured bright by the rain.

[reef_wheel_turn]
You heave the wheel round. Out on the reef the sea stops, thinks about it, and starts to move.

[reef_wheel_busy]
The wheel won't budge. The water's still moving.

[reef_crate]
A sea chest, nailed shut and tarred. It floats when the reef is drowned and sits like a rock when it isn't.

[reef_crate_bramble]
{thinking}If it falls in the cut, we can walk on it. If I fall in the cut, nobody walks on me.

[reef_coral]

[reef_rock]
Wet rock and coral, full of holes that suck and bubble.

[reef_buoys]
A heap of old buoys, lashed together so the sea can't have them.

[reef_pouch]
A purse caught in the weed at the foot of the rock.

# --- THE WRECK ---------------------------------------------------------------------

[wreck_sign]
Somebody has painted on a broken oar: ALL HANDS LOST. KEEP OFF HER DECK. DON'T GO IN THE DECKHOUSE.

[wreck_sign_ves]
{bored}Two out of three's not bad.

[wreck_shingle]
Black shingle in the lee of the wreck, the only bit of this rock the sea doesn't go over.

[wreck_side]
The ship's side, leaning over you, plate after rusted plate. Barnacles have got as far as halfway up.

[wreck_rail]
The ship's rail, bent where the sea came over it.

[wreck_deck]
The deck, tilted a little, wet and slippery. Water runs off it in sheets with every wave.

[wreck_gangway]
A gangway down the ship's far side to the rock.

[wreck_house]
The deckhouse. Its door is stove in and jammed with wreckage. Nothing bigger than a cat is getting in that way.

[wreck_porthole]
A porthole, the glass cracked in a star. Inside, a wheel on the wall, and a bolt running from it out to the gangway.

[wreck_gap]
A gap low in the deckhouse wall where a plate has rusted through. It's about the size of a ten-year-old.

[wreck_gap_bramble]
{sheepish}Why is it always me and a hole?

[wreck_gap_ves]
{bored}Because it's always you and a hole.

[wreck_bolt]
A bolt wheel on the deckhouse wall. Bramble hangs off it with all his weight, and it squeals round, and out on the gangway something goes CLUNK.

[wreck_bolt_not_you]
It's inside the deckhouse, and the deckhouse door is jammed solid.

[wreck_gate]
A gate across the gangway, bolted. The bolt runs back into the deckhouse.

[wreck_gate_open]
The gangway gates swing open with a long, rusty groan.

[wreck_wheel]
The ship's wheel, three spokes snapped off. Somebody tied it hard over, a long time ago, and it's stayed that way.

[wreck_sail]
A sail on a broken spar, more hole than sail.

[wreck_flag]
A flag on the stump of a mast. Whatever was on it has been rained off.

[wreck_figurehead]
The figurehead, broken off and lying on the shingle: a woman with her hand up to her eyes, looking out to sea. She's still looking.

[wreck_anchor]
The ship's anchor, dropped on the rock and never weighed. It didn't help.

[wreck_crate]
A cargo crate, burst. Whatever was in it went with the sea.

[wreck_pouch]
A purse in the lee of the deckhouse, dry, which out here is close to a miracle.

# --- THE FOOT OF THE LAST LIGHT ------------------------------------------------------

[foot_sign]

[foot_plinth]

[foot_wall]

[foot_wall_ves]

[foot_chain]

[foot_pouch]
