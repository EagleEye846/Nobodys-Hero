# THE ICE CAVES: things you can read and look at in the caves behind the
# Weathervane Shelf (iceCaveThroat.js, iceCaveGrotto.js, iceCaveHall.js).
# Ves is with you; she doesn't speak in these.

# What a save point says when you save here.
[save_point]
The crystal's glow fills the cave, and every ice crystal in the walls answers it.
Your progress is saved.

[save_failed]
The crystal flickers, but nothing happens...

(Your browser isn't letting the game save.)

# What an emptied chest says.
[chest_empty]
An empty chest, rimed with frost on the inside.

# --- Shared ---

[ice_wall]
Cold rock, with ice running through it in veins. It's slightly see-through, if you look long enough. You'd rather not.

[ice_stalactite]
An icicle as long as you are, hanging from the roof. It drips, very slowly, onto the exact spot where you're standing.

[ice_stalagmite]
A spike of ice grown up from the floor, one drip at a time, for longer than anyone has been alive.

[ice_crystals]
A cluster of ice crystals, glowing a soft blue from the inside. Nobody knows why they glow. They hum, a little, if you put your ear to them.

[ice_floor]
Frozen solid and smooth as glass. Mind your feet.

[ice_block]
A block of stone that fell from the roof long ago. Ice has glued it to the floor.

# --- The Glittering Throat ---

[throat_sign]
THE ICE CAVES. TURN BACK. THE HERO WILL NOT NEED TO COME THIS WAY.

Underneath, scratched in later: "Turned out we did. Mind the boulders. - L."

[throat_chest]
A chest tucked in a hidden alcove, behind a wall that wasn't. Somebody hid it here on purpose.

# --- The Goblin's Grotto ---

[grotto_stall]
Crates, stacked into a counter. Prices are chalked on the side, then rubbed out, then chalked on again, higher.

[grotto_barrel]
A barrel of something that smells sweet and very strong. There's a ladle hanging on it, and a sign: NOT FOR CLIMBERS.

[grotto_lamp]
A little lamp, burning with a steady, greenish flame. Whatever it burns, it isn't oil.

[grotto_chest]
A chest in the darkest corner of the grotto. You'd never have seen it without a light.

# --- The Hall of Glass ---

[hall_sign]
THE HALL OF GLASS. Walk soft. The walls listen.

[hall_upper_chest]
A chest at the dead end of the upper hall, furred with frost. Inside, a muff of white fur, far too small for you.

[hall_chest]
Another chest behind another wall that wasn't. Somebody in these caves likes their secrets.

# --- The Roost ---

[roost_bat]
A little bat, hanging from the ice, wrapped up in its own wings. It opens one eye at you, and closes it again.

[roost_remains]
The ROOST MOTHER, frozen where she fell. Frost has filled in every fold of her wings.

[roost_pup]
One of her little ones, pressed up against her. It doesn't fly off when you come close. It doesn't want to leave her.

[roost_onward]
A tunnel climbing up out of the roof of the Roost, plugged with ice. Cold air whistles down through the cracks, from somewhere very high above.

# --- The Ice Chimney ---

[chimney_daylight]
Daylight, at the top of the shaft. Real daylight. Cold air pours down out of it, and it smells of snow.

[chimney_ice]
A waterfall that froze halfway down and never finished falling. Rings are hammered into it, a long time ago.

[chimney_chest]
A chest up on the ice blocks, out of reach of anything that can't jump. Something sweet inside.
