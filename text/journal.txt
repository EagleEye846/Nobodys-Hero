# THE JOURNAL: one or two lines for each monster's page, under [<enemy id>]
# (the id is the file name in src/data/enemies/). The page itself (name, area,
# HP, loot, and how many you've beaten or spared) is filled in by the game from
# the enemy's data. Keep each to a line or two. Nothing here should give away
# what the story hasn't told yet.

[bunny]
Every speck that was ever swept under something. It would like very much to be left in its corner.

[dummy]
Went freelance and never got over it. Gives a firm handshake. Then the stuffing.

[waxling]
Always in a hurry, and for good reason. Do not hold it near a flame.

[moth]
It will follow any light anywhere, yours included, which makes you the best thing that has happened to it all week.

[columneon]
Convinced it passes for a pillar. The pillars have never said otherwise, out of politeness.

[duskling]
What's left when somebody walks off and doesn't look back. It is not angry. It is just still waiting.

[taper]
The oldest candle in the Ruins, and the kindest thing in them. She kept the door shut for a very good reason.

[step]
Loose, mossy and patient. It has been a step for a very long time and has opinions about being stood on.

[chough]
Likes shiny things, likes watching climbers fail, and sees no conflict between the two.

[craghorn]
A goat who meant to show somebody the way up. Years of waiting made it good at climbing and bad at manners.

[frostbite]
A snowdrift with a heart of gold and arms of ice. All it wants is a hug. Mind the hug.

[glintling]
A small bright fidget of ice. Plays with the light, and with you, until one of you stops finding it funny.

[iceStep]
The same sort of step as the ones below, only colder and a little smug about it.

[ice_moth]
Drawn to glowing crystal and quite unable to think about anything else. You know someone like that.

[rimewing]
A moth that grew very large in the cold, and has been trying to get close to a light ever since.

[ringer]
Hammered in once, and holds a grudge about it. Hits hard. Gets over it quickly.

[stone_golem]
A heap of cave stone that decided to get up. In no hurry whatsoever, and neither is its ice.

[great_bat]
Every bat in the dark is one of hers. She is not cross with you. She is cross, generally, and you're nearest.

[usurper]
Wants the name more than he wants anything else. He has had a hundred years to think about it. It shows.

[sapling]
A young tree spirit with terrible timing. It only moves when you're not looking, and gets caught every time.

[russet]
A fox the colour of the leaves, playing hide and seek with the whole wood. Currently winning.

[burrhog]
Soft from the front. Sharp from everywhere else. It would like you to know that this is not its fault.

[knotling]
A cross old stump that has never forgiven the axe. Fair enough, really.

[puffcap]
Startled by everything, which makes it very good at startling other things back.

[tatterwight]
The wind from a bad night, still going round the clearing, still carrying what it picked up. It means no harm. It just doesn't know how to stop.

[gloamworm]
Fishes with a light on a string. Lonely, a bit sticky, and delighted by company.

[grist]
A rat who took over the empty mill and gave itself a job title. Very good at the job. There is no one to tell.

[hob]
Fifty years of tidying a house with nobody in it. The house has never been cleaner. The hob has never been lonelier.

[hayward]
The scarecrow of Hollin. Its people left. Nobody told it to, so it stayed, and it has kept the crows out ever since.

[siltling]
A heap of drain silt and fifty years of lost things, mostly buttons. It is very proud of the collection.

[slipjack]
The fastest thing in the drains, and it will tell you so. Loudly, and while going past.

[clinker]
A crayfish in a mug that fell down a well. A bit deaf and very cross, and has made a home of it anyway.

[bog_toad]
A big old toad who had settled in for a very long nap. It is not a morning toad.

[reed_stalker]
A heron, as still as a reed. Slow to strike, and the strike is not slow.

[wisp]
A little marsh light that copies whatever glows nearby. It means it kindly. It would just like some company.

[cairn_hound]
A dog of stacked stones that guards a city nobody comes to. Loyal, heavy, and very good at it.

[lamp_mite]
Tiny dusty moths that nest in the old street lamps. Never alone, and never, ever quiet.

[heralds_echo]
A town crier's last cry, still going. It has the news of the day. The day was a long time ago.

[warden]
A statue of a hero, carved to greet one. It cannot be talked round. It does its job, and its job is the Gate.

[the_hush]
Quiet as snow and about as heavy. It has been in the wood a very long time, and the wood has got used to it.

[bunting]
A round little bird in a string of party flags. Every party it throws is a bit slower than it looks.

[dandylion]
Terribly vain about the mane. Blows apart when it roars and puffs back together when it sulks.

[haybob]
A hay bale that got loose and has been enjoying it. Rolls a great deal. Does not discuss the mouse.

[gleamsnail]
Carries a cracked geode for a house and keeps it spotless. Slow, shiny, and a little bit smug.

[prismoth]
Wings of coloured glass, bending every light that comes near. Pretty to look at. Less pretty to be under.

[quartzling]
A small crystal creature standing among crystals and doing its best impression of one. It's a good one.

[rumbold]
Loud, bright, and very pleased with himself, and he's known the Hero longer than anyone, he says. He says it often.

[drillbug]
A beetle with a drill for a nose and a lamp on its hat. Proud of every tunnel, however pointless.

[magpiemole]
A mole with a sack of everything shiny. Pops up wherever you stand. Never quite sure why it carries so much.

[slagling]
Furnace leftovers, crusty outside and molten in the middle. Spits sparks when it's nervous, which is always.

[blindfish]
Pale, eyeless, and rather good at knowing where you are. It can hear you stop, and it takes that personally.

[fiddler]
A blind cave cricket that plays the dark like an instrument. A fine ear, a bad habit of leaping at applause.

[olm]
Long, pale and terribly polite, and a good deal older than the stair. Hates being looked at. Be considerate.

[gnawer]
Blind, old, and generous with its teeth. Hears everything, up to and including you thinking about running.

[murk]
A patch of the dark that got up and followed a light. It is frightened of it and hungry for it, which is a hard way to live.

# Chapter 5's first coast monsters (batch 1).
[shellac]
A hermit crab who has moved into a teapot and will not be moved out. House-proud, snappish, sorry about the state of the beach. Wipe your feet.

[moonjelly]
Drifts through the tidepools and the sea caves like a moon in cloud. It isn't angry. It just goes everywhere it goes, and trails things.

[limpet_knight]
A limpet in tiny armour who holds a rock against all comers. It takes this very seriously, and so should you, up to a point.

[dunlin_dancer]
Five sandpipers that behave as one very quick bird. They run in rows, peck in diagonals and drop shells from the sky. Keep in step.

[bellbuoy]
A buoy that slipped its mooring and goes on ringing for ships that aren't there. Nobody has seen who rings the bell, and the bell is keeping quiet.

[salt_shade]
A drowned sailor still on watch, waiting for a ship that was meant to come back. He means no harm. He just throws what sailors throw.

[sandcastle]
A living sandcastle, sure the tide isn't coming. Only found on sand, where it can keep its flag up. Very proud of the moat.

# Chapter 5's coast monsters, batch 2.
[wrackeel]
A teal eel wired wrong, in the half-drowned lanes. It crackles when it's startled, and it's always startled. Keep off the wet, and bring a rubber boot.

[pinwheel]
A violet sea urchin, round and cross, whose spines turn like a pinwheel when it's upset. It rolls along the shingle in a bad temper. Sit down a good way off.

[old_diver]
An empty brass diving suit that walks the wrecks as if somebody were still inside it. Throws a harpoon on a line, and means well. Salute, if you can.

[storm_imp]
A thundercloud with a face and a lightning tail, who thinks the whole storm is a game. It is very pleased with itself. So, for once, is the storm.

[net_wraith]
A fishing net that drowned with its boat and still hangs as if in water. Very sad, entirely tangled, and wants only to be hauled in. A knot undone goes a long way.

[gullion]
A scruffy brawler gull with a sack of other people's things. He steals, but he always gives it back in the end. Offer him a chip.

[kelpie_foal]
A young water-horse, all knees and kelp, practising to be frightening. It's getting there. Pretend, politely, that it already has.

[seed_sheep]
A placid sheep whose fleece is a dandelion clock. It baas, a few seeds float off, and it looks pleased. Scratch it behind the ears.

[capbee]
A bumblebee in a tweed flat cap who has worked the same clover patch for forty years. He has opinions about the new flowers. Ask him about the clover and brace yourself.

[clover_snail]
A slow green snail with a four-leaf clover for a shell, always about to fall asleep. It is not dangerous so much as drowsy. Sit down beside it, if you have the time.

[dowager_crab]
A hermit crab the size of a cart who lives in a bathtub with a ship's bell on it. She is very polite and very angry about your boots. Mind the mat.
