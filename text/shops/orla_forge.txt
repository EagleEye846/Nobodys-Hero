[talk: Who are you?]
Orla Cobb. Smith. I light the forge from the big light's oil, and the big light's lit from my forge. Don't ask which came first. It's a loop. I like it.

[talk: The Isles]
Little lights, all in a row. Every one of them was somebody's job once. Now they're mine to mind, mostly, when I remember. I don't always remember all of them.

[talk: The lamp oil]
Burns clean. Burns hot. Burns a long time. Everything I make with it keeps its edge. I don't know why. I used to ask why. Now I just pour it.

[talk: Haddock]
That walrus owes me a boathook. Tell him when you see him. Tell him I'm not cross. Tell him I'm a little cross.

[talk: Bramble]
That boy's gripping his axe like it might swim away. Good. Keep a grip. The sea doesn't give things back, as a rule.

[talk: Ves]
She asked if I do umbrellas. I don't. I do iron. But I had a canopy frame that might do, if she can stand the noise. The storm one. It sings in the wind.

[talk: The Hero]
Small, once. Came through here years back. Stood at the door looking up at the light. Didn't come in. I think about that more than I'd say. The door was open.

[talk: The old light]
The one on the shelf? Nobody's used that in years. The draught up there keeps a wick going for days, if somebody's left one. And the tea'll be the crab-pot lot. They go up to look at the weather. They'd leave their own heads up there.

[forge_ask]
Let me see that. (She takes the sword in both hands and turns it in the forge-light. Her face goes very still.)

Hm. There's a piece missing off the edge. Snapped when it fell. There's a shard of it on a snapped mast in the Wreck Yard, up high, where the ship graveyard leans against the sky. I'd know it by the hum.

Bring it. I'll put it back where it goes.

[forge_now]
That's the one. Give it here.

(Orla sets the shard on the anvil. She stops. She looks at the sword in your hand, and then at you.)

...Before I start. Take it over to the window, where the big light comes down. Hold it up in that. I want to see something.

[forge_done]
It's as good as I can make it. Somebody with a bigger fire will have to take it further. I'd like to meet them.

[forge_later]
Not yet. There's an earlier smith's work to be done first. I only finish what someone's started.

[forge_hint]
Star-metal? I've heard of it. A while back, something came down over the coast and snapped off on a mast in the Wreck Yard. On a still night I can hear it humming from here.

If you ever find a sword that hums the same note, bring both. I'd like to hear that.

[forge_shard_only]
That hums. Same as the mast does. Same note.

...I don't know what to do with a shard on its own. You'd need the rest of whatever it broke off.
