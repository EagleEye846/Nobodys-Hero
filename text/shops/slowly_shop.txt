[talk: The city]
When I was young, this city was full. Bells every morning, in case it was the day. Flags on every roof. Everyone in their best, waiting.

They waited two hundred years. Then they stopped lighting the lamps.

Then one morning they didn't ring the bells. And then, one by one, they all went somewhere else.

[talk: The Hero]
I saw the statues go up. Hundreds of them. The sculptor never knew his face, so he left them blank, every one.

He said: when the Hero comes, I'll carve the face in. He waited a long time. Longer than most.

[talk: The Gate]
The Gate's at the end of the Last Street. It's never opened. Not once in three hundred years.

There's a great statue before it, waiting with its sword up. I used to think it was the saddest thing in the city. Now I think it might be me.

[talk: Something big]
Something came through the city last night. Over the roofs. Very quiet. The birds all stopped.

It went down the Last Street to the Gate. It had something in its mouth. Something purple.
