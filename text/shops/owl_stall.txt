# HOLLIS'S STALL: Hollis, an old snowy owl, on the Last Shelf just below the
# summit. Big, round, white, with yellow eyes that have watched the mountain
# a very long time. Speaks slowly, says little, misses nothing. Trades with
# the few climbers who get this high, and doesn't ask why they've come.
# Each section is one line of the shopkeeper's, shown in the shop's text box.
# Sections starting with "talk:" become topics under TALK, in this order.

[greeting]
Hoo. Two of you. Sit, if you like. I am Hollis. This is the last stall on the mountain.

[menu_prompt]
Take your time. I have plenty.

[buy_prompt]
Food, up top. It's cold, where you're going. Then iron, and feathers.

[sell_prompt]
I'll take what you don't need. You'll want to travel light.

[talk_prompt]
Talk, then. I listen better than I talk.

[bought]
Yours. Wear it well.

[bought_item]
Eat it before you need it, not after.

[bought_skill]
Hold still. There. You'll feel it at the top.

[skill_sold_out]
That was the last one. I only ever had the one.

[sold]
Hoo. Thank you.

[nothing_to_sell]
Nothing to sell? Good. You've packed well.

[cant_sell]
You're still wearing that. Take it off first, out there.

[owned]
You have one already. One is enough.

[wont_fit]
Not for you. For the girl with the umbrella. Bring her close.

[too_poor]
Not enough. I'm sorry. Owls don't haggle.

# Bought with your bag full: it went to storage.
[bought_stored]
Your bag is full. It's gone to the storage chest. It will be at the next save crystal.

[bag_full]
Your bag is full. Eat something, or sell me something.

[farewell]
Go carefully. Hoo.

[talk: Who are you?]
Hollis. An owl. I have kept this shelf a long time, longer than the village below has had a name. Climbers come. I sell them supper.

[talk: The summit]
Up the stair. Not far now. The wind up there is too strong for wings, so I keep to the shelf, and listen to it.

[talk: An owl shop?]
Things fall up here. Iron, from the sky. Feathers. Nuts, from somewhere. I tidy them up. Somebody may as well have them.

[talk: Ves]
Hoo. She holds on to that umbrella the way I hold on to this perch. Keep close to her.
