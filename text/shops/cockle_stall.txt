[talk: Who are you?]
The Cockle Stall. That's all anyone calls me, so I've stopped minding. Third generation. My gran sold cockles to the lamplighters, my mum sold cockles to the fishers, and I sell cockles to anybody who stands still.

[talk: The cockles]
Picked at the low tide, soaked in the right water, and not a grain of sand in one. Bad cockles come from the Wrack Shore side. Never buy a cockle from anyone you wouldn't leave your boat with.

[talk: The storm]
Business has never been better, which is a terrible thing to say. Everybody wants something hot. It's the weather. People will do anything for a warm bowl.

I put a bit more pepper in. For morale.

[talk: Bramble]
He's the small one. Isn't he? He's been looking at the pot like it might come over the counter at him. It won't. It's the only thing in Saltcombe that stays where it's put. I've given him a biscuit. We don't need to talk about it.

[talk: Ves]
Has she eaten? The tall one. Dry as a board. Mind she has something. Dry people forget.

[talk: The Hero]
A little lad, in a coat that didn't fit, a hundred years back. Stood where you're standing. Had a bowl of the chowder, didn't say a word, and left the bowl clean. My gran served him. Said it was the politest bowl she ever washed.
