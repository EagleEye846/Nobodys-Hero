# PEBBLE'S STALL: Pebble, a market shrew in Belowtown, with a tray of
# toadstools and a nose that never stops. Fast-talking, nosy, loves a
# bargain and loves gossip more. She's heard everything that's ever
# happened in Belowtown, mostly twice. She sells what grows and gets baked
# down here, and the cloaks the shrews weave from moss.
# Each section is one line of the shopkeeper's, shown in the shop's text box.
# Sections starting with "talk:" become topics under TALK, in this order.

[greeting]
Oh! New faces! Surface faces, by the colour. Pebble. Pasties, honey, toffee, cloaks. Touch the toadstools and you buy them.

[menu_prompt]
Anything else? Pebble's got ears. Pebble's got a lot of ears, really, on account of the gossip.

[buy_prompt]
Hot pasties, glow-worm honey, frog toffee. Candles for the dark bits. And a cloak, woven by my aunties. They're particular.

[sell_prompt]
Selling! I buy anything. I'll find a use. Or a cousin.

[talk_prompt]
Talk? Pebble LOVES talk. Pull up a toadstool. Not that one. That one's for sale.

[bought]
Sold! Good choice. Pebble's aunties will be thrilled. They like to hear it's gone to a good home.

[bought_item]
Eat it while it's hot! Or warm. Or at all. It's good.

[bought_skill]
There! A fresh idea, sold by the pound. Pebble doesn't know how it works. Pebble doesn't ask.

[skill_sold_out]
That's the last one, dear. I've sold all the ideas. Come back after the next brainwave.

[sold]
Lovely! I'll put it out front. It'll draw a crowd. Or a cousin.

[nothing_to_sell]
Nothing to sell? Pebble is shocked. Pebble is also curious.

[cant_sell]
You're wearing that, dear. Take it off first, with C. Pebble doesn't buy things off people's backs. Aunties' rule.

[owned]
You've got one of those already. Two of a cloak is a lot of cloak. You'd rustle.

[wont_fit]
That's for your friend. Let her try it. Moss suits a sharp tongue.

[too_poor]
Oh dear, not enough. Pebble would love to, Pebble can't. Aunties' rule again. They have a lot of rules.

[bought_stored]
Bag's full! I've put it in the chest by the crystal. Pebble's tidy like that.

[bag_full]
Your bag's full and so's the chest! Eat something. Pebble recommends the pasty.

[farewell]
Off you go! Mind the steps, mind the drips, and mind the king. Not necessarily in that order.

[talk: Belowtown]
Oh, it's the best town there is, and the only one, so that helps. Everybody knows everybody, and everybody's cousin runs a stall. Pebble's got forty cousins. They're all in the market. Don't buy from them. Buy from Pebble.

[talk: The king]
King Peptup! The Resplendent! Oh, he's marvellous. He's so loud. He's never once dropped by for a pasty, which Pebble finds odd, but kings are busy. He wouldn't eat anything that's not on a plate with a crest, anyway.

[talk: The Sunstone]
It's gone dim, dearie. It used to be bright as noon down here. Now it's more of a tea-time. Everybody says it's nothing. Everybody says it in a very quiet voice.

[talk: The Hero]
Oh, that old song. The Hero came through, long ago, little thing, hardly said a word. Pebble's gran gave them a pasty. Some say they ate it. Gran said they kept it for later. Gran was always a bit sad about that.

[talk: The Sunstone after the_light_goes_up]
Did you SEE it, dearie? Bright as noon again, all at once. Pebble dropped a whole tray of pasties. Nobody's saying anything in a quiet voice now. They're all saying it very loudly.

[talk: Belowtown after the_light_goes_up]
Listen to it! Somebody's got a fiddle out, and somebody else is dancing who shouldn't be. Pebble's forty cousins have put their prices up. Don't buy from them. Buy from Pebble.

[talk: Hobnail]
The mole at the vent? Grumpy as a wet cat, and the best smith there is. Don't ask him anything. Bring him something shiny, and he'll tell you everything. In grunts.
