# RIMBLE'S STALL: Rimble, a cave goblin, in the ice caves past the
# Weathervane Shelf. Small, green, all ears, with yellow eyes that see in the
# dark. Hoards whatever the mountain drops, cleans it up, and sells it back to
# the few climbers who come through. Chatty, cheerful, and a terrible haggler.
# Each section is one line of the shopkeeper's, shown in the shop's text box.
# Sections starting with "talk:" become topics under TALK, in this order.

[greeting]
A customer! A real one! Hello, hello. Rimble. Welcome to Rimble's Stall, the only shop for a mile in any direction. Mostly down.

[menu_prompt]
What else? Rimble's got all day. Rimble's got all year, really.

[buy_prompt]
Snacks up top, all cooked by Rimble. Pokey things and coats below. Umbrella bits at the bottom.

[sell_prompt]
Ooh, selling? Rimble loves selling. Buying. You know what Rimble means.

[talk_prompt]
Talk? Nobody wants to talk to Rimble! Ask anything. Ask two things.

[bought]
It's yours! Press C out there to put it on. No refunds. Rimble doesn't know what a refund is.

[bought_item]
Enjoy! Don't ask what's in it. Rimble doesn't.

[sold]
Shiny! Into the pile. Rimble has a pile. It's a good pile.

[nothing_to_sell]
Nothing? Not a button? Not a pebble? Rimble would've bought a pebble.

[cant_sell]
You're still wearing that! Take it off out there with C first. Rimble isn't a pickpocket. Anymore.

[owned]
You've got one of those already. Rimble only ever finds one of anything.

[wont_fit]
That's for the girl with the umbrella. Too big for you, and too pretty. Bring her by.

[too_poor]
Not enough gold! Rimble is sorry. Rimble is a businessman. Rimble is mostly sorry.

# Bought with your bag full: it went to storage.
[bought_stored]
Your bag's stuffed, so Rimble sent it to the storage chest. It'll be there at the next crystal. Don't ask how. Goblin trick.

[bag_full]
Your bag's full! Eat something. Rimble recommends the jerky. Rimble always recommends the jerky.

[farewell]
Come back soon! Bring gold. Bring friends with gold. Mind the icicles.

[talk: Who are you?]
Rimble! Goblin, merchant, finder of things. Rimble lives down here where it's dark and quiet, and once in a long, long while somebody climbs past. Big day, every time.

[talk: The caves]
Cold, dark, and full of lovely things people drop. Mittens. Buttons. Whole climbers' packs. Rimble cleans them up and sells them back. Everybody wins!

[talk: The Hero?]
Everyone sat up top for a hundred years waiting for the Hero. Rimble set up down here. Figured a Hero would get hungry halfway. Lots of jerky went stale.

[talk: Things in the dark]
Some of the stones down here get up and walk, when nobody's looking. And moths, and little shiny things. Leave them be, they leave Rimble be. Mostly.

[talk: Ves]
Your friend with the umbrella! Rimble likes her. She said "please" to Rimble. Nobody says please to a goblin. The canopy's for her. Ice glass, very fancy.
