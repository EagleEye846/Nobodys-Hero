[talk: The kiln]
Charcoal for the forges up the valley, once. Nobody comes for it now. I burn it anyway. A kiln wants burning, the same as a person wants work.

[talk: The Hush]
Old as the wood, and quieter than it. It keeps to its hollow down past Hollin and leaves folk be. Always has. Something's stirred it up. Something it wants.

[talk: Hollin]
The old village, at the bottom of the wood. Bramble's grandad was born there. They left the year the Hush came out of its hollow and walked down the lane. Nobody was hurt. Nobody stayed, either.

[talk: Bramble]
His mother's the best feller in the wood and the worst at sitting still, and he's her all over. Keep him out of trouble. You'll fail, but keep him out of the worst of it.

[talk: The girl]
Dark coat, plum umbrella under her arm, a face set like she'd decided something. She drew an arrow on my tree and said, "for the one coming after." I said, who's coming after? She said, "they'll come."
