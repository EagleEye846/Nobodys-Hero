# RUMMAGE'S HOLLOW: Rummage, a raccoon, in a hollow behind a split trunk in
# the Gorge (a secret). Bandit mask, a herald's red-and-gold coat three sizes
# too big that blew down the mountain, a monocle that's really the bottom of a
# bottle. Catches everything that falls off the mountain, and everything does,
# eventually. Fast-talking, delighted, slightly sticky-fingered, honest about
# it.
# Each section is one line of the shopkeeper's, shown in the shop's text box.
# Sections starting with "talk:" become topics under TALK, in this order.

[greeting]
Ooh! Customers! Come in, come in, mind the trumpet. Welcome to Rummage's Hollow. Everything falls somewhere, and somewhere is here.

[menu_prompt]
Take your time. Touch anything. Not that. Or that. Anything else.

[buy_prompt]
Snacks and bottles up top. Down below: the good stuff. All of it fell off the mountain. All of it is very, very good.

[sell_prompt]
Selling? To ME? Oh, yes please. Rummage collects.

[talk_prompt]
Talk! Nobody finds the hollow. Nobody talks to Rummage. Ask, ask, ask.

[bought]
Yours now! Finders keepers, then payers keepers. That's the law.

[bought_item]
Enjoy! Fell off the mountain this very year. Probably.

[bought_skill]
Hold still. There. That fell off a mountain too, in a manner of speaking.

[skill_sold_out]
That was the only one. Things like that only fall once.

[sold]
Into the pile! The pile thanks you. Rummage thanks you.

[nothing_to_sell]
Nothing? Turn out your pockets. ...No? Rummage believes you. Rummage checked.

[cant_sell]
You're wearing it! Take it off out there first. Rummage doesn't do the undressing.

[owned]
You've got one of those. Even Rummage only collects one of each. Mostly.

[wont_fit]
That's for the small one with the big axe. Bring him in where he can see it.

[too_poor]
Not enough shinies! Rummage is a raccoon of business. Come back richer.

[bought_stored]
Your bag's full, so Rummage put it in the chest by the crystal. Rummage knows a way. Raccoon secret.

[bag_full]
Your bag's full! Eat something. Or sell Rummage something. Rummage prefers the second one.

[farewell]
Come back! Tell nobody! Tell everybody! No, tell nobody.

[talk: Who are you?]
Rummage! Catcher of things. Things fall off the mountain, see: hats, trumpets, sleds, lunches, whole people once or twice. They roll down the Long Drop and Rummage is at the bottom with a net.

[talk: The coat]
Came down in a gale years ago, all on its own, still buttoned up. A herald's best. Rummage waited a long time for somebody to come down after it. Nobody did. So now it's Rummage's.

[talk: The umbrella?]
A plum umbrella? Rummage saw it come down over the trees, with a girl hanging off it like washing. ...Rummage didn't catch it. Rummage was very sad. Rummage would have given it back. Probably.

[talk: The Hush]
Doesn't bother Rummage, Rummage doesn't bother it. Rummage keeps very still, and very quiet, and very far away. Recommended.

[talk: Bramble]
The small one comes by sometimes. Trades Rummage acorns for buttons. Very bad at trading. Rummage gives him extra buttons. Don't tell him.
