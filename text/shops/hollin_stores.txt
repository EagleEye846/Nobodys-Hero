# HOLLIN STORES: the village shop in Hollin, left fifty years ago when the
# Hush came up the lane. Nobody minds it now. Mags, who kept it, left notes:
# a slate on the counter and the honesty tin in front of it. Every line here
# is something written down (on the slate, in the ledger, on scraps pinned up)
# or what you find when you look.
# Each section is one line of the shop's, shown in the shop's text box.
# Sections starting with "talk:" become topics under TALK, in this order.

[greeting]
Dust, and the smell of old candles. A slate on the counter says: BACK IN FIVE MINUTES. PAY IN THE TIN. - MAGS

[menu_prompt]
The tin waits. It's very patient. It's had practice.

[buy_prompt]
Prices are chalked on the shelf edges. Everything that would go off went off long ago. What's left keeps.

[sell_prompt]
A card pinned by the tin: SWAPS WELCOME. LEAVE IT ON THE COUNTER, TAKE WHAT'S FAIR. BE HONEST.

[talk_prompt]
There's a ledger under the counter, and papers pinned up behind it.

[talk: The slate]
BACK IN FIVE MINUTES. PAY IN THE TIN. - MAGS. The chalk's gone pale. Someone has gone over the letters once, a long time after, very carefully.

[talk: The ledger]
Mags wrote down who owed what. Near the bottom: "Little Oakley, one sherbet lemon, owes a penny. (He'll pay. He's a good lad.)"

[talk: The last page]
The last entry is half a line: "Hush seen at the top of the lane. Shutting early. Leave the tin out in case anyone"
