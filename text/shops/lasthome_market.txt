# PELL'S STALL: in Lasthome's square, under the great hall's broken roof. Pell
# is a young marmot who sells what the town grows, catches and bakes (and
# Bryony's tonic, on commission). Quick, cheerful, a terrible haggler, and
# not at all interested in heroes: nobody in Lasthome is.
# Each section is one line of the shopkeeper's, shown in the shop's text box.
# Sections starting with "talk:" become topics under TALK, in this order.

[greeting]
Evening! Apples, oatcakes, pie. Mum's oatcakes are the best in the valley. Don't tell her I said so.

[menu_prompt]
What else? I'm here till the lamps go down. Then I'm still here, but I'm asleep.

[buy_prompt]
All fresh today. Well. Most of it. The pie's fresh-ish.

[sell_prompt]
Selling? Go on, let's see what you've got.

[talk_prompt]
Talk? Sure. Business is slow. Business is always slow.

[bought]
Pleasure doing business!

[bought_item]
Enjoy it! Come back and tell me if you enjoyed it.

[sold]
I'll find somebody who wants that. Probably.

[nothing_to_sell]
You've nothing I can sell on. That's all right. Neither has anybody.

[cant_sell]
Oh, I couldn't take that. You'll want that.

[owned]
You've already got one of those!

[wont_fit]
That one's for your friend, not you. Bring her by and she can try it on.

[too_poor]
Short a few coins. I'd let you off, but Mum counts the till.

[bought_stored]
Your bag's full, so I've sent it to your storage. Don't ask me how. Nobody knows how.

[bag_full]
No room in your bag, and none in storage either! Eat something!

[farewell]
Mind how you go! Mind the bucket by the well. Everyone trips on it once.

[talk: Lasthome]
It's the old city's hall. The biggest building there ever was, my granny says. We just live in it.

Somebody built it for something important. We don't know what. We use it for keeping the rain off.

[talk: The Hero?]
The what? Oh, the Hero. The one the old city was waiting for.

Nobody's waited for him here in a long time. My granny's granny stopped. She said you can't keep a whole town on hold for a stranger.

[talk: The far door]
That's the way out into the rest of the city. The dead part. Nobody goes out there much.

There's things in the old streets. And it's all falling down. And there's the Gate, right at the far end.

[talk: The north ridge]
Thistle put a notice up. The birds on the north ridge went quiet, two days ago.

Birds going quiet's a bad sign, Mum says. She won't say what of.
