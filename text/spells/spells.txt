# Spell descriptions, shown on one line under the SPELLS list in battle for
# the spell the soul is on. The list shows each spell's SP cost by itself.
# One section per spell id in src/data/spells.js; a party member's spells are
# <member id>_<label> (their `commands` in src/characters/party.js).
# {amount} (and Ves's {def}, {calm}, {damage}, {heal}, {sp}) is the number it
# will do right now, after your armor's MAG, your skills and their gear.
# Keep each to one short line.

[heal]
A warm pulse of light. Heals {amount} HP.

[pacify]
Spares one enemy, but only once its MERCY is full.

[rend]
A blade of light that never misses: {amount} damage to one enemy.

[lull]
A soft hush. Raises one enemy's MERCY by {amount}%.

[starfall]
Light pours down on one enemy for {amount} damage.

[mend]
A quick patch-up. Heals {amount} HP.

[ember]
A ring of fire: {amount} damage to every enemy.

[ward]
A shell of light. Halves the damage you take for {amount} enemy turns.

[hush]
Snow falls indoors. Every enemy's MERCY rises by {amount}%.

[comet]
The sky itself comes down on one enemy for {amount} damage.

[spark]
A quick bolt of light: {amount} damage to one enemy.

[siphon]
{amount} damage to one enemy, and half of it comes back to you as HP.

[chain]
Lightning leaps through every enemy: {amount} damage to each.

[nova]
Everything goes white: {amount} damage to one enemy.
