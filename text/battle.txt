# Lines the battle screen says by itself (not any one enemy's).

# The very first time you fight: your shadow stands up to take the hits.
# Shown once, before the enemy's own intro line.
[first_shadow]
Danger! Your shadow peels up off the floor and stands between you and the enemy.
It doesn't look like you. It looks like a Hero.

# Running away (RUN), and getting away: a line about where you are. The
# section is the area the fight is in (see battleArea in overworldScene.js):
# ruins, stair (the green lower mountain), vigil (the snowy heights), cave
# (any cave), rest (Heralds' Rest). One is picked at random.
[escape: ruins]
You slipped away into the ruins!

You ducked behind a pillar and kept going!

[escape: stair]
You slipped away behind the rocks!

You ducked into the long grass and didn't look back!

You scrambled off down the path and out of sight!

[escape: vigil]
You slipped away into the snow!

You ducked behind a drift and kept low until it gave up!

The wind threw snow between you, and you were gone!

[escape: cave]
You slipped away into the dark!

You ducked behind a stalagmite and held your breath!

You squeezed into a crack in the ice until it went away!

[escape: rest]
You slipped away down a side street!

You ducked behind a snowman. It worked!

# --- VES, on her own turn (see ALLY_COMMANDS in src/battle/battleScene.js) ---

# Shown in the panel while she picks what to do. One is picked at random.
# Keep each to a line or two: it isn't split into pages.
[vesper_turn]
Ves shakes the snow off her umbrella. Her turn.

Ves taps the brass handle twice, for luck. Her turn.

Ves plants her feet. "Right. My go."

Ves peers out from under her umbrella. Her turn.

# Her TALK: a kind word to one enemy, which raises its MERCY a little.
# {foe} is the enemy's name. One is picked at random. An enemy's own text
# file can have a [vesper_talk] section of its own instead.
[vesper_talk]
Ves tells {foe} it's doing very well, considering.

Ves asks {foe} if it's eaten. It hasn't thought about it in years.

Ves holds her umbrella over {foe} for a moment. Just to keep the snow off.

Ves says sorry to {foe}, for everything. It isn't sure what for. Neither is she.

Ves hums a few bars of an old village song at {foe}.

# Her DEFEND: the plain version of SHELTER.
[vesper_defend]
Ves plants her umbrella in front of you like a little wall.
