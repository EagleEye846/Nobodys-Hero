# The very first time you fight: your shadow stands up to take the hits.
# Shown once, before the enemy's own intro line.
[first_shadow]
Danger! Your shadow peels up off the floor and stands between you and the enemy.
It doesn't look like you. It looks like a Hero.

# --- VES, on her own turn (see ALLY_COMMANDS in src/battle/battleScene.js) ---

# Shown in the panel while she picks what to do. One is picked at random.
# Keep each to a line or two: it isn't split into pages.
[vesper_turn]
Ves shakes the snow off her umbrella. Her turn.

Ves taps the brass handle twice, for luck. Her turn.

Ves plants her feet. "Right. My go."

Ves peers out from under her umbrella. Her turn.

# Her TALK: a kind word to one enemy, which raises its MERCY a little.
# {foe} is the enemy's name. One is picked at random. An enemy's own text
# file can have a [vesper_talk] section of its own instead.
[vesper_talk]
Ves tells {foe} it's doing very well, considering.

Ves asks {foe} if it's eaten. It hasn't thought about it in years.

Ves holds her umbrella over {foe} for a moment. Just to keep the snow off.

Ves says sorry to {foe}, for everything. It isn't sure what for. Neither is she.

Ves hums a few bars of an old village song at {foe}.

# Her DEFEND: the plain version of SHELTER.
[vesper_defend]
Ves plants her umbrella and ducks behind it like a little wall.

# --- VES, HURT (after the Lantern Marsh: her leg's bad, her umbrella's lost) ---
# While she's hurt these take the place of the ones above. She sits on the
# ground to fight.
[vesper_hurt_turn]
Ves shifts her bad leg and winces. "I'm fine. My turn."

Ves pushes her hair out of her eyes. Her turn.

Ves holds her coat tight round her. "Right. What can I do?"

[vesper_hurt_talk]
Ves tells {foe} it's doing very well, considering.

Ves asks {foe} if it's eaten. It hasn't thought about it in years.

Ves asks {foe}, very politely, to leave her leg alone.

Ves hums a few bars of an old village song at {foe}.

[vesper_hurt_defend]
Ves ducks down behind you and holds on.

# Her CALL OUT: what she shouts as the next attack starts, in the panel
# under the box. {foe} is the name of the enemy attacking. Keep each to a line.
[vesper_call_out]
Ves shouts: "{foe}'s winding up! Get ready!"

Ves shouts: "Here it comes! Watch it, watch it!"

Ves shouts: "{foe}! Now! Move!"

# --- BRAMBLE, on his own turn (see ALLY_COMMANDS in src/battle/battleScene.js) ---

# Shown in the panel while he picks what to do. One is picked at random.
[bramble_turn]
Bramble spits on his hands like the grown-ups do. His turn.

Bramble hefts his hatchet. It hefts him back a bit. His turn.

Bramble bounces on his toes. "Me! Me! My go!"

Bramble wipes his nose on his sleeve. His turn.

Bramble squints at the enemy like Ma does at logs. His turn.

# His TALK: a word to one enemy, which raises its MERCY a little. {foe} is
# the enemy's name. An enemy's own text file can have [bramble_talk] instead.
[bramble_talk]
Bramble tells {foe} it's not allowed in this bit of the wood. It looks confused.

Bramble asks {foe} if it wants to see a frog he found. It sort of does.

Bramble tells {foe} his Ma could beat it up. {foe} believes him.

Bramble offers {foe} half a biscuit. It's the smaller half, but still.

Bramble pulls a face at {foe}. It's a really good face.

# His DEFEND.
[bramble_defend]
Bramble gets behind his hatchet and makes himself very small.
