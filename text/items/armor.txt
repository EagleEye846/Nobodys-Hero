# Armor descriptions for the EQUIP menu (C in the overworld) and shops.
# One section per armor id in src/data/armor.js. The menus show DEF, MAG and
# SP by themselves, so these are just flavour.

[travel_clothes]
The clothes you wandered in wearing. Comfortable, and no use at all in a fight.

[padded_vest]
Quilted cotton, a little lumpy. Takes the edge off a hit.

[moth_silk_shawl]
Woven from the silk of Lantern Moth cocoons. Spells come more easily wrapped in it.

[waxed_coat]
Leather hardened in candle wax. Stiff, warm, and surprisingly tough.

[candlewick_robe]
Threaded through with old wicks. It hums with magic, and catches every blow.

[chain_shirt]
Heavy rings of old iron. Hard to hurt you in it, and hard to think straight.

[climbers_jacket]
Patched and padded, made for long climbs. One of the six who came before must have left it.

# Mount Vigil and Heralds' Rest.
[herald_cloak]
A herald's red cloak, trimmed in gold thread. Warm, and it makes you feel announced.

[fur_lined_hood]
A hood lined with snow-hare fur (shed, not taken, Marta insists). The cold stays out and your thoughts stay in.

[ringers_mittens]
Thick knitted mittens, the kind the bell-ringer wears. Hard to hold a sword in; easy to hold a spell.

[vigil_mail]
Mail forged for the Hero and carried most of the way up the mountain. It weighs as much as a promise. It's hard to think in it.

[snow_mantle]
A white mantle quilted from a hundred winters' worth of patience. Sold at Rest Stores to anyone who makes it that far.

[smiths_apron]
A heavy leather apron, scorched in a hundred places. Tilda had a spare. Tilda has a spare of everything.

[riveted_gambeson]
A quilted coat with iron plates riveted inside. Warm, solid, and a little hard to think in.

# Sold by Rimble, the goblin in the ice caves.
[crystal_cuirass]
A breastplate of clear ice crystal, grown rather than forged. It's lighter than it looks, and warm, somehow.

[aurora_scarf]
A long scarf that shimmers green and violet when it moves, like the sky on the coldest nights. It's made for Ves.

[rimemoth_shawl]
A shawl woven from the frosty silk ice moths leave on the cave walls. It glitters when you cast.

[patchwork_parka]
A huge parka, patched by Rimble with forty-one other coats. Warm, thick, and somehow always a little bit too long.

# Ves's coats, scarves and mittens. Only she can wear them (see `wielder` in
# src/data/armor.js). Enemies never aim at her, so they look after you.
[evening_coat]
Her evening-blue coat and the cream scarf with the coral stripe. Mended at both elbows. Good for standing in front of people.

[ember_knit_scarf]
A long scarf knitted in lamp-soot grey and ember orange. Wrapped round twice, it keeps whoever's nearest warm. That's usually you.

[goosedown_mittens]
Big puffy mittens. Ves claps them every time you graze a bullet, which shouldn't help as much as it does.

[oilskin_greatcoat]
A herald's oilskin, made for keeping watch on the Lookout in all weathers. Heavy as a door, and about as good at being stood behind.

[spark_proof_cape]
A short cape of felted wool, the kind Tilda wears at the forge. Sparks bounce off it. So do snowballs, and so, Ves hopes, will everything else.

[gale_ribbon_scarf]
A long scarf of coral ribbon that streams out in the slightest wind. Ves says it tells her which way to dodge. It mostly tells her it's windy.

[cragwool_poncho]
A heavy poncho of grey mountain-goat wool, cut wide enough to hide two people behind. That's the idea.

[icefox_muff]
A muff of soft white fur, still warm from somewhere. Ves keeps one hand in it and the other on your sleeve.

[cavers_hood]
A stiff leather hood with a brim, the kind cavers wear so nothing can drop on their heads. Ves wears it with the brim pulled very low.

[owl_down_coat]
A long coat quilted with owl down, the warmest thing you've ever worn. Spells seem to come easier with your hands warm.

[snowfeather_shawl]
A shawl of snowy feathers, soft as a cloud and heavier than it looks. Ves wraps it round you both when the wind gets up.

[rimeweave_cloak]
A cloak woven from frost-silk and lined with fleece. It sheds snow, ice and bad news, and it's warm enough to share.

# Chapter 3, below the clouds.
[woodcutters_flannel]
A thick red-and-black flannel shirt, patched at both elbows. It smells of woodsmoke and it's warm all the way through.

[fallen_leaf_cloak]
A cloak of scarlet leaves pressed and stitched together. You can disappear into the forest floor in it. Spells come easily in the quiet.

[barkplate]
Plates of seasoned bark laced over a leather shirt. It stops nearly anything. It also creaks when you think.

[reedwoven_coat]
A coat of woven marsh reeds, light and dry inside however wet it gets out. Your spells carry further in it, the way sound does over water.

[eelskin_waders]
Waders of tough, slippery eelskin, all the way up to the chest. Things slide off you, and you slide out of their way.

[mosaic_mail]
Mail made of the old city's mosaic tiles, each one sewn on, a picture you can't see while you're wearing it.

[woodsmoke_shawl]
A grey shawl that's sat by the woodcutters' fire for years. It still holds the warmth. Ves wraps herself in it and won't give it back.

[heron_down_coat]
A long coat quilted with heron down, waterproof and warm. When Ves holds it over you, the rain doesn't get through.

[mended_coat]
Ves's own evening coat, the one the beast tore, mended with careful stitches. The patches don't match. She wouldn't have it any other way.

[lamp_soot_shawl]
A lamplighter's shawl, left on a hook by a dead lamp. It still smells of smoke and oil. Warm all the way through.

[herald_banner_cloak]
A herald's banner, cut and hemmed into a cloak. Held up over you, it's as good as a wall.

[criers_mittens]
The town crier's mittens, red wool with the fingers worn thin. Good for grabbing things out of the air.

# The Hushwood.
[lookouts_oilskin]
A long oilskin coat left up the old lookout tree, still dry inside after all these years. It's watched a lot of weather.

[herald_greatcoat]
A herald's red-and-gold greatcoat that came down the mountain on the wind, according to Rummage. Nobody's worn it since. It's never been needed.

[hand_me_down_flannel]
A red flannel shirt that belonged to three cousins first. It comes down past Bramble's knees. He says it's a coat.

[kiln_jerkin]
A leather jerkin, baked hard by Nan Cinder's kiln and soft on the inside. It smells of charcoal. Bramble says it smells of winning.

[acorn_cap]
A woolly cap the colour of an acorn, with a stalk on top. Bramble wore it to his den every day last summer. It's lucky. It's been proven.

[mas_scarf]
Ma's red scarf, found at the trap. Bramble won't say anything about it. He just wears it, tight.

[oilcloth_poncho]
A poncho cut from an old oilcloth tent. It crackles when Bramble moves, which he says makes him sound bigger.

# Teasel's forge, in Lasthome's square.
[bell_bronze_breastplate]
A breastplate hammered out of bell bronze. It takes a great deal of hitting. It weighs on your spells a little. It weighs on everything a little.

[smiths_leathers]
Teasel's own leathers, the spare set, scorched down the front. Not much gets through them in a hurry, and you can still move in them.

[tin_lined_cape]
A short cape lined with hammered tin, against sparks. Ves says it crinkles. It does. It also stops a great deal.
