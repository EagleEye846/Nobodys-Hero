# Item descriptions for the ITEMS menu (C in the overworld) and shops.
# One section per item id in src/data/items.js. The menus show how much it
# heals by themselves, so these are just flavour.

[trail_mix]
Nuts, seeds and the odd raisin. Good for long walks through old halls.

[lantern_tea]
Tea brewed over a lantern flame. It tastes faintly of smoke and honey.

[ember_toffee]
Toffee cooked on an ember for a hundred years. Chewy enough to last another hundred.

[cotton_candy]
A tuft of the Rogue Dummy's stuffing, spun into candy. Surprisingly sweet.

[wild_carrot]
A Dust Bunny's prized carrot. Slightly dusty.

[glow_jam]
Jam that glows faintly in the dark. The Lantern Moth was saving it.

[wax_drop]
A drop of a Waxling's wax, set like a sweet. It tastes like candles. Of course it does.

[dark_chocolate]
Chocolate as dark as a Shade. It's gone if you look away.

[pillar_mint]
A Columneon's breath mint, shaped like a tiny pillar. Very convincing.

[candle_cake]
A cake shaped like a candle, from Taper's kitchen. "FOR OUR GUEST."

[taper_stub]
The candle Taper gave you. It's still warm. It always will be.
