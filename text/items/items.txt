# Item descriptions for the ITEMS menu (C in the overworld) and shops.
# One section per item id in src/data/items.js. The menus show how much it
# heals by themselves, so these are just flavour.

[trail_mix]
Nuts, seeds and the odd raisin. Good for long walks through old halls.

[lantern_tea]
Tea brewed over a lantern flame. It tastes faintly of smoke and honey.

[ember_toffee]
Toffee cooked on an ember for a hundred years. Chewy enough to last another hundred.

[cotton_candy]
A tuft of the Rogue Dummy's stuffing, spun into candy. Surprisingly sweet.

[wild_carrot]
A Dust Bunny's prized carrot. Slightly dusty.

[glow_jam]
Jam that glows faintly in the dark. The Lantern Moth was saving it.

[wax_drop]
A drop of a Waxling's wax, set like a sweet. It tastes like candles. Of course it does.

[dark_chocolate]
Chocolate as dark as a Duskling. It's gone if you look away.

[pillar_mint]
A Columneon's breath mint, shaped like a tiny pillar. Very convincing.

[step_shortbread]
Crumbly shortbread pressed into the shape of a stair step. A Loose Step gave it up, a little grudgingly.

[crag_cheese]
Sharp goat's cheese wrapped in a dock leaf, aged on a mountain for longer than you'd think.

[shiny_button]
A butterscotch sweet pressed in the shape of a brass button, polished to a shine. The Chough insisted you have it. It was laughing.

[snow_cone]
A cone of a Frostbite's very best snow, packed tight, with frostberry syrup on top. It gave you a little of itself. That's practically a hug.

[filings_toffee]
Dark treacle toffee with something grey flecked through it. Surprisingly chewy. The Ringer swears it's good for the joints.

[rime_jam]
Pale jam that glitters like frost on a window. A Rimewing was keeping it by a lamp, to stay warm.

[ice_shortbread]
Shortbread in the shape of a stair step, iced in pale blue. Its brother's recipe, but colder.

[geode_candy]
A lump of plain grey sugar that cracks open to show blue crystal candy inside. The Stone Golem grew it. Slowly.

[frost_nectar]
A thimble of nectar from the flowers that grow by the cave crystals, frozen into a pale blue bead. Ice Moths sip it by the glow.

[glint_drop]
A clear boiled sweet that throws little rainbows when you hold it up. A Glintling swapped it for nothing at all.

[roost_plum]
A plum from the Roost Mother's winter store, frozen sweet as jam. Her pup brought it to Ves, after. Ves couldn't keep it.

[roast_chestnut]
A chestnut out of a Burrhog's prickles, roasted in its own husk. It rolled it to you. Gently, for once.

[scarlet_syrup]
A twist of red sap from a Sapling, sweet as maple. It gave it to you when you weren't looking.

[stolen_pie]
A berry pie with one bite out of it and a paw print in the crust. The woodcutters have been missing this.

[stump_honey]
Honey from the bees in a Knotling's hollow. It calls them squatters. It has never once asked them to leave.

[hollin_apple]
An apple off the old tree Hayward still guards. The farmers never came back for it, so it's yours.

[gloam_honeydew]
A drop of the sweet dew a Gloamworm hangs its threads with. It glows a little on the way down.

[fifty_year_humbug]
A striped mint humbug in waxed paper, from the bottom of the well. Fifty years old, and somehow still good.

[hob_scone]
Still warm, though nobody's lit an oven in Hollin for fifty years. A Hob bakes one every morning, just in case.

[millers_bannock]
A flat oat loaf from the old mill, nibbled round the edges. Grist swears it was like that when it found it.

[mug_barley_sugar]
A barley sugar that was stuck to the bottom of a Clinker's mug. Fifty years old, and still sweet. Mostly.

[culvert_cress]
Watercress from under a grating, where a little light gets down. A Slipjack keeps the bed of it to show off in. It's peppery.

[wisp_cordial]
A thimble of something pale green that a Wisp left behind. It glows a little in the dark, and tastes of cold mint.

[bog_pudding]
A dark, wobbly pudding a Bog Toad was sitting on to keep warm. Sort of plum. Don't ask what else.

[bulrush_bread]
Flat bread made from bulrush roots, the old fen way. A Reed Stalker kept it under its foot, very still, for hours.

[toadstool_toast]
A slice of toast with a Puffcap's spare cap on top, still warm. It insists the spotty ones are fine. Mostly.

[dust_meringue]
A meringue so light it's mostly air, found in a nest of Lamp Mites. Dusty on the outside. Sweet as anything inside.

[cairn_biscuit]
A big, hard biscuit a Cairn Hound was keeping safe under a stone. It's a dog biscuit, really. It's very good anyway.

[welcome_cake]
A slice of the cake the old city baked to welcome the Hero. Nobody came to eat it, so a Herald's Echo kept it. Still fresh, somehow.

[heros_offering]
A honey cake sealed in wax, left in the Warden's open hand three hundred years ago, for the Hero. Nobody else came for it.

[candle_cake]
A cake shaped like a candle, from Taper's kitchen. "FOR OUR GUEST."

[taper_stub]
The candle Taper gave you. It's still warm. It always will be.

# --- Candles you make (CANDLES, in the C menu). In battle, lighting one does
# what the menu says; in dark places, the brightest one you carry lights your way.

[plain_candle]
A plain wax candle on a cotton wick. Nothing special, which is sometimes exactly what you need.

[hearth_candle]
A thick, slow candle that smells like a warm kitchen. Lighting it feels like coming home.

[moonbell_candle]
Wax pressed with moonbell petals. Its scent is so gentle that even angry things calm down a little.

[glow_candle]
Glow dust stirred into the wax. It burns bright and long, and makes you feel braver.

[ash_candle]
Shadow ash in dark wax. It gives off more smoke than light, and the smoke hides you well.

[lamplight_candle]
Lit from the lamplighter's lantern on the summit. Shadows lean away from it.

[hush_fur]
A hank of the Hush's bristles, caught on your sleeve. Coarse and black, and very, very quiet. Held up, it hides you like a shadow.

[climbers_ration]
Hard biscuit, dried fruit and a square of chocolate, sealed in waxed paper. Made for a long way up.

# Mount Vigil and Heralds' Rest.
[pine_candy]
Boiled sweets that taste of pine needles and sugar. The heralds make them for the kids. And the kids make them for the heralds.

[snowmelt_tea]
Tea brewed from snow melted over a fire. It tastes of nothing at all, and warms you right down to your boots.

[frostberry_jam]
Frostberries only ripen after the first hard frost. The jam is sharp, red and a little bit sparkly.

[hot_cocoa]
A mug of cocoa, still steaming somehow. There's a marshmallow in it shaped like a trumpet.

[herald_honeycake]
Dense, sticky honeycake, baked for the Hero's welcome and eaten by everyone else. A herald family's recipe. They've had time to perfect it.

[fanfare_fudge]
Fudge so rich the heralds only cut it for special occasions. You, apparently, are one.

[rest_soup]
Greta's soup, in a jar with a cloth over the top. Whatever is in it, it fixes everything.

[mulled_cider]
Apple cider heated with a hot poker straight out of the coals. It hisses when she does it. So will you, if you drink it too fast.

[smiths_pasty]
A fat pasty with a crimped edge, made to be eaten one-handed while the other hand holds a hammer.

[iron_pot_stew]
Root stew that has been simmering in the same iron pot since before Tilda was born. Nobody has ever got to the bottom of it.

Chewy, smoky, and salty. Rimble won't say what it is. "Meat," he says. "From an animal. Probably."

[glowcap_skewer]
Glowing cave mushrooms roasted on a stick. They keep glowing after you eat them, which is fine. Rimble says it's fine.

[seed_cake]
A dense round cake of seeds, nuts and frozen berries, pressed together by Hollis. It keeps forever. So does the owl.

[goblin_hotpot]
A hot pot of everything Rimble found that week, stewed for days. It's lumpy, it's brown, and it's delicious.

A bun baked in the ashes of a charcoal kiln. Black on the outside, which Bramble says is the best bit, and soft and sweet in the middle.

[ember_potato]
A potato baked in the embers till its skin cracks. Nan Cinder salts it with something she won't name.

[charcoal_toffee]
Dark toffee, smoky and very hard. Nan Cinder says it's good for your teeth. Nobody has ever checked.

[kiln_stew]
A whole pot of stew from the back of the kiln, in a jar with a cloth over the top. It's been cooking for two days.

[dandelion_salad]

[valley_apple]

[eel_pie]

[penny_bun]

[hob_porridge]
