# Item descriptions for the ITEMS menu (C in the overworld) and shops.
# One section per item id in src/data/items.js. The menus show how much it
# heals by themselves, so these are just flavour.

[trail_mix]
Nuts, seeds and the odd raisin. Good for long walks through old halls.

[lantern_tea]
Tea brewed over a lantern flame. It tastes faintly of smoke and honey.

[ember_toffee]
Toffee cooked on an ember for a hundred years. Chewy enough to last another hundred.

[cotton_candy]
A tuft of the Rogue Dummy's stuffing, spun into candy. Surprisingly sweet.

[wild_carrot]
A Dust Bunny's prized carrot. Slightly dusty.

[glow_jam]
Jam that glows faintly in the dark. The Lantern Moth was saving it.

[wax_drop]
A drop of a Waxling's wax, set like a sweet. It tastes like candles. Of course it does.

[dark_chocolate]
Chocolate as dark as a Shade. It's gone if you look away.

[pillar_mint]
A Columneon's breath mint, shaped like a tiny pillar. Very convincing.

[step_shortbread]
Crumbly shortbread pressed into the shape of a stair step. A Loose Step gave it up, a little grudgingly.

[crag_cheese]
Sharp goat's cheese wrapped in a dock leaf, aged on a mountain for longer than you'd think.

[candle_cake]
A cake shaped like a candle, from Taper's kitchen. "FOR OUR GUEST."

[climbers_ration]
Hard biscuit, dried fruit and a strip of salted something, sealed in waxed paper. Made to last a long climb.

[taper_stub]
The candle Taper gave you. It's still warm. It always will be.

# --- Candles you make (CANDLES, in the C menu). In battle, lighting one does
# what the menu says; in dark places, the brightest one you carry lights your way.

[plain_candle]
A plain wax candle on a cotton wick. Nothing special, which is sometimes exactly what you need.

[hearth_candle]
A thick, slow candle that smells like a warm kitchen. Lighting it feels like coming home.

[moonbell_candle]
Wax pressed with moonbell petals. Its scent is so gentle that even angry things calm down a little.

[glow_candle]
Glow dust stirred into the wax. It burns bright and long, and makes you feel braver.

[ash_candle]
Shadow ash in dark wax. It gives off more smoke than light, and the smoke hides you well.

[climbers_ration]
Hard biscuit, dried fruit and a square of chocolate, sealed in waxed paper. Made for a long way up.

# Mount Vigil and Heralds' Rest.
[pine_candy]
Boiled sweets that taste of pine needles and sugar. The heralds make them for the kids. And the kids make them for the heralds.

[snowmelt_tea]
Tea brewed from snow melted over a fire. It tastes of nothing at all, and warms you right down to your boots.

[frostberry_jam]
Frostberries only ripen after the first hard frost. The jam is sharp, red and a little bit sparkly.

[hot_cocoa]
A mug of cocoa, still steaming somehow. There's a marshmallow in it shaped like a trumpet.

[herald_honeycake]
Dense, sticky honeycake, baked for the Hero's welcome and eaten by everyone else. A herald family's recipe. They've had time to perfect it.

[fanfare_fudge]
Fudge so rich the heralds only cut it for special occasions. You, apparently, are one.

[rest_soup]
Greta's soup, in a jar with a cloth over the top. Whatever is in it, it fixes everything.
