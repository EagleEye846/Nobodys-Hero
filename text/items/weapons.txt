# Weapon descriptions for the EQUIP menu (C in the overworld) and shops.
# One section per weapon id in src/data/weapons.js. The menus show FIGHT power
# and the SHIFT ability automatically, so these are just flavour and how it works.

[worn_sword]
A dependable old blade. No tricks, full damage.

[old_shield]
Dented, but sturdy.
Raise a barrier around your SHADOW that destroys bullets. Takes a few seconds to recharge.

[duelist_blade]
Thin, quick and very sharp.
A fast slash around your SHADOW. Parry a bullet just before it hits and it recharges instantly.

[ash_spear]
Light enough to throw yourself after.
Dash the way you're moving. Nothing can hurt you mid-dash.

[hunting_bow]
Strung with something that hums.
Fire arrows straight up that break the first bullet they hit. Three arrows per turn.

[oak_staff]
Warm to the touch, even here.
Hold SHIFT to stand still and channel. Grazing builds SP three times as fast.

[stone_hammer]
Mostly rock. Partly handle.
Slam the ground to clear the bullets closest to you. Slow to recharge.

[spare_lantern]
The Keeper says it was never needed. Until now.
Leave a pool of light. Bullets inside it slow to a crawl.

[glass_dagger]
You can see yourself in it, slightly to the left.
Blink to the opposite side of the box.

[iron_gauntlets]
Heavy, cold, and eager.
Punch the nearest bullet back at the enemy for damage. It has to be close!

[chapel_bell]
One clear note, and everything listens.
Ring it to freeze every bullet for a moment.

[old_tome]
The pages remember what you forgot.
Spend 20% SP to heal 12 HP, right in the middle of an attack.

[ice_pick]
A climber's pick with a worn leather grip, found in the caves inside the spire. It bites deep, and it's made for lunging.

[herald_trumpet]
A herald's brass trumpet, polished for a hundred years. One blast and everything stops to listen (Toll).
