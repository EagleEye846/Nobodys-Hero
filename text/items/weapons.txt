# Weapon descriptions for the EQUIP menu (C in the overworld) and shops.
# One section per weapon id in src/data/weapons.js. The menus show FIGHT power
# and the SHIFT ability automatically, so these are just flavour and how it works.

[worn_sword]
A dependable old blade. No tricks, full damage.

[old_shield]
Dented, but sturdy.
Raise a barrier around your SHADOW that destroys bullets. Takes a few seconds to recharge.

[duelist_blade]
Thin, quick and very sharp.
A fast slash around your SHADOW. Parry a bullet just before it hits and it recharges instantly.

[ash_spear]
Made of ash wood, this spear is known to dash through attacks like a phantom. Some say you can hear the screams of its past wielders wailing from it.
Dash the way you're moving. Nothing can hurt you mid-dash.

[hunting_bow]
Strung with something that hums.
Fire arrows straight up that break the first bullet they hit. Three arrows per turn.

[oak_staff]
Warm to the touch, even here.
Hold SHIFT to stand still and channel. Grazing builds SP three times as fast.

[stone_hammer]
Mostly rock. Partly handle.
Slam the ground to clear the bullets closest to you. Slow to recharge.

[spare_lantern]
The Keeper says it was never needed. Until now.
Leave a pool of light. Bullets inside it slow to a crawl.

[glass_dagger]
You can see yourself in it, slightly to the left.
Blink to the opposite side of the box.

[iron_gauntlets]
Heavy, cold, and eager.
Punch the nearest bullet back at the enemy for damage. It has to be close!

[chapel_bell]
One clear note, and everything listens.
Ring it to freeze every bullet for a moment.

[old_tome]
The pages remember what you forgot.
Spend 20% SP to heal 12 HP, right in the middle of an attack.

# Mount Vigil and Heralds' Rest.
[ice_pick]
A climber's pick with a worn leather grip, found in the caves inside the spire. It bites deep, and it's made for lunging.

[herald_trumpet]
A herald's brass trumpet, polished for a hundred years. One blast and everything stops to listen (Toll).

# Sold by Rimble, the goblin in the ice caves.
[glintstone_sling]
A leather sling and a pouch of pebbles that glitter in the dark. Rimble picked them up somewhere he won't say. Fire them straight up (Shoot).

[coldlight_lantern]
A lantern with an ice crystal in it instead of a flame. It glows a cold, steady blue, and never goes out. Hold it up to slow the bullets in its light (Light).

[golem_knuckle]
A fist of cave stone, hollowed out to fit a hand. It still twitches sometimes. Slam the ground with it to clear the bullets around you (Quake).

# Ves's umbrella parts. Only she can use them (see `wielder` in
# src/data/weapons.js); the menus show her stats by themselves.
[lamplighters_handle]
The umbrella that came down on the wind: a brass hook worn smooth by her mother's hand, and the patched plum canopy. Ves won't hear a word against either.

[bellwire_ribs]
Umbrella ribs restrung with old wire from the belltower. They ring when she swings, and the sleet comes off them meaning it. Snow gets in the way.

[hearthwood_crook]
A handle carved from an offcut of the inn's hearth beam. Greta swears it's still warm. The wind it calls certainly is.

[weathervane_hilt]
An iron weathercock off a long-gone roof. It always turns into the wind.
With it, SHELTER brings a gust that slows the next attack.

[tempered_ferrule]
A steel tip for the end of an umbrella, hammered out and quenched in snow at Tilda's forge. Ves says it's for walking. It isn't for walking.

[skyiron_blade]
A long blade of dark iron that fell from the sky, Hollis says, and was found in the snow. It's light, it's sharp, and it rings when it parries.

[owlfeather_canopy]
A canopy of white owl feathers laid over each other like snow on a roof. It turns the wind, and Ves swears it's quieter inside.

[frostglass_canopy]
An umbrella canopy of paper-thin ice-glass panes, stitched on with goblin thread. It never melts. Its wind soothes, and it mends a little too.

# Chapter 3, below the clouds.
[woodcutters_hatchet]
A woodcutter's hatchet, the handle dark with years of hands. No tricks. It just hits very, very hard.

[pinewood_bow]
A bow cut from a red pine and strung with gut. It smells of sap, and it shoots straight up like the trees grow.

[punt_pole]
A long ash pole for pushing a boat through the marsh. Push it through a fight instead: you go where it points, and fast.

[reed_knife]
A knife of sharpened reed, so light you forget you're holding it. So do the bullets, for a moment.

[cistern_maul]
A great iron mallet from the drowned city's waterworks, made for knocking stuck valves loose. It knocks other things loose too.

[statue_buckler]
A round stone shield, broken off the arm of one of the city's statues. It was made to greet a hero. It'll do to guard a nobody.

[hollow_blade]
A sword found in the ruined city, plain and perfectly balanced, with no name on it anywhere. It rings like a bell when it parries.

[scarlet_silk_canopy]
An umbrella canopy of red silk from the woodcutters' good chest, the colour of the Pinefall in autumn. It hits like a slammed door.

[forum_bronze_ferrule]
A heavy bronze tip off one of the forum's railings, cut down to fit an umbrella. For when Ves has hers back. It'll hit like a church bell.

[vellum_canopy]
An umbrella canopy of old vellum pages from the library, waxed and stitched together. The writing's still on them. Things go quiet under it.

[fountain_brass_handle]
A curved brass handle off a dry fountain, polished smooth by a hundred years of hands. It feels warm, somehow.

[bog_oak_handle]
A handle of black bog-oak, a thousand years in the marsh and hard as iron. The wind it calls smells of rain, and it mends.

# The Hushwood.
[fallen_star]
A sword of pale star-metal, pulled out of the stone that brought it down. It's warm. It glows in the dark like it remembers being up there.

[tidemark]
A sword out of the old city, kept dry in its vault for longer than Hollin stood. A line runs along the blade like the mark water leaves on a wall, and it turns a blow aside the way a weir turns water.

[bookmark]
A thin old blade, left in a book to keep somebody's place, three hundred years ago. It was a very long book. It's light as a page and it slips through things like a page through fingers.

[sculptors_mallet]
The mallet the old city's sculptor carved all the Hero's statues with. Its head is worn into a shape that fits stone exactly. Swing it, and stone remembers it.

[reedcutter]
The old city's toll-keepers kept the causeway clear with this: a long, curved blade on a short haft, for cutting back the reeds. It still goes through them like a breath. It goes through other things, too, and it wants to go forward.

[sled_runner_sabre]
A herald's sled runner, ground down to an edge and given a grip of twine. Rummage swears it went downhill faster than anything alive. So do you, now.

[clock_hand_pike]
The minute hand off a great clock, on a broom handle. Hold it right and you're somewhere else for a second. It's always five to something.

[kettle_shield]
A huge iron kettle lid with a strap riveted on. It's taken worse than bullets. It's taken a woodcutters' breakfast.

[grandads_hatchet]
Bramble's grandad's hatchet, far too big for him. The handle's worn to the shape of a much bigger hand. He holds it where his grandad did anyway.

[mas_felling_axe]
Ma's spare felling axe, left at the trap. Twice as heavy as Bramble's. He says she'd want him to have it. She would also want him at camp.

[twin_hatchets]
Two little hatchets, one for each hand. Bramble insists he's always been ambidextrous. He found out today.

[splitting_maul]
A woodsplitter's maul, all head and no manners. Bramble can barely lift it, but when it comes down, everything flinches.

[heartwood_axe]
An axe with a head of black heartwood, harder than iron, from the oldest tree in the wood. It never needs sharpening. It never needs anything.

# Teasel's forge, in Lasthome's square: the old city's iron and the bell's
# last bronze, made into weapons for the first time in anyone's memory.
[hinge_cleaver]
A hinge off one of the old city's doors, ground to an edge and hafted. It isn't pretty. It's very heavy. No tricks, full damage.

[bell_bronze_gauntlets]
Cast from the last ingot of the chapel bell. They still ring when they're struck.
Punch the nearest bullet back at the enemy for damage. It has to be close!

[forge_lantern]
An iron lantern with a coal from Teasel's forge in it. It never goes out, and it's too hot to hold for long.
Leave a pool of light. Bullets inside it slow to a crawl.

[stokers_staff]
The iron bar Teasel stirs the fire with. It's warm all the way up.
Hold SHIFT to stand still and channel. Grazing builds SP three times as fast.

[bell_shard]
A piece of the chapel bell that never went into the pots, on a leather thong. Teasel kept it. She won't say why.
Ring it to freeze every bullet for a moment.

[iron_shod_ferrule]
A heavy iron tip for the end of Ves's umbrella, for when she has it back. Teasel made it without ever seeing the umbrella. "Bring it and I'll see."
