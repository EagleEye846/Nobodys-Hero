[ruins]
Long ago, deep beneath the earth, there were the Ruins.

[prophecy]
Carved into their oldest stone was a promise.

"When the lanterns burn their brightest, a Hero will descend the stair, and the Ruins will wake."

[preparations]
So the Ruins got ready.

They built a dummy to train the Hero.
They stocked a shop to arm them.

[beacon]
And the Keeper lit a lantern, so the Hero could find the way.

[waiting]
Then they waited.

And waited.

[stair]
One hundred years passed.

No Hero came.

[stranger]
Until today?

A glimmer came from above the stairs.

And a shadowy figure stepped forth.

[breath]
The entire world held their breath,

Had their hero finally arrived?
